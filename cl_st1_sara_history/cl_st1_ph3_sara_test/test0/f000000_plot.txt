A man named Mr. Manrich recounts his strange encounters with Dr. Dorrimore, a sinister figure he first met years earlier in San Francisco. Dorrimore seemed connected to mysterious, supernatural illusions, such as conjuring a dead body on the street that turned out to be his own likeness.

Years later, Manrich meets him again by chance while traveling near Auburn. Disturbed by Dorrimore’s unsettling presence, he nevertheless introduces him to his fiancée, Margaret Corray, and her mother when they unexpectedly arrive at the same hotel. To Manrich’s dismay, the women seem charmed by Dorrimore, which fuels his jealousy and unease.

One night, in an abandoned cemetery, Manrich sees Margaret with Dorrimore and, in a fit of rage, attacks. He later wakes battered and delirious, only to be told that no one named Corray had ever stayed at the hotel. Even more confusing, Dorrimore has vanished.

Eventually, the truth comes out: Margaret had never been in Auburn at all—she was in Oakland the entire time. The whole episode with her presence there was an illusion induced by Dorrimore, a master hypnotist trained in India. Later reports confirm that Dorrimore publicly demonstrated his ability to keep people under hallucinations for weeks or months, explaining Manrich’s bizarre ordeal.
