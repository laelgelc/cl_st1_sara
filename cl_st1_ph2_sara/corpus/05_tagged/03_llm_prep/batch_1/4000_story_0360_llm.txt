**THE LAST DREAM OF AHASVERUS**

*The end of time. A deserted earth beneath a colorless
heaven. The sun, enormous and red, leans upon the western
mountains, which are worn almost level by the ages. No
tree stands; no river moves. On a solitary rock lies
an ancient man, wrapped in a torn mantle. Iron chains
descend into a cleft nearby. Two eagles circle high above.*

**AHASVERUS.** Farewell, thou exhausted sun! Thou hast risen upon the
bridal chamber and the battlefield, upon the cradle and the
gibbet; now sink, and rise no more. Farewell, pale sky,
once blue as the robe of the Galilean! Farewell, clouds,
which bore the dreams of shepherds and the thunderbolts of
kings! Farewell, roses, lilies, cedars, and the small grass that
pushed between the stones of Jerusalem! Farewell, fountains whose coolness
was denied to thirsty lips, seas that divided nations and
swallowed navies, winds that carried prayers and pestilence with equal
indifference! Earth, ancient mother and tireless grave, farewell!

At last we are alone—thou and I.

Thou art dying; I, of a surety, shall die with
thee.

*He raises himself and looks across the plain.*

No smoke. No bell. No footstep. No child crying in
the ruins. The last city fell before the last forest,
and the last forest perished before the last spring. The
wolves devoured the sheep; hunger devoured the wolves; silence devoured
hunger. Man is gone.

O blessed consummation! O mercy long delayed! There remains none
to command me, none to curse me, none to ask
my name and recoil at the answer. The sentence has
lost its judge, the road its milestones, and my punishment
its earth. Death, who refused me at Babylon, at Rome,
at Antioch, at Paris, at Samarkand—death, who shut his door
when I knocked with the sword, the poison, the plague,
and the sea—must receive me now, for his own house
is falling.

*A sound of iron is heard beneath the rock.*

Who stirs there? Is some root yet alive? Some serpent
dragging its scales through the dust?

**PROMETHEUS.** Neither root nor serpent.

**AHASVERUS.** A voice!

**PROMETHEUS.** Hast thou forgotten voices?

**AHASVERUS.** I had forgotten everything save silence. Show thyself, remnant
of a vanished world!

**PROMETHEUS.** Come nearer.

**AHASVERUS.** I obey no man.

**PROMETHEUS.** Then come from curiosity, which hath governed more men
than obedience.

**AHASVERUS.** Curiosity is dead.

**PROMETHEUS.** Yet thou answerest.

**AHASVERUS.** Who art thou?

**PROMETHEUS.** First tell me who asks.

**AHASVERUS.** One who hath been called by many names and
welcomed by none. In Judea they named me Ahasverus.

**PROMETHEUS.** The wanderer?

**AHASVERUS.** The immortal.

**PROMETHEUS.** The man who would not pity?

**AHASVERUS.** The man whom pity would not release.

**PROMETHEUS.** Then descend two steps. Thou wilt find a crack
in the stone.

*Ahasverus descends. From the shadow appears Prometheus, chained by wrists
and ankles to the rock.*

**AHASVERUS.** Another immortal!

**PROMETHEUS.** Another prisoner.

**AHASVERUS.** Thy face is older than Nineveh, yet pride burns
in it.

**PROMETHEUS.** Pride hath survived where cities have not.

**AHASVERUS.** And these chains?

**PROMETHEUS.** The handiwork of Vulcan.

**AHASVERUS.** Thy name?

**PROMETHEUS.** Prometheus.

**AHASVERUS.** Thou!

**PROMETHEUS.** Even I.

**AHASVERUS.** The fire-thief? The friend of man? The Titan whom
Jupiter fastened to Caucasus, that an eagle might feed upon
his liver?

**PROMETHEUS.** Thou rememberest well.

**AHASVERUS.** I remember all things against my will. Yet Hercules
broke thy chains.

**PROMETHEUS.** Once.

**AHASVERUS.** And they were forged anew?

**PROMETHEUS.** Gods forgive rebellion no more readily than men forgive
truth. When old Olympus faded, I thought myself free. But
divine names perish more swiftly than divine cruelties. The thunderer
became an idea; the idea became a law; the law
became necessity. Thus was I bound again—not by Jupiter, but
by the world he made.

**AHASVERUS.** And the eagle?

**PROMETHEUS.** Look above.

**AHASVERUS.** There are two.

**PROMETHEUS.** Punishment also multiplies.

**AHASVERUS.** Let them come. Soon they shall find no flesh
but ours. Yet why dost thou smile?

**PROMETHEUS.** Because thou art mistaken.

**AHASVERUS.** In what?

**PROMETHEUS.** In believing this to be the end.

**AHASVERUS.** What remains? The oceans are pits of salt, the
valleys urns of ash. I have searched from the pillars
of Hercules to the deserts beyond Cathay, from the drowned
temples of Yucatán to the ice where no compass points.
No woman breathes; no infant quickens beneath a heart. Humanity
is finished.

**PROMETHEUS.** Humanity, perhaps. Life, no.

**AHASVERUS.** Distinguish not between the corpse and its shadow.

**PROMETHEUS.** Beneath these rocks the fires labor. In caves beyond
thy sight, seeds sleep that no winter can destroy. In
the deep places, waters gather. After the long night, clouds
shall return; after clouds, rain; after rain, moss, flower, wing,
and song. Then shall arise a race not born of
the infirm humanity thou knewest, but fashioned from its purified
remembrance.

**AHASVERUS.** No!

**PROMETHEUS.** Yea.

**AHASVERUS.** Men again?

**PROMETHEUS.** Not men as they were. The tree is not
slain because its rotten branches are cut away. A nobler
stem shall rise: a race without greed, without falsehood, without
masters or slaves; strong without cruelty, wise without pride, beautiful
without vanity. They shall possess fire, yet burn no city;
speech, yet utter no lie; love, yet forge no chain
from love. Their temples shall have no victims, their laws
no executioners, their frontiers no graves.

**AHASVERUS.** Be silent!

**PROMETHEUS.** Why?

**AHASVERUS.** Thou disturbest death.

**PROMETHEUS.** Death is not so light a sleeper.

**AHASVERUS.** I had attained certainty. Dost thou know what certainty
means to one who hath walked through doubt for a
million dawns? I had reached the last gate of the
many-gated city of life. My hand was upon the latch.
Beyond it there was no road, no command, no morrow.
And thou criest that the road begins anew!

**PROMETHEUS.** Would that grieve thee?

**AHASVERUS.** Grieve me? Hear, Titan, and judge.

I stood once at a door in Jerusalem. The noon
was white, the dust bitter, and the streets howled with
a multitude hungry for suffering. A man approached beneath a
cross. Blood covered his brow; his knees trembled; behind him
came soldiers, women, mockers, and those mild spectators who commit
every cruelty by merely looking on. He leaned toward my
threshold and asked one moment's rest.

I refused him.

Perhaps I feared the Romans. Perhaps I loved my clean
stone more than his anguish. Perhaps evil needs no great
cause, but enters through a crevice no wider than a
thought. I thrust him away and cried, "Go on!"

He looked at me.

No thunder spoke. No angel drew a sword. He said
only, "I go; but thou shalt go until I return."

Thenceforth I went.

**PROMETHEUS.** Century after century.

**AHASVERUS.** Century after century; dawn upon dawn, sunset upon sunset.
I crossed lands whose names were not yet conceived, and
returned when their names had been forgotten. I beheld Rome
crowned, drunken, Christian, divided, sacked, and buried beneath the palaces
of men who called themselves Romans. I saw the Cross
lifted above the eagle, and the eagle painted upon shields
beneath the Cross. I heard monks dispute concerning mercy while
peasants starved outside their walls. I saw caliphs enter cities
with wisdom and their heirs depart them with treasure. I
followed crusaders to Jerusalem and merchants to Cathay; I watched
black ships carry chains toward the west and golden ships
carry death toward the east.

Everywhere the race, the creed, the tongue changed; everywhere the
cry remained.

**PROMETHEUS.** Yet there was also laughter.

**AHASVERUS.** Brief laughter between two funerals! I saw Abraham's children
slay one another over Abraham's blessing. I saw Moses engraved
above courts where justice was sold. David's songs were sung
by armies marching to plunder. The Nazarene's wounds were painted
upon banners that covered fresh wounds. Philosophers praised reason and
flattered tyrants; poets praised love and abandoned their children; kings
erected monuments to peace with the spoil of war.

Do not speak to me of peoples. I have known
barbarous and civilized peoples, clothed and naked peoples, peoples of
islands and continents, of suns and snows. The barbarian broke
the skull with a stone; the civilized man pressed a
lever beyond the horizon. Progress altered the instrument, not the
hand.

**PROMETHEUS.** Thou hast seen much.

**AHASVERUS.** I have seen too much and done nothing. There
lay the cruelty of my sentence. Had I been commanded
to build, the labor might have comforted me. Had I
been permitted to heal, a single child's gratitude might have
shortened an age. But I wandered. Empires rose while I
crossed a mountain and fell while I crossed it again.
Men begged me for the future, supposing age must know;
I knew only that the future would resemble the past
wearing another mask.

I warned Sardanapalus; he called for music. I warned a
conqueror before he entered the snow; he laughed at my
rags. I warned a republic that it had begun to
adore its own reflection; it raised statues to itself. I
spoke in markets, courts, camps, universities. Sometimes they stoned me,
sometimes crowned me, sometimes printed my words and understood not
a jot of them. The stones, the crowns, and the
books became dust. I remained.

**PROMETHEUS.** Knowledge remained in thee.

**AHASVERUS.** Knowledge without use is a lamp in a tomb.

**PROMETHEUS.** Nevertheless, thou wert the memory of mankind.

**AHASVERUS.** A book no man could finish! Each generation opened
me at the page flattering to itself. The happy asked
for tales of prosperity; the miserable for proof that others
had suffered more. No man desired truth. Truth is a
mirror, and men came to me seeking portraits painted in
advance.

**PROMETHEUS.** Yet thou lovedst them.

**AHASVERUS.** I hated them.

**PROMETHEUS.** Thou rememberest them too tenderly for hatred.

**AHASVERUS.** Tenderly! I remember their repetition. A mother bending over
her fevered son in Thebes, another in Florence, another in
a nameless town beneath electric towers—the same prayer, the same
cooling hand, the same morning without an answer. I remember
soldiers leaving home beneath bronze trumpets, silver trumpets, wooden drums,
and the voiceless signals of machines—the same boast at departure,
the same astonishment in death. I remember hungry men outside
granaries in Egypt, Gaul, India, and the proud republics of
the final age—the same locked door, the same polished speech
explaining why bread must wait.

If I speak passionately, 'tis because weariness itself hath grown
passionate in me.

**PROMETHEUS.** And now thou wouldst die?

**AHASVERUS.** Now I shall die. Mark the difference. Desire hath
been mocked too often; certainty alone consoles me.

**PROMETHEUS.** But if the coming race should need a witness?

**AHASVERUS.** Let it be born blind. Sight hath profited none.

**PROMETHEUS.** If it should need one who can tell how
pride ruined Babel, luxury enfeebled Babylon, faction consumed Athens, power
corrupted Rome, and despair silenced the final nations?

**AHASVERUS.** It would listen, praise my wisdom, and repeat them
all.

**PROMETHEUS.** Not this race.

**AHASVERUS.** Who shall create thy immaculate children? What god hath
learned from his failures?

**PROMETHEUS.** I shall create them.

**AHASVERUS.** Thou?

**PROMETHEUS.** I fashioned the first.

**AHASVERUS.** What sayest thou?

**PROMETHEUS.** From clay I formed mankind. When my creatures shivered,
I stole fire from heaven and placed it in their
hands. With fire they warmed their caves, hardened their tools,
lighted the darkness, and began the ascent toward thought.

**AHASVERUS.** Thou didst create them?

**PROMETHEUS.** I.

**AHASVERUS.** And gavest them fire?

**PROMETHEUS.** I.

**AHASVERUS.** Then the murderer is found!

*He seizes a length of chain.*

**PROMETHEUS.** What dost thou?

**AHASVERUS.** Completing justice, which the gods abandoned. O author of
the interminable crime! Thou didst warm the cave—and afterward burnedst
Carthage. Thou didst harden the ploughshare—and the sword sprang beside
it. From thy spark came the lamp of the scholar,
but also the torch of the inquisitor; the hearth where
children gathered, but also the furnace into which children vanished;
the beacon guiding ships, but also the flame cast down
upon sleeping cities!

**PROMETHEUS.** Man chose his use.

**AHASVERUS.** The child chose, but thou placedst lightning in his
cradle! Thou gavest him mastery before wisdom, desire before restraint,
imagination before pity. Why didst thou not fashion his heart
as carefully as his hands?

**PROMETHEUS.** Freedom cannot be fashioned complete.

**AHASVERUS.** A phrase! I have heard gods excuse themselves with
mystery and rulers with necessity. Thou shalt not escape by
a word.

*Ahasverus draws the chain around Prometheus's body and tightens it.*

There! Let iron embrace thee. Let the eagles descend. Had
I known thee at the beginning, I might have spared
the earth its history.

**PROMETHEUS.** By killing me?

**AHASVERUS.** Thou canst not die.

**PROMETHEUS.** Then thy vengeance resembles that of Jupiter.

**AHASVERUS.** I have studied under excellent masters.

**PROMETHEUS.** And wilt thou punish the giver for the receiver's
sin?

**AHASVERUS.** I punish hope, whose first accomplice thou art. Hope
bade the slave endure one more day, the soldier fight
one more war, the dying king poison one more heir.
Hope rebuilt every fallen prison. Had men despaired earlier, they
might have rested.

**PROMETHEUS.** Thou blasphemest that which sustained thee.

**AHASVERUS.** Nothing sustained me. I could not fall.

**PROMETHEUS.** There thou art deceived again.

**AHASVERUS.** Speak plainly.

**PROMETHEUS.** Thy immortality was not bestowed merely as punishment.

**AHASVERUS.** Not merely?

**PROMETHEUS.** The Galilean saw farther than revenge.

**AHASVERUS.** Take care, Titan.

**PROMETHEUS.** Why wert thou commanded to wander until his return?
Why not for a thousand years? Why not until Jerusalem
fell, or the last altar cracked? Because thy journey had
a purpose beyond repentance. Thou wert reserved.

**AHASVERUS.** For what?

**PROMETHEUS.** For the new world.

**AHASVERUS.** Thou liest to loosen thy chains.

**PROMETHEUS.** Tighten them, then, and hear me. When the waters
return and the earth is clothed anew, my children shall
awaken without memory. Perfection itself would imperil them, for innocence
knows not the disguises of evil. They shall require one
who hath seen every temptation crowned and every falsehood unmasked;
one whom no splendor can dazzle, no threat terrify, no
length of years corrupt.

**AHASVERUS.** And where is such a one?

**PROMETHEUS.** Before me.

**AHASVERUS.** I?

**PROMETHEUS.** Thou.

**AHASVERUS.** I myself?

**PROMETHEUS.** Who else hath walked through every chamber of the
old humanity? Who spoke with slave and Caesar, patriarch and
atheist, harlot and saint? Who knows the beginning of roads
and the graves to which they lead? Thou shalt be
the bridge between the buried race and the race unborn.

**AHASVERUS.** A bridge?

**PROMETHEUS.** A living bridge.

**AHASVERUS.** Speak on.

**PROMETHEUS.** As Hercules once delivered me from the eagle, so
shalt thou deliver me now. Therefore art thou the new
Hercules—not mighty in sinew, but mighty in duration. Together we
shall descend when the rocks divide. I shall shape the
bodies of the coming people; thou shalt instruct their souls.

**AHASVERUS.** Speak on!

**PROMETHEUS.** They shall gather about thee at evening. The young
shall touch thy garment and marvel that eyes so ancient
still contain fire. Thou shalt recount the vanished seas, the
towers, the gardens, the swans upon blue water, the swallows
beneath the eaves. Thou shalt tell of music, of lovers
meeting beneath almond blossoms, of ships returning with lamps upon
their masts.

**AHASVERUS.** There were such things.

**PROMETHEUS.** Thou seest? Already memory changes from burden into treasure.

**AHASVERUS.** And shall they suffer?

**PROMETHEUS.** Not as the old race suffered.

**AHASVERUS.** Shall they deceive?

**PROMETHEUS.** They shall know truth as naturally as birds know
the south.

**AHASVERUS.** Shall they grow old?

**PROMETHEUS.** Slowly, beautifully, without terror.

**AHASVERUS.** And I?

**PROMETHEUS.** Thou shalt not grow old at all.

**AHASVERUS.** I shall remain?

**PROMETHEUS.** Forever.

**AHASVERUS.** Forever!

**PROMETHEUS.** Their counselor in peace, their judge in doubt, their
guardian against the buried errors of mankind.

**AHASVERUS.** Their guardian?

**PROMETHEUS.** Their king.

**AHASVERUS.** King!

**PROMETHEUS.** Not king by sword or inherited jewel, but by
suffering. Every scar shall be a province; every century, a
gem in thy crown.

**AHASVERUS.** I had thought existence empty because it lacked a
task. Give me a task, and eternity itself is short!
O wondrous justice! O secret mercy of the Galilean! Then
my wandering was apprenticeship, my grief instruction, my loneliness preparation!

*He loosens one chain.*

Why didst thou not tell me at once?

**PROMETHEUS.** Wouldst thou have believed?

**AHASVERUS.** I believe now. Tell me of their cities.

**PROMETHEUS.** They shall build with living crystal and white stone.
No wall shall surround them. Water shall sing through every
street, and gardens shall climb the roofs. Their halls of
counsel shall stand open beneath the sky.

**AHASVERUS.** And no prisons?

**PROMETHEUS.** None.

*Ahasverus loosens another chain.*

**AHASVERUS.** No armies?

**PROMETHEUS.** None.

**AHASVERUS.** No beggars at palace gates?

**PROMETHEUS.** No palaces save the common house of joy.

**AHASVERUS.** And they will honor me?

**PROMETHEUS.** As the first among them.

**AHASVERUS.** Nay, I shall be humble. I, who have seen
the pomp of Pharaoh and Caesar, shall wear a plain
robe. Yet perhaps a staff would be fitting—not gold, but
cedar. Are cedars to return?

**PROMETHEUS.** Cedars taller than Lebanon's.

**AHASVERUS.** And roses?

**PROMETHEUS.** Roses without thorns.

**AHASVERUS.** Keep some thorns, lest beauty become insipid. I have
learned somewhat, Prometheus. Perfection must contain remembrance of danger, even
as sweetness requires a grain of bitterness.

**PROMETHEUS.** Thou speakest already like a king.

**AHASVERUS.** A teacher, say rather. Though if disputes arise, authority
must reside somewhere. Their love may freely confer what destiny
hath appointed.

*He draws the chain from Prometheus's wrists.*

There. Rise, creator! The earth is not dead, but sleeping;
the sun not setting, but descending to kindle another dawn.
I was a fool to salute extinction. Life! Life renewed,
purified, immeasurable! Let the mountains lift again; let fountains break
the seals of stone; let the green multitudes march from
the valleys! I shall name each flower and teach each
bird its forgotten song.

**PROMETHEUS.** First free my ankles.

**AHASVERUS.** At once, companion. Brother! We two shall restore the
world.

*He kneels and struggles with the final chain.*

I hear waters.

**PROMETHEUS.** Thou hearest them.

**AHASVERUS.** I smell rain.

**PROMETHEUS.** It approaches.

**AHASVERUS.** I see children coming across the plain—tall, radiant, crowned
with leaves. They call me. Hark! They call me father,
guardian, king! Wait, beloved ones! Ahasverus comes! He shall teach
you all that was noble and conceal all that was
vile. No grief shall touch you. No death shall divide
us. We shall live—thou, I, and they—forever, forever, forever—

*The light fades. Prometheus and the chains vanish. Ahasverus lies
motionless upon the rock, his hands closed around dry weeds.
The two eagles descend and perch beside him.*

**FIRST EAGLE.** Is he dead?

**SECOND EAGLE.** The last breath hath scarcely left him.

**FIRST EAGLE.** He smiled.

**SECOND EAGLE.** He dreamed.

**FIRST EAGLE.** Of death?

**SECOND EAGLE.** Of life.

**FIRST EAGLE.** Strange. He cursed life until the final moment.

**SECOND EAGLE.** Because he loved it beyond measure.

**FIRST EAGLE.** And when it could no longer keep him?

**SECOND EAGLE.** He made eternity promise him a crown.

**FIRST EAGLE.** Poor king.

**SECOND EAGLE.** Poor man.

*The eagles spread their wings. The sun goes out.*
