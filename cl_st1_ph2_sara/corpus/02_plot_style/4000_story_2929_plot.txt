Fifteen years after leaving Amberley to make his fortune, unsuccessful but incurably romantic Jedediah Crane returns as a tin pedlar. He visits Mattie Adams, the woman he once loved but was forced to abandon after her meddlesome sister Selena opposed their relationship. Mattie, now unmarried and managing the prosperous family farm, welcomes him warmly.

Jed begins visiting weekly, while Selena watches, criticizes, and insinuates that he wants Mattie only for her property. Initially, Mattie encourages the friendship partly to defy Selena, but her old love for Jed gradually revives. Jed loves her as well, yet his poverty and pride convince him that proposing would be presumptuous.

When Jed announces that he plans to leave again, Selena mocks Mattie for being rejected. Provoked, Mattie declares that she and Jed will marry at Christmas and that he will manage the farm. Determined to make her claim true, she tells Jed what she has said. He joyfully confesses that he has always loved her but never believed himself worthy of her. They become engaged, and Jed departs his final tin-peddling round delighted that his long-awaited romance has finally come true.
