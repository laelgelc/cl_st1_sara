Lori, a young woman unhappy with her monotonous rural marriage, feels mysteriously summoned by the last living apple tree in an abandoned orchard. She visits it despite her husband Steve’s objections and tells it she will never return. The possessive tree tries to seize her, but she escapes with a dried apple that rolls down the hill after her. Hungry on the way home, she eats it.

That night Lori suffers violent abdominal pain and dies. A doctor attributes her death to a ruptured appendix, briefly wondering whether she might have swallowed a seed. Months later, Steve and his father visit her grave and discover a vigorous young apple tree growing in the cemetery. Though it is only April, the tree already bears unnaturally red apples whose color reminds Steve of Lori’s flushed cheeks, suggesting that the old tree’s seed took root through her death.
