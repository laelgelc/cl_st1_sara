An ailing, lonely assistant professor takes a room in a nearly empty country hotel and becomes fascinated by a pale, mysterious guest who appears to move steadily from room to room along the corridor toward him. When an elderly woman occupying one of the rooms dies, the stranger immediately advances into her room, then continues closer as the narrator’s health deteriorates.

After the hotel clerk insists that no such guest is registered and that the rooms are empty, the narrator realizes the pale man is Death. His progress through the hotel marks his approach, and his detour through the old woman’s room coincided with her death. Now confined to bed, the narrator waits behind a bolted door, knowing the stranger’s final destination is his room. The story ends as the door slowly opens.
