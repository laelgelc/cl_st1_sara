A spaceship crew buying provisions on Mars acquires a huge, piglike creature called a wub. Captain Franco plans to slaughter it for food, but the wub reveals that it is a peaceful, intelligent telepath capable of discussing philosophy, mythology, and ethics. Although it argues for its right to live and briefly immobilizes Franco in self-defense, the frightened and stubborn captain shoots it.

The crew reluctantly eats the wub while Franco enjoys the meal. Afterward, Franco begins speaking with the wub’s mannerisms and resumes its discussion of Odysseus, revealing that the wub’s consciousness has transferred into him.
