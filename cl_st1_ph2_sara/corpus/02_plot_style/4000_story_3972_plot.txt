Spider Buffin, a pickpocket obsessed with revenge, is imprisoned after Constable Keating catches him attacking an enemy. Upon release, Buffin befriends Keating only to learn where he goes off duty and ambush him. This apparent friendship makes the Frith Street Gang suspect Buffin of being a police informer, forcing him to act quickly.

While Buffin waits to attack Keating outside the policeman’s home, gang members Otto the Sausage and Rabbit Butler assault Keating first. Enraged that they have stolen his intended victim, Buffin rushes in and fights them, inadvertently saving Keating’s life and helping secure their arrest.

In court, Buffin tries unsuccessfully to protect the attackers by denying their involvement, while Keating and the magistrate publicly praise him as a hero. Seeing gang leader Sid Marks watching, Buffin realizes he will now be regarded as an informer and must flee London. Lacking money, he pawns his possessions—including the gold watch he has just stolen from the magistrate—and escapes by Tube.
