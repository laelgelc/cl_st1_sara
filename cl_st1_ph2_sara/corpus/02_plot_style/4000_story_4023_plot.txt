While wandering near Kinvara, the poet Hanrahan enters a house where music and dancing are underway. He becomes captivated by Oona, the host’s daughter, and enchants her with tales, poetry, and a song about an immortal country beyond death. Oona appears ready to abandon everything and follow him, alarming her mother.

Because forcibly expelling a poet might bring a curse, Oona’s mother and a neighbour trick Hanrahan into helping them twist a hay rope. Absorbed in boasting and working, he backs through the open doorway, whereupon they lock him outside and draw Oona into the dancing. Rejected and humiliated, Hanrahan goes to the seashore and sings alone.

There he is overcome by supernatural visions. A fairy queen mocks his weakness, the rope seems to become a monstrous sea-creature binding the entire world, and shadowy voices declare that because he refused the call of the fairy women, he will never find comfort in mortal love and will carry the cold of the grave in his heart forever.
