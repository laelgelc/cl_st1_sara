Joby Teague develops a severe squint as a baby after watching his family dance in circles. As an adult farmer, the condition causes him constant trouble, so his wife urges him to seek help from Tommy Warne, a supposed conjurer whose apparent magic actually stems from trickery.

Tommy instructs Joby to sleep atop his four-poster bed with the bedding arranged backward and counterclockwise. That night, Joby dreams that his long-absent sea-captain brother leads him to a fairground ride, which becomes a boat crossing the Equator. The journey reverses Joby’s squint but does not cure it. Abandoned at sea, he rescues the enchanted weathercock from Wendron Church, which carries him home and spins him counterclockwise until his eyes become straight.

From the church tower, Joby sees his wife apparently preparing to marry Tommy. To stop the wedding, he tries to turn back the church clock, but awakens to discover that he is actually twisting his wife’s arm while asleep. When she lights a candle, they find that the strange cure has worked: Joby’s eyes are straight and remain so thereafter.
