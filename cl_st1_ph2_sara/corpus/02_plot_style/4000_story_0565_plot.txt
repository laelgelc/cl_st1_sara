A poor, hardworking farm couple lives on barren land where the wife must carry water uphill from a brook. After she twice rescues a helpless beetle, it reveals itself as the king of the insects and tells the couple to dig a well. Though the well contains no water, a pump placed in it produces gold coins gathered by insects from money lost and buried throughout the countryside.

Initially delighted, the honest couple worries that the gold may be stolen or magical, but the beetle assures them it rightfully belongs to them. They then spend lavishly in town and openly display their newfound wealth. Their boasting attracts thieves, who break into their home and steal all the remaining gold while they are away. When the couple returns, the well produces nothing more, and they are publicly humiliated.

The beetle explains that their misfortune resulted from their pride and ostentation. With the treasure gone, the couple must return to honest labor, learning that good fortune should be accepted humbly and used with moderation.
