While honeymooning in Nuremberg, a narrator and his wife befriend Elias P. Hutcheson, a cheerful, adventurous American. During a walk near the city walls, Hutcheson accidentally drops a stone that kills a black kitten. Its mother reacts with extraordinary hatred and follows the travelers through the city.

At the Torture Tower, Hutcheson recklessly volunteers to be tied inside the Iron Virgin, whose door is fitted with spikes. As the custodian slowly lowers the door, the black cat appears and attacks him, gouging his eye. He drops the restraining rope, causing the heavy door to slam shut and impale Hutcheson. The cat then sits on the dead man’s head and licks his blood until the narrator kills it with an executioner’s sword.
