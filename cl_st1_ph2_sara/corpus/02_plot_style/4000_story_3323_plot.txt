A weary fisherman seeks refreshment at a welcoming farmhouse, where an elderly farmer and his capable, unmarried daughter, Marthy, offer him water, cream, and dinner. While showing the visitor around, the father explains that Marthy was the eldest of three daughters and had once brought home a young army lieutenant named Morris. The father feared Morris might marry his favorite daughter, whom he hoped would remain at home, but Morris instead proposed to Marthy’s younger sister Annie. When Marthy heard the news, she fainted, revealing feelings for Morris that her unsuspecting father never recognized. Morris married Annie, while Marthy remained unmarried, cared for her widowed father, and became the enduring center of his household. The fisherman leaves deeply impressed by her strength, hospitality, and concealed sacrifice.
