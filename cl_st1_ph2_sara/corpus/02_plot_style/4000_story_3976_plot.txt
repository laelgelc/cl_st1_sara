At Monte Carlo, George Lattaker expects to receive an inheritance from his uncle while staying aboard a yacht with his friend Reggie Pepper and Stella Vanderley. George proposes to Stella, but moments after she accepts, she sees him impulsively kiss her maid, Pilbeam. Stella ends the engagement, and a distraught, intoxicated George wanders ashore, where he becomes involved in a fight he can barely remember.

The next morning, George fears he was responsible for the reported assault on a prince, especially when private investigator Denman Sturgis arrives carrying George’s lost hat. Reggie devises a scheme based on a letter claiming that George has a newly discovered twin brother, Alfred: George will impersonate Alfred until the yacht sails. The deception works, and as “Alfred,” George persuades Stella to forgive “George.”

George’s uncle then reveals privately that there is no twin and that he invented Alfred to delay discovery that he had squandered George’s inheritance. George cannot expose him without also exposing his own disguise. Meanwhile, Reggie’s valet, Voules, who saw George kiss Pilbeam and believes George has stolen her affection, learns of the impersonation. After winning a fortune gambling, Voules publicly reveals that “Alfred” is really George.

The exposure unexpectedly saves George. The recovered prince explains that George was not his attacker but the man who fought off the masked assailant and saved him. The grateful prince intends to reward George, resolving his financial troubles. Voules reconciles with Pilbeam and leaves service as a wealthy man, while George and Stella also reconcile and resume their romance.
