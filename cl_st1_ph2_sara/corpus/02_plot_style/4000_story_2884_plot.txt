During a fierce March nor’easter on the New England coast, a brig called the Huron is driven ashore near a life-saving station. Captain Litchfield and his crew use a breeches buoy to rescue six of the eight sailors despite repeated line failures, freezing conditions, and enormous surf. Before the final two men—the brig’s captain and a Spanish sailor—can be brought ashore, the mast supporting the rescue line collapses. Both are later recovered from the breakers, but attempts to revive them fail. By morning, the brig has been destroyed and scattered along the beach, underscoring both the storm’s devastation and the life-savers’ largely unrecognized heroism.
