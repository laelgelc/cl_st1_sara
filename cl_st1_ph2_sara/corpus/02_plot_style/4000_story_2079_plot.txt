A schoolboy, tired of routine and inspired by adventure stories, plans to skip school with two companions and explore Dublin’s waterfront. One boy fails to appear, so the narrator and Mahony continue alone, wandering through the docks, crossing the river, watching foreign sailors, and imagining escape to distant places. Tired and unable to reach their intended destination, they rest in a secluded field.

There, a strange older man approaches and begins an increasingly disturbing conversation. After speaking obsessively about girls and briefly leaving the boys, he returns and fixates on whipping unruly boys. Frightened by the man’s manner and intentions, the narrator cautiously gets up and calls for Mahony using a false name they had agreed upon. As Mahony runs toward him, the narrator feels relieved and ashamed that he had previously looked down on his companion, now seeing him as a source of rescue.
