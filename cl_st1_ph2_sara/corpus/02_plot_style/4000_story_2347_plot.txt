Three gold prospectors, Dave Regan, Jim Bently, and Andy Page, decide to catch fish in a creek by detonating an oversized, carefully waterproofed blasting cartridge. Before they can use it, their foolish but good-natured retriever, Tommy, picks it up. The loose fuse trails through the campfire and ignites.

Believing his mates are playing a game, Tommy happily chases them with the live explosive in his mouth. Dave, Jim, and Andy flee in panic, but the dog follows whichever man is nearest. Attempts to seize or throw away the cartridge fail because Tommy immediately retrieves it. Jim hides in a mining hole, Andy takes cover behind a log, and Dave runs to a nearby hotel, inadvertently bringing the dog and bomb with him.

The terrified patrons scatter and barricade themselves as Tommy enters the hotel and then follows them into the yard. A vicious yellow cattle dog attacks him, causing him to drop the cartridge. The yellow dog and several other curious dogs gather around it just as it explodes. The blast kills the yellow dog, injures and terrifies the others, shakes the hotel kitchen, and sends horses bolting, while the human witnesses collapse into laughter or hysterics.

Tommy returns unharmed and cheerfully follows Dave back to camp, apparently pleased with the afternoon’s excitement. Andy chains him up, remakes dinner, and Dave helps Jim from the hole. For years afterward, passing bushmen mock Dave by asking how the fishing is going.
