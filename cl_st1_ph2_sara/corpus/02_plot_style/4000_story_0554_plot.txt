Keo, a cheerful young hippopotamus, falls into a concealed pit dug by Gouie, a villager hoping to capture a full-grown hippopotamus. Too young to be valuable, Keo is released after solemnly promising to return in a year and a day as Gouie’s prisoner.

As the deadline approaches, Keo’s worried family seeks help from the ancient magical creature Glinkomok. Glinkomok makes Keo’s skin impenetrable, grants him tremendous strength and speed, and secretly tells him how to defeat his captor.

Keo honors his promise and returns to Gouie, who discovers that he cannot kill him and instead sells rides on his back. Keo carries groups of villagers to Glinkomok’s cave, where they disappear. He then takes Gouie to the river and leaves him trapped between hostile hippopotamuses on both banks. Gouie begs for rescue, agreeing to become Keo’s captive in a year and a day and swearing by his grandfather’s bones.

Keo releases Gouie, but Gouie flees to a distant country with his earnings and never returns. Although he becomes a wealthy chief, his conscience torments him at night—even though his oath was technically meaningless because his grandfather, a famously boneless sorcerer, had no bones.
