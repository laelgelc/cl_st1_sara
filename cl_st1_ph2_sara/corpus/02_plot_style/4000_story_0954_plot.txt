On New Year’s Eve, William obtains free sweets from Mr. Moss and, in return, agrees to make a resolution to be polite for New Year’s Day. Mr. Moss also reveals that he intends to repeat his annual marriage proposal to a woman who has refused him for ten years.

The next morning, William’s exaggerated politeness confuses and irritates his family. He then visits Mr. Moss, who asks him to watch the sweet shop briefly while he catches a train to make his proposal. When the replacement shopkeeper cannot come, William remains in charge and begins eating the stock.

Treating the shop as his own, William overcharges an unpleasant customer, gives a large quantity of sweets to a girl he admires, and invites his friends to help themselves. A confrontation with an older boy escalates into a street battle fought with acid drops, leaving the shop depleted and disordered.

Mr. Moss returns delighted because his proposal has finally been accepted. Too happy to care about the damage or lost stock, he thanks William for minding the shop. William abandons his resolution after half a day and heads home feeling sick from overeating sweets.
