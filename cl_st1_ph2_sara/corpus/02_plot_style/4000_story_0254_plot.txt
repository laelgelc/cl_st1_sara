Karen, a poor but beautiful girl, is adopted by a wealthy old woman after her mother’s death. She becomes vain and obsessed with a pair of elegant red shoes, wearing them to her confirmation and later to church despite her guardian’s disapproval. A mysterious old soldier calls them dancing shoes, and they soon force Karen to dance uncontrollably.

When her guardian falls gravely ill, Karen neglects her duty to attend a ball in the red shoes. They carry her away into the wilderness, where an angel condemns her to dance as punishment for her vanity and pride. Unable to stop even after learning that her guardian has died, Karen begs an executioner to cut off her feet. He does so, but the severed feet continue dancing in the shoes.

Given wooden feet and crutches, Karen initially believes her suffering has made her worthy, but the dancing shoes repeatedly bar her from church. She finally repents sincerely, enters service at a parsonage, and lives humbly and devoutly. One Sunday, as she prays alone, the angel returns bearing roses instead of a sword. Through divine mercy, Karen experiences the church within her room and dies filled with peace and joy, her soul rising to God.
