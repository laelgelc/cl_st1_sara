A family builds a secluded house and garden in an ancient woodland near a river, displacing some of the native vegetation and soon discovering that the surrounding animals are their new neighbors. They encounter harmless spring snakes, kill one found swallowing a frog, and trap a woodchuck that has been eating their lettuce and beans, only to regret its death.

In their conservatory fountain, the family welcomes a solemn bullfrog named Unke and a lively smaller frog named Cri-Cri. Their affection for Unke ends when he swallows Cri-Cri, and they drive him back to the river. Other woodland visitors include muskrats and owls, while a flying squirrel repeatedly enters the house at night and causes mysterious noises. Once discovered and trapped in a room, it is guided into the conservatory and released through a window. Through these encounters, the family learns that living in the woods brings many unconventional neighbors, whose behavior is not so different from that found in human society.
