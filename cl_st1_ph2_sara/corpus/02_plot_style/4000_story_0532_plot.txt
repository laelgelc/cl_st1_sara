Humpty Dumpty, the youngest egg laid by a Speckled Hen, is accidentally kicked from the crowded nest by his siblings. Eager to explore the world, he befriends a Bantam’s egg named Coutchie-Coulou, and the two escape from the barn and enter a royal garden. Coutchie-Coulou is crushed beneath a horse’s hoof, leaving Humpty alone.

A compassionate Princess finds Humpty and shows him the palace and its beautiful grounds. She later places him atop the garden wall so he can watch the King and his riders return. Humpty leans too far forward, falls, and is shattered beyond repair.

That evening, the King challenges the Princess’s suitors to offer him a riddle he cannot solve, promising her hand to the winner. The Princess secretly gives her favored suitor, Master Gracington, a riddle inspired by Humpty’s fate. When the King cannot answer it, Gracington reveals that Humpty Dumpty was an egg. He wins the Princess’s hand, so Humpty’s story ultimately repays her kindness.
