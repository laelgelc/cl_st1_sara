Little Jack Martin, a kind, speech-impaired cripple beloved by the cowboys for his exceptional cooking and care, decides to leave the ranch and claim land in newly opened Oklahoma. His companions support him by choosing their best horse and lightest rider to secure the tract he wants on Big Turkey Creek. When they discover a “sooner” already occupying it illegally, they stage a mock hanging and frighten him into fleeing.

The cowboys then help Jack establish his homestead, planting a garden and building him a log cabin before reluctantly returning to their ranch. Months later, Jack visits and privately tells the foreman, Miller, that he plans to marry. Delighted, the whole outfit suspends its cattle work, gathers improvised gifts, and travels to his expanded cabin for the wedding celebration. Surrounded by his friends, neighbors, and bride, Jack hosts an exuberant all-night feast and dance, completing his transformation from cherished camp cook to successful homesteader and family man.
