On Christmas Eve, six ancient bell spirits gather in a city steeple to review the dying year. Each reports on the part of the city beneath his watch: one condemns greed and dishonesty in business while honoring upright, generous citizens; another criticizes the extravagance and spiritual emptiness of the wealthy but praises those who use their fortunes charitably; and a third mourns the neglected poor, recounting how an impoverished young seamstress, unable to survive on her wages and unnoticed despite the bell’s desperate warnings, dies alone.

The remaining spirits offer more hopeful perspectives. A seafaring bell spirit praises a vigorous preacher and the compassionate character of sailors, then lightens the somber gathering with a joke. A Catholic bell spirit celebrates immigrant gratitude, communal worship, and a church open to rich and poor alike, while urging other denominations to make religion warmer and more welcoming. The final spirit surveys political, social, educational, literary, and religious reform, acknowledging continued conflict but expressing faith in progress, tolerance, and the younger generation.

As dawn arrives, the spirits end their vigil by resolving to use their voices throughout the coming year to promote charity, justice, peace, and goodwill. They depart singing the angels’ message of peace on earth and goodwill toward humanity.
