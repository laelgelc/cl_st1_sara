Dr. Elton repeatedly answers the anxious Marvel family’s calls, often at night, for minor or imagined illnesses. Their excessive reliance on medicine gradually weakens their young son, yet when they receive the doctor’s sizable annual bill, they accuse him of overcharging and resolve never to employ him again.

Soon afterward, Elton treats a visiting merchant’s daughter through a dangerous case of typhus fever. After weeks of attentive care and intense anxiety, he saves her life, only for her father to denounce the modest bill as dishonest and exploitative. These incidents reveal the physician’s thankless position: patients urgently demand his skill and sacrifice while illness threatens, but once health returns, they forget his service and resent paying for it.
