Maida, a poorly paid shopgirl, saves for months to buy a fashionable purple dress for her employer’s Thanksgiving dinner, hoping to impress Mr. Ramsay, the admired head clerk. The night before the event, however, she gives the final four dollars for her dress to her coworker and romantic rival, Grace, who is about to be evicted after spending her rent money on a red dress.

Unable to pay the tailor, Maida misses the dinner while Grace attends in her new outfit. Later, the sympathetic tailor lets Maida take the finished dress on credit. Delighted, she wears it outside despite a heavy rain, determined to enjoy the reward of her long sacrifice. She encounters Mr. Ramsay, who admires both her appearance and what he mistakes for sensible devotion to his harsh health principles. He asks to walk with her, and Maida responds by blushing and sneezing.
