A shy, awkward man named Dutton reluctantly spends a weekend at a grand country house, where an Irish servant carefully arranges his small, shiny belongings and warns that a tiny supernatural “Borrower” steals bright, lovely objects for its collection. Dutton initially doubts him, but his collar stud, nail scissors, and pencil sharpener repeatedly vanish and reappear whenever he pretends not to care.

Intrigued, Dutton sets the objects out at night as bait. In the moonlight, he senses a tiny creature approach and sees what appears to be a slender hand slipping into the mirrored surface of the dressing table. When he lunges to catch it, his silver pencil sharpener disappears for good. Unable to recover it before leaving, he keeps the servant’s secret and buys a replacement in town.
