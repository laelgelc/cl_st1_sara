Montresor, nursing a secret grievance against Fortunato, vows to take revenge without being caught. During carnival, he exploits Fortunato’s pride in his knowledge of wine by claiming to possess a rare cask of Amontillado and suggesting that another connoisseur might authenticate it. Drunk and eager to prove his expertise, Fortunato follows Montresor deep into the family catacombs despite his worsening cough.

Montresor leads him to a remote recess, chains him to the wall, and reveals that the wine was merely a lure. He then slowly seals the recess with stone and mortar. As Fortunato sobers, he struggles, screams, and finally begs for mercy, but Montresor completes the wall and conceals it behind a pile of bones. Fifty years later, Fortunato’s remains are still undiscovered.
