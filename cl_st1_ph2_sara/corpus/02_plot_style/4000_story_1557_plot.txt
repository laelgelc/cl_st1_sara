Webb Yeager resents that his capable wife, Santa, rules the Nopalito Ranch’s business affairs while he merely manages the cattle. After she publicly overrules his choice of cattle buyer, he leaves her and becomes manager of another ranch.

Nearly a year later, Webb orders a herd of Sussex cattle from Nopalito. Still loving him, Santa secretly brands a white heifer with a heart enclosing a cross—the signal she used during their forbidden courtship whenever she wanted him to come to her. When the herd reaches Webb, he recognizes the sign and immediately rides back to Nopalito.

The couple reconcile, each relinquishing pride and claims to royalty. Santa introduces Webb to their infant son, calling the child the ranch’s true king. Webb resumes his place at home, now content to share authority with Santa, while the branded heifer remains evidence of the message that reunited them.
