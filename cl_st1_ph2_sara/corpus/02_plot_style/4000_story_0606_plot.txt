Sir Cloud Malpas, a prominent landowner and philanthropist, returns home unexpectedly and finds a letter from his wife, Gertrude, declaring that she is leaving him for Italy. She accuses him of neglecting her in favor of his public duties and insists their marriage is over. Shocked, Cloud recognizes some truth in her accusations.

Gertrude then returns to retrieve the letter, unaware that Cloud has read it. Concealing this fact, he pretends to be ill, and her concern revives their forgotten tenderness. He proposes that they take a long holiday together, offering the possibility of repairing their marriage.

That evening, Cloud confides in Gertrude’s cousin, Lord Bargrave, who urges him to abandon his work temporarily, devote himself to his wife, and never reveal that he read the letter. Meanwhile, Gertrude tells Lady Bargrave that she returned only because she feared the unsealed letter might be read by a servant. Believing Cloud never saw it, she burns the letter and decides to attempt a fresh start. Thus both spouses embrace reconciliation, each mistakenly believing the other does not know the full truth.
