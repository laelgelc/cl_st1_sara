After ten years abroad, a shy but newly prosperous man returns to the grand London home of the woman he has always loved. Believing her widowed, he hopes only to see her again, though he regrets that poverty and timidity once prevented him from declaring himself.

Because his common surname causes his telegram to be mistaken for that of a professional visitor, he is admitted and left alone for tea. The woman then appears exactly as he remembers her, welcomes him lovingly, and reveals that she had understood and returned his feelings all along. When he reaches for her, however, she says that he is not yet free as she is and laments that he never spoke.

A butler abruptly enters and asks him to bring his papers upstairs to meet the current lady of the house. The visitor realizes he has been conversing with the spirit of his beloved, who died without his knowing. Overcome, he makes excuses and leaves, briefly glimpsing the young woman who now holds her former title—the wife of the late woman’s stepson.
