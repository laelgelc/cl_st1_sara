Gerald “Jerry” Hutton is disappointed when his admission to Winthrop Academy’s exclusive Lyceum society is postponed, largely because senior Herbert Welch believes he has not yet proved himself. Jerry resents Welch, who has disliked him since Jerry intervened when Welch was hazing a freshman, and fears Welch will also prevent him from making the baseball team.

During a late-winter thaw, Jerry walks into town while many students skate on the river’s weakening ice. On his return, he sees a skater stranded on a broken ice floe and being swept downstream. Realizing a rescue is possible only from the bridge, Jerry races there despite falling and injuring his shoulder. The stranded boy turns out to be Welch. Jerry leans over the bridge, catches Welch’s wrist as the floe breaks apart, and holds him above the rushing water until other students arrive and pull them both to safety.

Jerry suffers a broken collarbone, but Welch is unharmed. Afterward, Welch declares that he will personally nominate Jerry for the Lyceum, as Jerry’s courage and selflessness have finally “proved” his worth.
