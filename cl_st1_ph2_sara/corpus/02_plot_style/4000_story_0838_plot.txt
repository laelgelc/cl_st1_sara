Azélie, the daughter of an impoverished tenant farmer, regularly visits plantation manager ’Polyte for provisions her family cannot afford. Irritated by her demands yet fascinated by her, ’Polyte refuses several items. That night he catches her breaking into the store to take them. Although he could have her arrested, he lets her go and promises to provide whatever she needs.

The encounter awakens an obsessive love in ’Polyte. He supplies Azélie’s family at his own expense, seeks her company, and showers her with affection and gifts, though she neither returns his feelings nor understands his moral judgment of her. Hoping to rescue her from her family’s influence, he asks her to marry him and remain on the plantation when they leave. Azélie refuses, choosing her father and their home on Little River.

When Azélie departs with her family, ’Polyte becomes unable to continue his work. He resigns from the plantation and decides to follow her to Little River.
