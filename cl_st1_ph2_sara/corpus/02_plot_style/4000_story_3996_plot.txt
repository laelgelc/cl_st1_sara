Mary Hill leaves her quiet Canadian hometown for New York, hoping to become a stenographer. After struggling to find work, she accepts a position with Joe Rendal, an awkward former admirer who has become a confident, successful broker. Resenting his authority, Mary turns for comfort to their charming hometown friend Eddy Moore, who helped secure her job.

As Mary works with Joe, she grows to admire his competence and discovers that his old love for her remains. Meanwhile, Joe warns her that Eddy is untrustworthy. Mary initially defends Eddy, but he eventually asks her to betray Joe by secretly copying a valuable business letter. Joe, anticipating this scheme because Eddy had similarly corrupted Mary’s predecessor, exposes him with deliberately misdirected letters.

Realizing that Eddy’s charm conceals selfishness while Joe is loyal and sincere, Mary admits Joe was right. When Joe confesses that he still loves her, she reveals that New York has changed her feelings too, and she now returns his affection.
