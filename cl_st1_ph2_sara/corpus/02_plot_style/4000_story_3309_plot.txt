Don Alonzo Pitkin is a shy, physically disabled youth who lives with his kindhearted brother Joe and sister-in-law Mira. When Joe is away, Mira learns that two burglars have been targeting vulnerable households nearby. Grateful for Mira’s loving care, Don Alonzo vows to protect her, though he worries that he lacks the strength to confront grown men.

That night, he hears the burglars approaching and devises a plan inspired by an old story. Using luminous paint from his chemical experiments, he disguises himself as a glowing skeleton. When the thieves try to break in, he leaps onto one burglar’s back and lashes him with Joe’s whip. Terrified by the apparent supernatural creature, both men flee through the village, raising enough commotion to awaken the neighbors.

Mira initially mistakes Don Alonzo for a real apparition and faints, but she soon learns the truth. The neighbors celebrate the formerly reclusive boy as a hero, and he confidently recounts his exploit instead of hiding from visitors. When Joe returns, Mira proudly declares that Don Alonzo has proved himself the greater man.
