Tina Lenz, the flirtatious daughter of a prosperous Lake Como hotelkeeper, defies her father by favoring Pietro, a poor and volatile Italian carriage driver. After her father dismisses Pietro, the couple continue meeting secretly, but Tina’s interest fades when Pietro proves capable of discussing little beyond horses.

Walter Standish, an English watercolor artist, arrives at the hotel and becomes fascinated with Tina, repeatedly painting her while they teach each other Italian and English. They fall genuinely in love, though Tina conceals her former relationship with Pietro. Jealous and vengeful, Pietro attacks Standish with a stiletto, but Standish blocks the thrust and knocks him unconscious. Tina persuades everyone that the attacker must have mistaken Standish for someone else, while Pietro flees across the mountains.

After Tina nurses Standish through his recovery, they marry and set out across the Stelvio Pass. Pietro secretly replaces their driver and sends the carriage racing down the dangerous mountain road, intending to plunge himself, Tina, and Standish over a precipice. Standish jumps from the carriage, uses a shortcut to catch up, lands on its footboard, and kicks Pietro onto the road. He then stops the horses and saves Tina. She prevents him from returning to confront Pietro, thereby keeping her past hidden, and the newlyweds continue on to live happily together.
