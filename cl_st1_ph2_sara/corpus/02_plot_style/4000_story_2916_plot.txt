Jessamine Stacy, a country woman forced to live in the city after losing her family home, becomes an unpaid servant in her brother John’s household. Overworked, neglected, and desperately homesick, she grows thin and unhappy under her demanding sister-in-law.

When the usual vegetable seller breaks his leg, his nephew, Andrew Bell, temporarily takes over his route. He notices Jessamine’s longing for the countryside and begins bringing her wildflowers. After learning that she is unmarried and merely treated like a servant in her brother’s home, Andrew befriends her and invites her on a country drive to Cloverside Farm. There, welcomed by his aunt and surrounded by fields, woods, flowers, and animals, Jessamine briefly regains her happiness and vitality.

Andrew realizes he loves her and wants to give her the affectionate country home she craves. After his uncle resumes the vegetable route, Andrew is absent during the harvest. When he returns, he learns that John has failed in business and that Jessamine is expected to move west with the family. Andrew immediately asks her to marry him and live at Cloverside Farm. Overwhelmed that someone truly cares for her, Jessamine accepts. Despite her sister-in-law’s resentment, Andrew marries Jessamine and takes her back to the countryside, where she finds lasting love and happiness.
