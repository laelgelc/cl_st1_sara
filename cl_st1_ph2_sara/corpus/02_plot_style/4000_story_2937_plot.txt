A surgeon is summoned to an Indian rajah’s palace and saves a woman in the royal household. During his stay, he becomes interested in Neranya, a violent but intelligent servant. After Neranya murders a dwarf, the rajah orders his right arm amputated. When Neranya later attacks the rajah, his remaining arm is also removed, turning his former devotion to his master into consuming hatred.

Despite his disability, Neranya learns to use his legs and feet with extraordinary skill. The rajah’s beloved son is then found murdered and mutilated, with both arms removed. The surgeon investigates, proves Neranya’s guilt, and persuades the rajah not to execute him by torture. Instead, the rajah has Neranya’s legs broken and amputated, then confines his limbless body in an elevated iron cage within the palace’s great hall, intending to torment him indefinitely.

Neranya initially curses the rajah but eventually becomes quiet and seemingly resigned. One night, while the rajah sleeps beneath the balcony, the surgeon secretly watches Neranya tear his clothing into strips, fashion a rope, and use astonishing strength and ingenuity to escape his cage. He drags himself across the hall and laboriously climbs the staircase to the balcony. Appearing to seek freedom, he instead positions himself above the sleeping rajah and deliberately falls onto him, crushing his chest and biting through his throat. The surgeon separates them, but both are fatally injured. Neranya dies smiling, triumphant in having finally achieved his revenge.
