Drusilla, a devoted milkmaid, lives with her elderly father and tends their rare white cow with golden horns, whose extraordinary milk supports them. One day, after Drusilla falls asleep in the meadow, the cow mysteriously disappears. While searching for it, she meets the King, who promises to find the cow if she agrees to become his adopted princess and prepare to marry the Prince of Egypt.

Drusilla reluctantly leaves her father and attends a royal seminary, where she excels in courtly accomplishments but remains homesick. Despite an exhaustive search, the King cannot find the cow. A jealous student spreads the rumor that Drusilla invented it to become a princess, and after a year the King believes the accusation and sends her home in disgrace.

Drusilla joyfully reunites with her father, but they worry about surviving without the cow or royal support. The cow then suddenly returns, carrying baskets filled with gold and jewels. Convinced of Drusilla’s honesty, the King again offers to make her a princess and marry her to the Egyptian prince. She refuses, preferring to remain with her father and care for the cow.

Now wealthy, Drusilla replaces their cottage with a castle resembling their old home and gives the cow a splendid stable. The King and Queen continue to visit and eventually persuade her to become a countess, while she remains happiest living with her father and tending her beloved cow.
