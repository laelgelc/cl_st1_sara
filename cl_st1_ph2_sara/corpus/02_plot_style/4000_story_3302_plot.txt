Six sailors stranded in an Arctic hut after their shipwreck endure months of darkness, isolation, illness, and mounting psychological strain. One night, something outside lifts the door latch. Long Ede goes onto the roof to investigate and discovers bloody footprints made by bare human feet. Fearing the sight will drive his companions mad, he conceals the tracks, then collapses from exposure outside the door.

After being revived, Ede repeatedly counts seven people in the hut, although only six men remain alive, suggesting the presence of a mysterious seventh companion. From that morning onward, the group’s spirits and health unexpectedly improve. Days later, Ede asks that the sleeping bag he left outside be placed in their dead shipmate Bill’s unused hammock. When a man climbs onto the roof to retrieve it, he discovers that the sun has finally returned.

By June, all six men have recovered and are rescued by a whaling fleet. Ede believes the unexplained footprints, seventh presence, and revival of hope were a divine miracle, while the group’s leader cautiously accepts that some providential force may have preserved them.
