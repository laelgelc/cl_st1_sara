Lord Oxhead faces financial ruin and fears that a lifelong secret will be exposed. His daughter, Gwendoline, announces her engagement to Edwin Einstein, a wealthy American whose father is in business. Though initially appalled by the family’s commercial background, the earl becomes interested upon learning the size of Edwin’s fortune.

When Edwin enters, Lord Oxhead recognizes—or appears to recognize—him, cries out, and suddenly dies. The cause remains unexplained. Edwin then marries Gwendoline in a deliberately austere ceremony and afterward becomes noticeably better dressed and more fluent in English. Whatever secret troubled the earl dies with him.
