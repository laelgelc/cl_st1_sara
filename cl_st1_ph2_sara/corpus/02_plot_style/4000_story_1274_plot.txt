A young Colorado woman struggling to find work in Chicago takes comfort in reading her hometown newspaper at the library. There she meets an older Colorado woman who is rapidly going blind and desperately longs to return home for one final sight of the mountains associated with her husband, children, and happiest memories. The woman cannot afford the journey, and her relatives lack the means or understanding to help her.

Though she has only eleven dollars left herself, the young woman donates five dollars and posts an appeal beside the Denver newspaper, asking fellow homesick Coloradans to contribute. Library patrons—many poor workers unable even to subscribe to the paper—respond generously because they understand the older woman’s longing.

Enough money is raised to send her home. She reaches Denver safely and returns to the mountains before sunset, where she watches the light fade and the shadows deepen over the landscape she loves, finally finding peace before her sight disappears.
