Tidbury Epps is a timid, conscientious clerk who has spent ten years at a New York hat company without advancement. When an assistant sales manager position opens, the company’s leaders pass him over because, despite his reliability, they consider him dull and incapable of entertaining important clients. The promotion instead goes to Hydeman, a flashy, worldly newcomer.

Tidbury’s passivity stems from a harsh childhood under his puritanical Aunt Elvira, who taught him that enjoyment was sinful. His uneventful life begins to change when he falls in love with Martha Ritter, a new employee. Although they court, Martha grows frustrated by his excessive quietness and lack of initiative. She soon becomes interested in Hydeman, whose confidence and sophistication contrast sharply with Tidbury’s diffidence.

After seeing Hydeman take Martha to lunch, Tidbury experiences intense jealousy for the first time. Hydeman later approaches him, boasts that Tidbury would envy his plans for that night, and produces two tickets, apparently signaling a date or outing that may involve Martha.
