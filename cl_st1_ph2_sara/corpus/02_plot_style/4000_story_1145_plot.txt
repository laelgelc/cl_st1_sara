Aimee Fox, the sole woman in a touring theatrical company, becomes desperately lonely during an unexpected day off in a dreary hotel. Seeking female companionship, she summons Pearlie Schultz, the hotel’s stout, practical stenographer. The two quickly bond over clothes, sewing patterns, personal memories, and the frustrations of women traveling alone.

Pearlie invites Aimee to help at a church strawberry social, introducing her as “Ethel Evans” to avoid scandalizing the congregation. Beautifully dressed and rejuvenated, Aimee charms the town’s young men, draws a large crowd, and helps make the fundraiser an exceptional success. After enjoying an evening of friendship and attention, she reveals to her admirers that she is actually an older professional actress who will be leaving town early the next morning.
