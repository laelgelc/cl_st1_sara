A bacteriologist shows a strange, pale visitor what he claims is a tube of living cholera bacteria, vividly explaining how it could devastate London’s water supply. The visitor, secretly an anarchist, steals the tube and flees by cab, intending to poison a reservoir. Realizing the theft, the bacteriologist races after him, while his concerned wife follows with her hatless, shoeless husband’s clothes.

During the chase, the tube breaks in the anarchist’s hand. Believing himself contaminated and determined to become a martyr, he drinks the remaining liquid, stops fleeing, and boasts that he has released cholera. He then walks away, deliberately brushing against people to infect them. The bacteriologist reveals to his wife that the tube did not contain cholera at all, but an experimental bacterium that causes blue patches on animals. He is amused by what may happen to the anarchist, though annoyed that he must prepare another culture.
