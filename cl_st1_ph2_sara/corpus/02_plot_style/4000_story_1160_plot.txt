At a summer cottage, carefree Julie Marvis lingers in the only bathtub while her more conservative sister, Lois, impatiently waits to bathe before meeting Mr. Calkins, a divorced literary man her mother dislikes. After discovering that Julie entered without clothes, a robe, or even a towel, Lois indignantly leaves and takes the towel with her.

Mr. Calkins then appears outside the bathroom window. Unable to see inside, he mistakes Julie for Lois, while Julie mischievously allows the error to continue. They banter about clothing, literature, and love as Julie invents explanations for the sounds of bathwater. Calkins finally declares his love, just as the real Lois returns. Horrified to find him speaking to her naked sister, Lois faints. Calkins announces that he is coming inside to help her, while Julie grabs Lois’s dropped towel and prepares to escape the bathtub.
