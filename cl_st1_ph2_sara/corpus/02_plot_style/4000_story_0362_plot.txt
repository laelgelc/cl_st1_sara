Camillo begins an affair with Rita, the wife of his childhood friend Villela. After anonymous letters expose their relationship, Camillo withdraws from Villela’s household, while Rita consults a fortune-teller who assures her that their secret is safe.

When Villela urgently summons Camillo to his home, Camillo fears their affair has been discovered. On the way, he impulsively visits the same fortune-teller, who convinces him that Villela knows nothing and that neither lover is in danger. Reassured, Camillo proceeds confidently to the house, only to find Rita murdered. Villela then seizes Camillo and shoots him dead.
