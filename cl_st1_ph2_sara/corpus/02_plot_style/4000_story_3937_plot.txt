Hughie Erskine is a charming but penniless young man who cannot marry Laura Merton until he acquires £10,000. While visiting his painter friend Alan Trevor, Hughie sees a miserable-looking beggar posing as a model. Moved by compassion, he gives the man a sovereign despite having little money himself.

Later, Alan reveals that the “beggar” is actually Baron Hausberg, one of Europe’s wealthiest men, who had commissioned the portrait. Hughie is deeply embarrassed, but the Baron is impressed by his generosity. The next morning, the Baron sends Hughie a cheque for £10,000 as a wedding present, enabling him to marry Laura.
