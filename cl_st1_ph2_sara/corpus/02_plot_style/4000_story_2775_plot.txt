At a stag dinner, Pierre Letoile explains how he married his charming wife despite having been determined to remain single. While attending a cousin’s wedding in Normandy, he becomes extremely drunk after dancing and drinking with the peasants. Trying to find his bedroom in the dark, he mistakenly enters the room of Mademoiselle Dumoulin, the colonel’s daughter, and falls asleep in an armchair beside her bed.

The next morning, the colonel discovers Pierre and attacks him, creating a scandal. Although Pierre insists that nothing happened, no one believes his drunken explanation, and the young woman’s reputation is considered ruined. Threatened by her father and pressured by his relatives, Pierre reluctantly proposes marriage.

The wedding follows before he can escape the arrangement. On their wedding night, the distressed bride offers to obey him completely, even to kill herself if he wishes. Moved by her beauty and sincerity, Pierre embraces her. Five years later, he considers the accidental marriage a fortunate bargain and has no regrets.
