During an Atlantic crossing, a passenger encounters a starving stowaway hiding among folded deck chairs. Moved by the man’s claims of poverty and fear of punishment, he secretly feeds him and promises not to report him.

The passenger confides in Roger Cupples, a supposed San Francisco lawyer. Cupples warns that aiding a stowaway carries severe legal penalties and advises him to report the man before witnesses. Later, the passenger discovers Cupples applying makeup and realizes that Cupples has been impersonating the stowaway as an elaborate practical joke.

Turning the trick against him, the passenger alerts the ship’s officers and gathers other passengers to witness the capture. Sailors drag the disguised Cupples from hiding despite his protests that he is a cabin passenger. Cupples eventually accepts the humiliation and buys drinks for everyone. He is later revealed to be a New York actor, and he performs the same stowaway character at a shipboard charity entertainment.
