Jimmy Doyle, the sheltered son of a wealthy Dublin merchant, joins his sophisticated European acquaintances after a motor race. Thrilled by their status, wealth, and cosmopolitan glamour, he is also preparing to invest much of his money in Charles Segouin’s planned motor business.

After an elegant dinner, Jimmy and the group meet an American named Farley and continue their celebration aboard Farley’s yacht. They drink, dance, make speeches, and eventually begin gambling. Intoxicated and careless, Jimmy repeatedly misplays his cards and signs I.O.U.s without understanding how much he is losing. By daybreak, he and Farley are the heaviest losers. Exhausted and ashamed, Jimmy realizes he will regret his reckless folly once morning comes.
