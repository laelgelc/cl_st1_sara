In rural Corsica, ten-year-old Fortunato, son of the respected marksman Mateo Falcone, is left guarding the family home. A wounded outlaw, Gianetto Sanpiero, arrives while fleeing soldiers and pays Fortunato to hide him in a haystack. Fortunato conceals him skillfully and initially resists the soldiers’ threats, but betrays Gianetto when their commander offers him an expensive watch.

When Mateo returns and learns what happened, he is horrified that his son has violated the Corsican code of loyalty and brought disgrace upon the family. After smashing the watch, Mateo leads Fortunato into a ravine, makes him say his prayers, and shoots him. He tells his wife that he has carried out justice and prepares to bury the boy, arranging for a son-in-law to become his heir.
