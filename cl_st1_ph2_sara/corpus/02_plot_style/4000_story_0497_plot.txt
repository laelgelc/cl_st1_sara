A newspaper condemns the captain of the ocean liner Arrowic for supposedly risking nearly a thousand lives by leaving New York Harbor in dense fog to race the rival Dartonia. A passenger aboard later recounts what actually happened. Both ships depart close together, but the worsening fog forces them to halt amid dangerous traffic. While the Dartonia anchors to await clear weather, the Arrowic’s experienced captain and pilot carefully exploit brief openings in the windblown fog, advancing only whenever visibility returns. Racing against the tide rather than recklessly against the other vessel, the captain times each movement until a final clearing allows the Arrowic to cross the bar safely and enter the Atlantic.
