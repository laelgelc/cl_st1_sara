Iranon, an eternally youthful wandering singer, arrives in the grim city of Teloth claiming to be an exiled prince searching for his beautiful homeland, Aira. The practical townspeople reject his songs and order him to work, but a boy named Romnod shares his longing for beauty and leaves with him to seek the distant city of Oonai.

After years of wandering, they reach Oonai, where Iranon’s songs initially earn admiration and luxury. Romnod embraces the city’s drunken revelry, grows old and coarse, and eventually dies, while Iranon remains young and devoted to his dream of Aira. Forgotten when new entertainers capture Oonai’s attention, Iranon resumes his search.

At last, an aged shepherd recognizes Iranon’s descriptions as fantasies invented in childhood by a local beggar’s son. He reveals that Aira never existed and that Iranon was never a prince. Confronted with the truth that his identity and lifelong quest were built on dreams, Iranon suddenly becomes an old man and walks into lethal quicksand, still imagining the golden city where his songs might finally be understood.
