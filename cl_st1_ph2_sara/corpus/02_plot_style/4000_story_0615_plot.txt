Richard Morfe and his sister Mary, a deeply attached but habit-bound bachelor and spinster, reserve every Friday evening for each other. Their routine is disrupted when Eva Harracles, a singer in Richard’s choir, arrives with a false rumor that the tenor Simon Loggerheads is leaving for a rival choir. When the drawing-room clock appears to stop, Eva misses her last connection home, and Mary surprisingly urges Richard to escort her.

Once outside, Richard and Eva reveal that they have secretly become engaged. Neither realizes that Mary has deliberately stopped the clock to get Richard out of the house. Soon afterward, Simon Loggerheads arrives; he and Mary are also secretly engaged and had planned to tell Richard that evening. Alone together, they celebrate and discuss how to break the news to him. Mary dismisses gossip about Richard and Eva, while Richard similarly dismisses rumors about Mary and Simon.

Mary later confesses to Simon that she stopped the clock specifically to secure time alone with him. Simon departs just as Richard returns from accompanying Eva, and the two men pass without revealing anything. Both siblings intend to announce their engagements at breakfast the next morning, each believing the news will completely astonish the other.
