After being swindled by Jonas Medderbrook and told by his beloved Syrilla that a faulty scale proves she weighs “five pounds less than nothing” and therefore cannot marry him, amateur detective Philo Gubb falls into despair. A hungry tramp named Chi Foxy later witnesses a couple secretly burying bones, then invents an elaborate story claiming to be both a famous detective and a murderer so Gubb will arrest and feed him. Foxy leads Gubb to the buried bones and slips away, but the bones prove to be those of a stolen calf. The homeowner immediately confesses to stealing it, allowing Gubb to solve his current case by accident.

Foxy later returns seeking another meal and casually reveals that he recently saw Syrilla alive and still fat at a circus, where Medderbrook had arranged for her to marry the Living Skeleton. Realizing Syrilla and Medderbrook deceived him, Gubb is so devastated that he never laughs again.
