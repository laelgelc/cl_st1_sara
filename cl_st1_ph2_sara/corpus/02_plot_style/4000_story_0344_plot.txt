Kate Darlington, a wealthy and perceptive young woman, rejects the shallow fashionable men attracted by her beauty and fortune. She has grown up alongside Edwin Lee, the orphaned son of her father’s friend, whom her father has taken into his home and employed as a trusted clerk.

As Kate matures, Edwin realizes that he loves her. Believing it dishonorable and ungrateful to court his benefactor’s daughter, he decides to leave both the household and the city by taking a position aboard a ship bound for Valparaiso. He refuses to explain his sudden decision despite the family’s efforts to persuade him to stay.

During a private conversation, Kate recognizes from Edwin’s embarrassment that he is secretly in love with her. She tells her father that she is the object of Edwin’s affection and reveals that she now regards him as more than a brother. Although her father initially notes Edwin’s poverty, Kate defends his virtue, intelligence, and honor and asks her father to prevent his departure.

The next evening, Kate and her father again urge Edwin to remain. When Kate asks whether he will be happier away, Edwin admits that he will be miserable. Her father tactfully leaves them alone, and Edwin abandons his voyage. Kate eventually marries him despite society’s surprise and ridicule, choosing genuine worth and affection over wealth and fashion, and becomes a happy wife.
