In colonial New England, friends Submit Thompson and Sarah Adams argue over whose family has the heavier Thanksgiving turkey. They wager prized possessions: Submit’s work-box against Sarah’s beloved doll, Thankful. Although Submit learns that Sarah’s turkey is likely larger, her desire for the doll leads her to secretly stuff Revolutionary-era bullets into her family’s turkey before it is weighed. The trick makes the Thompson turkey appear heavier, so a heartbroken Sarah surrenders Thankful.

Questioned by her family, Submit admits both the wager and her deception. The doll is returned, and both girls are sent to their rooms for gambling. From facing windows, they confess their sorrow and reconcile. Because Submit told the truth, she is forgiven and allowed to join Thanksgiving dinner, as is Sarah. Sarah’s visiting aunt then promises to make Submit a doll like Thankful, provided she behaves well.
