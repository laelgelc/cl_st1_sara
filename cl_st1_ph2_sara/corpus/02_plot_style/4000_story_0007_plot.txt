Mortazu Khan races across the Afghan mountains to fetch an English doctor for his wife, Azeena, who is suffering a dangerous childbirth. Though speed is essential, he is attacked by a mother bear while crossing a plateau. Unarmed except for a dagger, he takes refuge behind a tree and spends hours evading her charges, growing exhausted as precious time slips away.

After wounding the bear, Mortazu discovers that she is defending a cub hidden in the tree. Recognizing that both of them are fighting to protect their families, he confronts her without hatred and kills her in a desperate close struggle, suffering deep wounds himself. Moved by sympathy, he takes the orphaned cub with him and resumes his urgent journey toward Ghuzni, hoping the doctor can save Azeena and their unborn child.
