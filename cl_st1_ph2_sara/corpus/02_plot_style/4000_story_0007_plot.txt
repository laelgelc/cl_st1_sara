Mortazu Khan hurries across the Afghan mountains to fetch an English doctor for his wife, Azeena, who is suffering through a dangerous childbirth. Though speed is essential, he is attacked by a mother bear and becomes trapped in a prolonged struggle around a fir tree. Armed only with a dagger, he wounds the bear but gradually exhausts himself as precious hours pass.

When he discovers a cub hiding in the tree, Mortazu understands that the bear, like him, is fighting to protect her young. With compassion and respect rather than hatred, he confronts her in the open and kills her by stabbing her heart, suffering severe wounds himself. He then takes the orphaned cub, intending it as a companion for his hoped-for son, and continues toward Ghuzni for the doctor, bleeding but still hurrying.
