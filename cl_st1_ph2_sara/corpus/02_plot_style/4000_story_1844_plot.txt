Roloff Van Ripper writes to a magazine editor claiming that the Dutch descendants around Communipaw have maintained a secret, generations-long conspiracy to reclaim New Amsterdam from the Yankees. Through intermarriage, traditional gatherings, and organizations such as the Society of Saint Nicholas, they preserve Dutch identity while awaiting their eventual restoration to power. Van Ripper interprets the spread of “Knickerbocker” institutions and the reverent attention paid to Communipaw as signs that their triumph is approaching. His confidence collapses when a Yankee elopes with a Dutch heiress, marries her, and divides her cabbage garden into town lots for sale—suggesting that commercial development and assimilation may defeat the conspiracy from within.
