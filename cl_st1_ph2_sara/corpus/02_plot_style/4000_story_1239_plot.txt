After Edward Glynn dies suddenly following a bitter argument with his physician brother Henry, their three sisters privately suspect that Henry may have poisoned him. Their fears intensify when a life-sized shadow resembling Edward appears on the study wall and cannot be explained or removed. Henry reacts with panic, repeatedly rearranging the furniture and even thrusting a sword through the space where the shadow should originate, but it remains unchanged.

Henry soon leaves for the city on unexplained business and fails to return as expected. When the sisters finally reenter the study and light the lamp, they discover that two shadows now haunt the wall. At that moment, a telegram arrives announcing that Henry is dead.
