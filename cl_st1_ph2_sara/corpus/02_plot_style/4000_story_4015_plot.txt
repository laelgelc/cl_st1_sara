On Samhain Eve, Red Hanrahan, a hedge schoolmaster and poet, receives word that his sweetheart, Mary Lavelle, wants him to come and marry her before she loses her land. As he prepares to leave, a mysterious old man persuades him to join a card game. The game becomes supernatural: the old man conjures a hare and a pack of hounds, and Hanrahan follows their wild hunt into the mountains.

There he enters an enchanted house and encounters a beautiful, waiting queen and four old women bearing treasures representing Pleasure, Power, Courage, and Knowledge. Overawed and unable to speak or choose among them, he is judged weak and fearful, and the queen remains asleep. Hanrahan then falls unconscious.

He awakens outdoors with no memory of what happened and wanders for an entire year, becoming thin, wild, and seemingly unable to read. On the next Samhain, handling cards restores his memory of Mary. He hurries to her home, only to find it abandoned. During his absence, Mary was evicted, married a laborer, and left for a distant city. Hanrahan never sees or hears of her again.
