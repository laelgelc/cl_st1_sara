After fifteen years away, Lovell Stevens returns to visit Aunt Sally and Uncle Tom, the couple who took him in as an orphan and raised him. He discovers that illness and debt cost them their house and possessions, forcing them into the poorhouse shortly before their fiftieth wedding anniversary.

Grateful for their past kindness, Lovell secretly buys back their home and, with help from the community, recovers their old furniture and arranges a golden-wedding celebration. On the anniversary, he brings the couple home to find their house restored and filled with friends. He also gives them enough money to live independently for years.

The sacrifice consumes nearly all the savings Lovell intended to use to start a business, leaving him only enough for his return journey west. Nevertheless, he considers Aunt Sally’s happiness, the couple’s blessings, and his own ability to work more valuable than the money he has given up.
