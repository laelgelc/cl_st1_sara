While traveling through a dark ravine near Auburn, Mr. Manrich encounters Dr. Dorrimore, a sinister man he met five years earlier. At that first meeting, Dorrimore demonstrated apparent Hindu “jugglery” by making Manrich see his murdered double lying in the street, then rising with a sword through his chest before the entire scene vanished.

After their reunion, Manrich’s fiancée, Margaret Corray, and her mother seemingly arrive in Auburn. Dorrimore charms them, provoking Manrich’s jealousy as Margaret increasingly spends time with him. One night, Manrich sees Margaret meet Dorrimore in an abandoned cemetery and attacks in a murderous rage. He is later found beaten and nearly strangled and remains delirious for days, while Dorrimore disappears.

Upon recovering, Manrich learns that Margaret and her mother were never in Auburn. Margaret, now his wife, confirms that she was in Oakland throughout the entire period. A later newspaper report identifies Dorrimore as a powerful hypnotist trained in India who can impose vivid, prolonged hallucinations upon susceptible subjects, revealing that Manrich’s experiences—including Margaret’s visit and perhaps much more—were hypnotic illusions engineered by Dorrimore.
