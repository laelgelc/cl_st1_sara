Newly self-styled Irish baronet Sir Patrick O’Grandison becomes convinced that his widowed neighbor, Mrs. Tracle, is in love with him. A diminutive French count, who also desires her, offers to introduce Patrick, and the two visit her together. Seated on either side of the widow, both men secretly reach behind the sofa and believe they are squeezing her hand, interpreting each answering squeeze as encouragement.

When Patrick angrily insults the Frenchman, Mrs. Tracle abruptly leaves. Patrick then discovers that he and his rival have actually been holding and squeezing each other’s hands the entire time. Her footman throws them both downstairs, but Patrick crushes the Frenchman’s hand before releasing it, leaving the rival’s hand injured and in a sling.
