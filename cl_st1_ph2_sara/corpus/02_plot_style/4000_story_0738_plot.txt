Spaceman Leonard Sale crashes on an apparently harmless planetoid and radios for rescue, which is due in six days. Whenever he begins to sleep, however, his mind is invaded by the surviving disembodied intelligences of two ancient enemy nations. Their leaders, Iorr and Tylle, intend to use Sale’s body and brain as a battlefield on which to resume their millennia-old war.

Realizing that sleep may leave him insane and permanently possessed, Sale tries to remain awake with coffee, stimulants, reading, cards, and a rigid schedule. After several days, exhaustion overwhelms him. During one lapse, the armies battle inside his mind, and his half-conscious hand fires his gun, grazing his forehead and waking him. With two days still remaining before rescue, Sale grows increasingly delirious and suicidal, struggling desperately to stay awake as night falls and the hostile minds wait to seize him again.
