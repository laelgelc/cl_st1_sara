Bored with his immortal life, Popopo the knook visits a human city at night. In a millinery shop, he mistakes stuffed birds decorating women’s hats for living captives. As guardian of birds, he magically restores them to life and frees them, only to discover that their loss may ruin the milliner.

Trying to repair the damage, Popopo replaces the birds with mice, but the terrified milliner and her sister faint when the mice escape. He then considers returning the birds, but they argue that they were created free and should not be enslaved for human fashion. Unable to choose between the birds’ freedom and the milliner’s livelihood, Popopo consults the king of the knooks.

The king advises him to change human fashion itself. Popopo enchants newspapers and magazines throughout the land to declare that fashionable hats should use only ribbons and lace, not birds. The milliner’s business is saved, and Popopo travels from shop to shop reviving and freeing the discarded stuffed birds.
