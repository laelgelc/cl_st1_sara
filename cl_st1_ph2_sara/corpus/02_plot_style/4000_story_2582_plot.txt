A sixteen-year-old sailor joins the San Francisco Bay fish patrol and helps raid Chinese shrimp fishermen whose illegal fine-meshed nets destroy young fish. The patrol surprises the fleet at dawn, captures six junks, and divides the prisoners between two boats. The narrator takes a large group aboard his leaking sloop, the Reindeer, accompanied only by George, a sickly and cowardly patrolman.

As the Reindeer heads toward San Rafael, the prisoners discover that the narrator is unarmed and refuse to help bail the rapidly flooding vessel. Led by the defiant Yellow Handkerchief, they steal provisions, destabilize the boat, and demand to be returned to shore. George panics and urges surrender. When the narrator refuses, George points his revolver at him and fires, but the narrator wrestles the weapon away and regains control.

Even at gunpoint, the prisoners still refuse to bail. Seeing a strong breeze approaching, the narrator deliberately keeps the sail set, allowing the waterlogged Reindeer to heel dangerously until seawater pours aboard and everyone faces capsizing. The prisoners finally beg for mercy and begin bailing with every available container. Once the boat is saved, they become compliant and even tow it through the slough to San Rafael. George subsequently leaves the fish patrol for a safer job ashore.
