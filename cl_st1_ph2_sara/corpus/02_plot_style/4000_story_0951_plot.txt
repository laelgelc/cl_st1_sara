Bored schoolboy William resents the vain, angelic-looking Evangeline after she is elected May Queen and he is made part of her court. He befriends shy, overlooked Bettine, who admires him and secretly longs to be queen. Learning that Evangeline is exceptionally greedy, William spends a gift of five shillings on cakes and lures her into his woodshed just before the May Day celebration, leaving her absorbed in eating them.

William then dresses Bettine in stolen fairy costume pieces and a tinsel crown, outfits himself as a makeshift May King, and leads her onto the May Queen’s platform. The dance begins before their stunned teacher can intervene, while the audience assumes William’s bizarre costume and moustache are part of the pageant. Bettine enjoys her triumphant moment as queen beside William, while Evangeline remains in the shed finishing the cakes.
