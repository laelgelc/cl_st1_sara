Impoverished seamstress Miss Sophie attends a church wedding and faints when she recognizes the groom, Louis Neale, as the former fiancé who abandoned her. Later, she overhears that Neale faces ruin because he cannot claim an inheritance without a distinctive Roman ring he once gave her. Sophie had pawned the ring to help her dying father, but, still loving Neale and wishing to spare him and his new bride hardship, she works relentlessly, depriving herself of food and warmth to save enough money to redeem it.

On Christmas Eve, Sophie finally retrieves the ring and plans to return it anonymously. The next morning, her landlady finds her dead in her tidy room, the ring clasped to her cold breast beside a note telling Louis that she is returning it because she heard he needed it.
