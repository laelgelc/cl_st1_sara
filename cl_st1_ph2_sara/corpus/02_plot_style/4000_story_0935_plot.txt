A group of visitors tours a grim coal colliery, first watching child slate-pickers labor amid dust, deafening machinery, and crushing poverty. Guided deep underground, they traverse a dark maze of tunnels, meet miners drilling and blasting beneath unstable roofs, observe mule drivers and animals imprisoned below for years, and witness the constant dangers of gas, flooding, cave-ins, machinery, and lung disease. After ascending to the surface, they are overwhelmed by sunlight and fresh air, while the mine’s hidden struggle continues below and coal cars endlessly feed the monstrous breaker above.
