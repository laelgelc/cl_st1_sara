Amateur detective Philo Gubb agrees to investigate the theft of Mrs. Smith’s forty-cent hen, planning to collect his fee from the thief. Convinced the culprit will return, he spends two nights concealed atop an abandoned brick kiln near the chicken coop.

On the second night, while Gubb sleeps, two fugitives named Wixy and Sandlot hide beneath him and discuss murdering their associate, a criminal nicknamed “the Chicken.” Sandlot leaves after an argument, while the drunken Wixy falls asleep. Gubb awakens, tries to climb down, and accidentally lands on Wixy, knocking him unconscious. Believing he has captured the chicken thief, Gubb revives and interrogates him.

Wixy assumes Gubb is an agent of a criminal matriarch also named Mrs. Smith and believes he is being confronted over the murder of “the Chicken.” When Gubb demands payment for the chicken and his detective work, a misunderstanding over the amount leads Wixy to pay him five hundred dollars. Gubb accepts, thinking the stolen hen must have been extraordinarily valuable, and allows Wixy to leave. Only afterward does he begin to realize that Wixy was talking about a man rather than a bird, while the bewildered Mrs. Smith denies knowing any of the criminals involved.
