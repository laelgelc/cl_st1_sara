A newly commissioned lieutenant, recovering from fever and stranded alone in Christiania on Christmas Eve, expects to miss the holiday with his family. His elderly landladies invite him to join their small gathering, where he, the children, and several older women exchange folktales about trolls, brownies, and ghosts. The evening ends cheerfully with Christmas food and good wishes, but the supernatural stories, rich supper, and his weakened condition give the lieutenant a restless night filled with bizarre dreams.

He awakens to find Thor, his father’s servant, standing over him with a letter, a fur coat, and the welcome news that a horse is waiting outside. His father has sent Thor to bring him home if he is well enough to travel. Overjoyed, the lieutenant leaves the city by sledge and returns to his family’s house, where he receives a jubilant welcome and enjoys an exceptionally happy Christmas.
