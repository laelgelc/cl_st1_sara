An old villager tells a traveller about a conjurer who once performed at the local inn. After alarming the patrons by producing animals from their clothes and drinks, the conjurer stages a show in which he smashes Dicky Weed’s treasured gold watch, loads the pieces into a pistol, and claims that firing it will restore the watch in the target’s pocket. He shoots at Bob Pretty, a notorious rogue, who pretends to be wounded and flees, apparently keeping the restored watch.

When the conjurer returns, he exposes Bob’s injuries as fake and tries to repeat the trick with John Biggs’s silver watch. Bob deliberately smashes it himself, prompting the conjurer to refuse responsibility. The villagers nevertheless load the fragments into the pistol and insist on firing it at the conjurer so the repaired watch will appear in his pocket. Terrified, he tricks them into looking away and escapes just before the pistol destroys his empty chair. Bob, having stolen the first watch and ruined the second trick, smugly claims that he has vindicated his good name.
