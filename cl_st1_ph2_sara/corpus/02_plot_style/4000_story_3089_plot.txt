A New York boardinghouse reputed to be haunted initially produces no supernatural activity. One night, after smoking opium and discussing the nature of ultimate terror with his friend Dr. Hammond, the narrator is attacked in bed by a solid, breathing creature that is completely invisible. He wrestles it into submission, and Hammond confirms its existence by touch before helping bind it.

To discover its form, they anesthetize the captive and make a plaster cast, revealing a small, hairless, immensely muscular, grotesquely humanoid being. Unable to determine what it eats, unwilling either to release it or kill it, they keep it confined. The creature refuses all offered food, gradually starves to death, and is secretly buried in the garden, while its cast is preserved in a doctor’s museum.
