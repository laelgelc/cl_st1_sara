Thirty-year-old Bertha Young is overwhelmed by an inexplicable sense of bliss as she prepares to host a dinner party. Delighting in her baby, comfortable home, husband Harry, fashionable friends, and flowering pear tree, she believes her life is perfect. She feels an especially intense, almost mystical connection with the enigmatic Pearl Fulton, imagining that Pearl shares her overflowing joy. Together, they silently admire the pear tree, which Bertha sees as a symbol of her fulfilled life.

During the evening, Bertha’s heightened emotions awaken sexual desire for her husband for the first time. After the guests begin leaving, however, she secretly witnesses Harry and Pearl embracing and arranging to meet the next day, revealing their affair. Pearl then pointedly praises Bertha’s pear tree before departing. Shocked and uncertain about her future, Bertha looks outside and finds the tree still beautiful, flowering, and unchanged despite the collapse of her happiness.
