At seventeen, a young woman visiting her elderly friend, Miss Persis, is unexpectedly invited to help Madam Le Baron entertain her grandniece and a party of fashionable young guests. Since she owns no evening dress, Madam Le Baron lends her an old-fashioned green satin gown from her own youth. The girl is initially mortified at the thought of being ridiculed, but decides that pleasing her kind hostess matters more than her vanity. She completes the historical look shown in Madam Le Baron’s portrait, deeply touching the old woman, who lends her a treasured chrysoprase necklace.

Although some guests laugh at her outdated appearance, she endures the evening with dignity. A year later, after Madam Le Baron’s death, she inherits the gown, its accessories, the necklace, and a pocketful of gold coins. She also reveals that one young man at the gathering admired rather than mocked her: the man who later became her husband.
