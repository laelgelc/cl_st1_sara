After ten weeks of isolation, scientist Crawford Tillinghast summons his estranged friend to witness the success of a machine designed to awaken humanity’s dormant senses. The device enables them to perceive invisible dimensions overlapping ordinary reality, filled with alien worlds and strange, semi-fluid creatures that move through solid matter.

Tillinghast reveals that his servants vanished after electrical vibrations accidentally exposed them to these hidden realms. He then admits that he invited his friend not merely to demonstrate his discovery, but to take revenge for having discouraged his research. Predatory entities are approaching, and movement will make his guest vulnerable to them.

As Tillinghast urges him to look at a creature behind him, the terrified narrator fires his revolver into the machine, destroying it. Police find Tillinghast dead of apoplexy and the narrator unconscious. Although a doctor dismisses the experience as hypnosis induced by a homicidal madman, the narrator remains convinced it was real because the missing servants’ bodies are never found.
