Tommy Brock, a badger, visits old Mr. Bouncer while he is supposed to be watching Benjamin Bunny and Flopsy’s seven babies. After food, wine, and strong tobacco make Mr. Bouncer drowsy, Tommy Brock kidnaps the babies in a sack and carries them to one of Mr. Tod the fox’s empty houses.

Benjamin follows the badger’s trail and enlists Peter Rabbit’s help. They find Tommy Brock asleep in Mr. Tod’s bed and discover that the babies are alive but imprisoned inside the kitchen oven. Unable to enter the locked house, they spend the night digging a tunnel beneath the kitchen floor.

Mr. Tod returns in the morning and, furious to find Tommy Brock occupying his home, rigs a pail of water above the bed. Tommy Brock secretly replaces himself with a bundled dressing gown, so Mr. Tod believes his trap has killed him. When Mr. Tod enters to dispose of the body, he finds Tommy Brock calmly drinking tea. The two enemies begin a violent fight that carries them out of the house and down the hill.

With the house unguarded, Benjamin and Peter enter, rescue the babies from the oven, and carry them home in the sack. Flopsy joyfully welcomes them, Mr. Bouncer is forgiven for his carelessness, and the family celebrates their safe return.
