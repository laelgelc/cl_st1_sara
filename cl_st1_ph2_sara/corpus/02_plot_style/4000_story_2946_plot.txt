After losing part of his nose in a fight, a powerful Italian woodcutter receives a skin graft from his hair-covered scalp, leaving him with a grotesque tuft of hair around his reconstructed nose. He becomes Hoolagaloo, a highly paid “wild man” in Castellani’s dime museum.

Both Hoolagaloo and Sampey, a poor but clever writer, fall in love with Castellani’s daughter, Zoe, who performs as the illusionary “Severed Lady.” Zoe dreams that her destined hero will have pale amber eyes. To win her, Sampey secretly uses colored glass shells placed over his eyeballs, appearing before her with amber eyes and convincing her that he possesses hidden heroism. He also frightens the jealous Hoolagaloo into retreat by confronting him with scarlet-colored eyes.

Claiming he can change his eye color at will, Sampey persuades the commercially minded Castellani to make him a museum attraction. Realizing the act’s immense value, he negotiates a half-interest in the museum, becoming wealthy and acceptable as Zoe’s husband.

Hoolagaloo eventually discovers Sampey’s artificial eyes and exposes the deception during a celebratory supper. His triumph fails, however, when Sampey reveals that he and Zoe have already been married for thirty hours. Castellani supports his new son-in-law and business partner, while the defeated Hoolagaloo abandons show business and returns to chopping wood. Sampey remains a prosperous showman and happy husband.
