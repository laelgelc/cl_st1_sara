After being stabbed in the chest with an antique stiletto by a woman he refused to marry, the fearless young Arnold is told that removing the weapon would cause him to bleed to death. An inventive surgeon, Dr. Entrefort, realizes that the round blade has pierced the aorta without allowing blood to escape. He cuts off the ornate handle, leaves the blade inside Arnold, and prescribes muriatic acid for five years.

Four years later, Arnold is still alive but has become a reclusive hypochondriac, terrified that any movement or shock might dislodge the blade. Entrefort returns from abroad with his wife, who proves to be Arnold’s former assailant. When Arnold unexpectedly sees her, he believes the shock has loosened the blade and dies instantly from fear. An autopsy reveals that the acid had gradually dissolved the blade and that his aorta had completely healed; physically healthy, Arnold dies only because of his mistaken terror, allowing the woman to achieve her revenge unintentionally.
