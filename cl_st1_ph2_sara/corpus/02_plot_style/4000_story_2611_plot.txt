A philosophical traveler secretly leaves his tour group to explore forbidden passages in Mammoth Cave and becomes hopelessly lost. As his torch dies, he resigns himself to starvation but shouts in a final attempt to attract help. Instead, he hears a stealthy, apparently four-legged creature approaching through the darkness.

Believing a wild beast has caught his scent, he arms himself with stones. When the creature draws near, he throws them, striking and incapacitating it. He flees and soon encounters the guide, who has been searching for him for hours.

With the guide and his torch, the traveler returns to inspect his victim. They find a pale, emaciated, ape-like being adapted to cave life, with long claws, white skin and hair, and deeply sunken black eyes. As it dies, it rolls over and utters recognizable human speech, revealing that the supposed monster is—or once was—a man transformed by prolonged isolation in the cave.
