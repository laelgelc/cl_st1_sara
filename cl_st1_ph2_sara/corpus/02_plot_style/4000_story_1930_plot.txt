Trayton Burleigh has embezzled money from the business he shares with James Fletcher. Fletcher insists on repaying the loss to preserve the firm’s reputation, but orders Burleigh to leave their office and shared home, offering him two hundred pounds to start over. Enraged by Fletcher’s refusal to conceal the crime, Burleigh stabs him with a decorative Japanese sword and assumes he is dead.

While preparing to flee, Burleigh hears an intruder enter the house and discover Fletcher’s body in the library. Realizing he can frame the stranger, Burleigh locks him inside, fires through the door, and summons nearby police officers. The intruder is captured and protests that Fletcher was already lying there when he entered. Burleigh pretends to be horrified and claims the intruder must have taken the sword from the wall and committed the murder.

The police sergeant notices an injury on Burleigh’s wrist, disarms and handcuffs him, then reveals that Fletcher is still alive. A doctor revives the dying man long enough for him to identify Burleigh as his attacker. The sergeant records Fletcher’s statement and leads Burleigh away under arrest.
