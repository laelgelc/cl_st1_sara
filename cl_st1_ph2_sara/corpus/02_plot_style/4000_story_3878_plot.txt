Three horsemen pursue a half-caste girl and her companion across a desolate valley, led by an imperious nobleman enraged that she has fled him. As the wind rises, animals flee and enormous webs filled with giant spiders drift into the valley. The swarm attacks the riders, killing the loyal, scar-faced pursuer and scattering the others.

The nobleman and the surviving servant escape into a ravine. When the servant condemns both of them as cowards for abandoning their companion—and threatens the nobleman’s reputation—the nobleman kills him and takes his white horse. Believing the fugitives must also have perished, he prepares to leave, but then sees smoke from their camp across the valley. Once the wind dies and the spiders become harmless on the ground, he resumes the pursuit, reflecting that next time he must trap his prey through cunning rather than brute force.
