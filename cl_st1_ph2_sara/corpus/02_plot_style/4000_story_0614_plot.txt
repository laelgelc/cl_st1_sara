After a whirlwind courtship, Annie marries William Henry Brachett, a pottery worker in the Five Towns. Though happy with him, she finds married life monotonous and oppressively frugal. She especially resents his strict payments to a mysterious Going Away Club, for which he sacrifices even small comforts.

In August, William Henry reveals that the club has yielded twenty pounds for their holiday. The couple travel to Douglas on the Isle of Man, where his usual stinginess disappears. For six exhilarating days they enjoy lavish meals, excursions, entertainments, dances, and other pleasures, spending nearly all their money. They return to Hanbridge with only sixpence, but William Henry has prudently hidden six shillings at home to cover their arrival and immediate needs.

Their austere routine resumes, yet Annie no longer minds because the extravagant holiday has given her the excitement she craved. Soon William Henry begins paying into the Going Away Club again, preparing to repeat the escape the following year.
