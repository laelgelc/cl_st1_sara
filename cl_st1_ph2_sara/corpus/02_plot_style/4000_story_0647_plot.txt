A wealthy traveler visiting San Francisco reunites with his old friend Mohun Dampier, now prematurely aged, haunted, and convinced that death is near. During their stormy-night meeting, both men hear mysterious tapping from the tower’s exterior wall, though no one could possibly be there.

Dampier explains that ten years earlier he had silently fallen in love with a beautiful young woman living next door. Pride, class prejudice, and romantic idealism kept him from approaching her, but they began exchanging three-tap signals through their shared bedroom wall. When she suddenly stopped responding, he assumed she had rejected him. One night, after weeks of silence, he heard her faintly repeat their signal, but spitefully refused to answer. The next morning he learned that she had been gravely ill and had died during the night. Her final request had been to move her bed beside his wall, where she used her remaining strength to tap to him.

Since then, the signal has returned as a supernatural visitation. Dampier believes its third appearance fulfills an occult prophecy and foretells his death. After his friend departs, Dampier dies alone that same night, consumed by sorrow and remorse.
