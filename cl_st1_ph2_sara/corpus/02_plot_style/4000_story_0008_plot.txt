Stranded alone in the French Congo after his porters flee with his toiletries, trader Lloyd Merriwether begins canoeing down the Ogowe River toward the coast. Fever, exhaustion, isolation, and an obsessive longing for civilization steadily erode his sanity. After becoming lost in a jungle tributary, he discovers an abandoned European-style house and enters it for shelter.

Inside, Merriwether becomes convinced that an invisible enemy is stalking and mocking him. He pursues the imagined intruder through the rooms until he finally confronts a gaunt, scarred, armed stranger and shoots. The “enemy” is his own unfamiliar, unshaven reflection in a mirror. Delirious and driven mad by fever and isolation, he is later found by a missionary doctor, whom he attacks before being rescued and hospitalized.
