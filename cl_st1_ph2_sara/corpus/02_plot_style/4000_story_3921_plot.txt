Eighteen-year-old Barbara Lowe, a spoiled and flirtatious city girl visiting relatives in the northern woods, dresses elegantly for a walk but strays from the trail and becomes lost. After ruining her clothes crossing rough terrain and a swamp, she collapses in terror.

A capable young woodsman named Garrett Stanton finds her. Since night is approaching, he persuades her to camp rather than attempt the journey in darkness. He builds fires and a shelter, dries her shoes, cooks her supper, and firmly counters her imperious behavior. As they talk, Barbara becomes fascinated by his self-possession, kindness, and mysterious mission, while Stanton develops a guarded tenderness toward her.

The next morning, Stanton leads Barbara out of the forest, revealing that they had camped almost within sight of her relatives’ cabin. He admits he deliberately prolonged the situation to give her an adventure and asks forgiveness. Barbara, already imagining a romance between them, asks when he will visit her. Stanton says he must leave immediately for Painted Rock; reaching it on time is his one chance to complete a mission for which he has struggled for years.

Barbara asks him to stay, but he refuses. Wounded, she regains her pride and dismisses him, though she quietly admits that she has much to forgive. Stanton kisses her fingertips and disappears into the wilderness, leaving Barbara motionless and heartbroken.
