Marie, determined to give her children the loving childhood she never had, takes them to buy a puppy from Callie’s impoverished family. At the filthy house, Marie sees Callie’s troubled young son, Bo, tethered to a tree for his own safety. Interpreting this as cruel abuse, she refuses the puppy and plans to report Callie to Child Welfare.

Callie, meanwhile, believes the tether allows Bo—who is prone to dangerous wandering—to play safely outside without medication. After Marie leaves, Callie abandons the unwanted puppy in a cornfield so her husband will not have to kill it. She returns home reassuring herself that, despite her mistakes, restraining Bo was an act of love because it gave him a happier, safer day.
