Detective Philo Gubb uses reward money from a previous case to reduce the large debt keeping him from marrying Syrilla, who remains trapped as an overweight sideshow performer. Meanwhile, Riverbank residents report bizarre “un-burglaries”: someone enters their homes, leaves engraved silver spoons, and escapes through cellar windows. Town joker Billy Getz and Marshal Wittaker encourage Gubb to investigate, expecting his amateur methods to make him look foolish.

Gubb initially suspects Billy, but stains from a flower bed lead him to wealthy, miserly John Westcote. Believing Westcote may be secretly making amends for wedding gifts he never gave, Gubb follows him. Westcote drops a package near a lumberyard, where hidden men retrieve it. Gubb trails them to a railway boxcar and overhears that they are dynamiters who have extorted one thousand dollars from Westcote and plan further violence. He locks them inside the car and accompanies the train until authorities can arrest them; their confession implicates three accomplices.

Back in Riverbank, the police catch the actual un-burglar, a harmless half-wit who dreamed that giving silverware to prominent families would earn him invitations to their parties. Thus, while the others expected Gubb to fail comically, he inadvertently solves the more serious dynamiting case and earns a share of a five-thousand-dollar reward.
