Margaret Lee, a gentle, unmarried woman, loses her ancestral home and fortune through the reckless schemes of her stepsister Camille’s husband, Jack Desmond. Reduced to poverty, Margaret is forced by Jack and Camille to support them by exhibiting her obesity in a traveling circus, where she endures public ridicule and profound humiliation.

In the village of Greenhill, Sydney Lord, a similarly obese but respected gentleman, recognizes both her suffering and her refinement. Unable to buy her freedom, he secretly takes her place in the show, disguising himself and submitting to the same degrading exhibition so that Margaret can remain peacefully with his sister. Unaware of the full extent of his sacrifice, Margaret regains a dignified home life, while she and Sydney form a deep, spiritual bond founded on compassion, gratitude, and mutual recognition of the person beyond the body.
