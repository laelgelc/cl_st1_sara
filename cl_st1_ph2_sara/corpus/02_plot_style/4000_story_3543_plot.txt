During a bear hunt, the narrator and an experienced young tracker, Damian, follow a wounded bear through deep snow and successfully determine where it has settled. The next morning, they return with hunters and beaters to surround and drive it out.

The bear appears, but the narrator and his companion miss their first shots. Terrified and wounded, it charges directly at the narrator. He fires twice at close range, breaking its jaw but failing to stop it. The bear knocks him down and begins biting his face. Damian rushes forward armed only with a stick, shouting until the animal releases the narrator and flees.

A doctor stitches the narrator’s severe facial wounds, which eventually heal with little scarring. A month later, the hunters pursue the bear again. Damian kills it, and the narrator has its enormous black-furred body stuffed and displayed in his room.
