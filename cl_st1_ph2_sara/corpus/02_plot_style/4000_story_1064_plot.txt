Inspector Wield recounts three detective cases. In the first, gloves found beneath the pillow of murdered Eliza Grimwood appear to implicate a young upholsterer, Mr. Trinkle. After tracing the gloves through London’s cleaners, Wield arrests him, but learns that Grimwood had innocently taken the discarded gloves from her cousin to use for cleaning, so they have no connection to the murder.

In the second, Sergeant Witchem and Wield arrest four professional thieves after one steals a valuable diamond pin at Epsom. During the struggle, Witchem subtly taps the thief’s hand as an accomplice would, tricking him into passing over the stolen pin. The thief later escapes from court but is recaptured hiding in a tree and convicted through Witchem’s evidence.

In the third, Sergeant Dornton investigates repeated thefts from students’ coats at a hospital. Rejecting suspicion of a porter, he hides beneath a sofa and watches a student search the coats and steal marked money from a planted pocketbook. Caught in the act, the student pleads guilty but poisons himself in prison before sentencing.
