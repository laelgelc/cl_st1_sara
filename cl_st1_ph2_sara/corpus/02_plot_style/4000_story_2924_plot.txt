On April Fools’ Day, fourteen-year-old Julius Barrett, frustrated by his failure to trick anyone, invents a rumor that two unlikely middle-aged neighbors—confirmed bachelor Thomas Everett and unmarried Adelia Williams—are engaged. He tells the story to scrupulously honest Dan Chester, who believes it and spreads it throughout Valley View.

The rumor quickly becomes accepted as fact, acquiring elaborate details about the wedding and Adelia’s preparations. Thomas repeatedly denies it, but the community’s teasing only intensifies. The gossip makes him notice Adelia for the first time and consider her admirable qualities, especially as he struggles to manage his untidy household without a housekeeper. Adelia is similarly harassed and begins thinking about Thomas.

When Thomas receives a wedding present from a distant cousin, he decides the situation has gone too far and resolves to marry—preferably Adelia. He visits her, and their shared laughter over the absurd rumor dispels their embarrassment. After confirming that neither started the gossip and that Adelia is not engaged to anyone else, Thomas admits that the story made him think about her until he genuinely wished it were true. He proposes, and Adelia, who has developed similar feelings, effectively accepts, turning Julius’s April Fools’ lie into reality.
