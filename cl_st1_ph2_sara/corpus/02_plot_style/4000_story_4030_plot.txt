Sara, a poor immigrant factory worker, falls deeply in love with David, a medical student who helps her adapt to American life and promises to marry her after graduation. But David’s wealthy uncle, appalled by Sara’s poverty and dependent family, threatens to withhold the money needed to establish David’s medical practice. Torn between love and ambition, David abruptly abandons Sara and opens an office uptown.

Sara is devastated. When David later returns, remorseful and begging forgiveness, she is too emotionally broken to respond, and her father drives him away. She eventually marries Sam and has children, but remains haunted by the love she lost.

Years later, Sara unexpectedly encounters David and his wife at a wedding. All her rehearsed plans to show that she has survived collapse at the sight of him, and she is overwhelmed by memories of their happiness, his betrayal, and the lasting emptiness it created in her life.
