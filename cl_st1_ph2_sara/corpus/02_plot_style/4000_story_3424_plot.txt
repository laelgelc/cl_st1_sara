An adventurous English traveler near Munich ignores his frightened coachman’s warnings about Walpurgis Night and sets out alone toward an abandoned village reputed to be haunted by the undead. A violent snowstorm drives him into an ancient cemetery, where he finds the marble tomb of Countess Dolingen. When he opens it, he sees the beautiful, lifelike woman inside, but lightning strikes the tomb’s iron stake, destroying the sepulcher and burning the figure within.

The traveler loses consciousness and awakens beneath a gigantic wolf that lies on his chest, warming him and licking his throat without piercing it. Soldiers, alerted by the wolf’s yelping, arrive and rescue him, though they are terrified by the creature and what they discover in the cemetery. Back at his hotel, he learns that the search was organized after his runaway coachman returned and, more mysteriously, after Count Dracula sent an urgent telegram ordering that every effort be made to protect his English guest. The traveler realizes that he has been caught between sinister supernatural forces and an equally mysterious protection.
