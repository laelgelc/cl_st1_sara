A stubborn little girl named Kitty receives new shoes that become magical on midsummer day. Whenever she refuses an errand, disobeys, or acts unkindly, the shoes force her to do what is right, whisking her to the kitchen, post office, school, garden, and other destinations. They also prevent her from stealing cake, dawdling, and kicking down another child’s toy. When Kitty willingly helps others, however, the shoes reward her with extraordinary leaps and graceful dancing.

Kitty eventually chooses to run charitable errands pleasantly, delivering clothes, books, food, and money to families in need. Afterward, she visits a grove called Fairyland, where the shoes allow her to understand the speech of animals and insects. At sunset their magic ends, but Kitty remembers their lessons. She becomes more obedient and helpful, learns to dance beautifully, and develops a lasting affection for the creatures of the woods and fields.
