The British sailing ship Lochleven, carrying 4,000 tons of coal to San Francisco, becomes becalmed south of the equator. Captain Stafford detects gas rising from the hold and discovers that the coal has begun to combust spontaneously. The crew digs trenches, pumps in water, seals the hatches, removes flammable materials, and prepares the lifeboats, but smoke and poisonous fumes reveal that the hidden fire is spreading.

A series of squalls carries the ship closer to the Galapagos Islands, so the captain delays abandoning it as long as possible. When smoke, sparks, and suffocating gas finally make remaining aboard too dangerous, the passengers and crew escape in three boats. Soon afterward, the decks burst open and the entire ship becomes a vast blaze. The survivors reach the Galapagos three days later, but seven-year-old Howard is heartbroken because his treasured one-armed toy sailor, Lord Nelson, was left aboard the burning vessel.
