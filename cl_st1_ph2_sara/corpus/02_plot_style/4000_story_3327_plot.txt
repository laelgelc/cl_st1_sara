A weary traveler seeks lodging at a remote farmhouse whose inhabitants initially greet him with suspicion and hostility. A kind young woman quietly softens the savage dog, her gruff father, and her reluctant mother through gentle words and affectionate touch, securing the traveler food and shelter.

Throughout the evening, he observes her calming a rebellious child and soothing her father during a heated political argument. The next morning, when the family’s stubborn pony refuses to move despite whipping and rough handling, she approaches it tenderly, and it immediately becomes cooperative.

As the traveler departs, the father explains that everyone and everything loves her because she is good. The traveler concludes that her remarkable influence comes from genuine kindness, reflecting on the often underestimated power of gentleness and love.
