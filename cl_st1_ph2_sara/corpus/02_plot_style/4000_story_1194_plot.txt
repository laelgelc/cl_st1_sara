After Angeline Laurence dies, she leaves her house and possessions to Ada Lambert, the daughter of Edward, the man Angeline had loved for thirty years after their engagement ended over a petty quarrel. Moved by Angeline’s lifelong devotion, Ada preserves her belongings and eventually moves into the house after her grandparents die.

Living alone, Ada becomes acquainted with her reclusive neighbor, Sylvester Noble. He offers to protect her, helps her through the winter, and gradually earns her admiration and affection. Although villagers call him “love-cracked,” Ada believes he is good and sympathizes with the romantic sorrow she assumes lies behind his solitude.

Ada’s friend Ellen then reports that Sylvester cheated his brother of an inheritance and stole his brother’s fiancée. Believing the rumor, Ada coldly rejects Sylvester’s concern just before he leaves for his annual stay in the nearby Green Hills. Weeks later, Ellen admits the story was reversed: Sylvester’s brother stole Sylvester’s beloved, and Sylvester generously surrendered his inheritance to provide for them.

Ashamed and anxious when Sylvester fails to return, Ada watches his empty house and waits in vain. After many weeks, she finally walks alone to the Green Hills to search for him, following directions toward the isolated cabin where he studies nature and kills snakes.
