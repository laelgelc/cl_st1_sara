On a warm Sunday evening in Dublin, two unemployed drifters, Corley and Lenehan, discuss Corley’s relationship with a servant girl whom he has been exploiting for gifts and money. Corley plans to persuade her to steal from her employer, while the admiring yet anxious Lenehan waits to see whether the scheme succeeds.

After observing Corley meet the girl, Lenehan wanders the city alone. Hungry and nearly penniless, he eats a cheap meal and reflects bitterly on his poverty, empty friendships, and desire for a stable home and wife. He later returns to the appointed meeting place and nervously follows Corley and the girl to her employer’s house.

The girl enters the house, returns briefly to Corley, and then disappears inside. When Lenehan asks whether the plan worked, Corley silently opens his hand to reveal a small gold coin she has obtained for him.
