Confederate soldier Edmond carries a cherished locket given to him by his beloved Octavie. On the eve of a battle, the locket is secretly stolen by a messmate. After the fighting, a priest finds the thief dead with the locket around his neck and returns it to Octavie, leading her to believe Edmond has been killed.

Octavie mourns deeply and resigns herself to a lonely life. The following spring, Edmond’s father, Judge Pillier, takes her to his home, where he surprises her with the miraculous truth: Edmond is alive. Reunited with him, Octavie learns that he lost the locket during the hurried battle and retreat. They realize it must have been stolen by the silent messmate whose body was mistaken for Edmond’s.
