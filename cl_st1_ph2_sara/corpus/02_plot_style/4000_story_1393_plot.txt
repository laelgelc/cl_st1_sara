When young Peggy falls ill with a fever and pain in her side, Mrs. Workington Bancroft fears appendicitis and sends the governess, Miss Craig, across the moors to fetch a doctor staying at a distant farm. Despite bad weather, frightening local legends, and the desertion of the family dog, Miss Craig reaches the farm, only to learn that the doctor has already returned to Liverpool.

On the lonely journey home, she meets a clergyman who offers to escort her. To ease her fear of the moors, he tells her about a walk he took eleven years earlier, when a threatening stranger joined him at the same spot, demanded money, and murdered him with an ash stake just before nine o’clock. The clergyman calmly describes his painless death, then suddenly disappears. Miss Craig runs home in terror, arriving as the clock strikes nine.
