For years, a meek German woman works tirelessly beside Tony, a brutal Italian shopkeeper who abuses her while allowing the neighborhood to assume they are married. She finds brief relief only when his gout or illness leaves him unable to beat her.

When Tony becomes terminally ill, she rejoices at the prospect of freedom but grows fearful after his brother John arrives. She begs Tony to summon a priest and formally marry her before he dies, revealing that their supposed marriage was never legal. Tony spitefully refuses, accusing her of wanting his money and declaring that John will inherit everything. Even at the moment of death, he points to his brother rather than acknowledge her as his wife.

After Tony’s funeral, John claims the shop and finds Tony’s hidden money. The woman who served Tony faithfully for years is expelled penniless, carrying only a bundle of clothes while her worn hands seem to long for the knitting that sustained her through her miserable life.
