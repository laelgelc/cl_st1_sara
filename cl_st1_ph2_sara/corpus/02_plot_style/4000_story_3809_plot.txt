A music lover escapes the city’s harsh noise by attending a performance of Beethoven’s Fifth Symphony from his favorite balcony seat. At first, he views the orchestra as a perfectly controlled machine under the conductor’s command. As the symphony progresses, however, he recognizes that its power comes not from mechanical precision but from the cooperation, emotions, and lived experiences of the individual musicians.

Watching their distinct faces and personalities, he imagines the joys, sorrows, conflicts, and relationships hidden beneath their unified performance and wishes that a musician might tell one of their stories. After the triumphant finale leaves him absorbed and motionless, two friends approach and invite him to join them by the fire, where one has a story to read aloud.
