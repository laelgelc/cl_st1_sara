A solitary but cheerful miller avoids friendship and sings that he cares for nobody because nobody cares for him. One evening, he rescues a box floating down the river and discovers a baby girl inside. He adopts her, names her Nathalie, and raises her tenderly as his daughter. When she grows old enough to understand his song, she tearfully reminds him that she loves him, prompting him to change his tune.

Years later, Nathalie becomes a beautiful young woman and falls in love with a local Squire. Fearing loneliness, the miller initially opposes their courtship, but he finally consents when he realizes how deeply they love each other. After their marriage, he returns to his old song, believing himself abandoned. Nathalie assures him that her new love has not diminished her devotion to him, and she visits the mill every day. Comforted, the miller learns a happier song and remains content for many years.
