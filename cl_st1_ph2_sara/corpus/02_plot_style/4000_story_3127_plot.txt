Twenty-six children living in the restrictive Santa Maria flats fear that Santa Claus cannot reach them because strangers are barred from the entrances and the narrow gas fireplaces cannot accommodate him. After inspecting every fireplace and consulting the knowledgeable Telephone Boy, they conclude that Christmas will pass without gifts and grow increasingly despondent.

On Christmas Eve, a violent windstorm tears the skylights from the building. The next morning, the children awaken to stuffed stockings, decorated trees, and boxes of presents, realizing that Santa Claus entered through the open skylights. Even the impoverished Telephone Boy receives gifts in the basement beneath a skylight shaft. The holiday ends happily, with the usually gloomy janitor finally smiling after receiving a handmade necktie from Kara Johnson.
