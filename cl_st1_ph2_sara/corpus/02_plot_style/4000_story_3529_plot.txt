A prosperous peasant, Iván, falls into a bitter feud with his neighbor Gabriel after their families quarrel over a missing egg. The trivial dispute escalates over six years into insults, fights, theft accusations, lawsuits, and punishments, despite repeated pleas from Iván’s elderly father to forgive and reconcile.

After Gabriel is sentenced to flogging for striking Iván’s pregnant daughter-in-law, he threatens revenge. Iván briefly considers making peace but instead remains suspicious and watches his property at night. He catches Gabriel setting fire to his thatch, yet pursues him rather than immediately extinguishing the small flame. The fire spreads, destroying both families’ homes and much of the village.

As Iván’s father lies dying from injuries sustained during the fire, he makes Iván recognize that his own pride and vengefulness helped cause the disaster. He instructs Iván not to expose Gabriel’s guilt but to conceal his sin and obey the law of forgiveness. Iván complies, his anger fades, and Gabriel eventually abandons the feud. The families rebuild beside each other, become good neighbors again, and Iván thereafter resolves to stop quarrels at their first spark by answering injury with reconciliation rather than revenge.
