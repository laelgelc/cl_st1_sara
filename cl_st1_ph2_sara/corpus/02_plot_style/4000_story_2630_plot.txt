Earth’s gods, driven from one mountain refuge after another by human explorers, now dwell on inaccessible Kadath under the protection of terrifying higher beings. Barzai the Wise, confident that his occult learning makes him immune to divine wrath, sets out with his disciple Atal to witness the gods dancing atop Hatheg-Kla.

After a perilous ascent, they wait until clouds conceal the summit during a full moon. Barzai climbs ahead, exulting that he can hear and soon see the gods. But an unnatural eclipse falls, the mountain seems to rise into the sky, and Barzai realizes too late that the frightened earth gods are guarded by monstrous gods from the outer abysses. He cries out in horror that he is falling into the sky and vanishes amid supernatural thunder.

Atal is mysteriously returned unharmed to the lower slopes. A later search finds no trace of Barzai, only an enormous, ancient symbol carved into the summit. Thereafter, the surrounding peoples fear eclipses and cloud-covered peaks, while the earth gods continue visiting Hatheg-Kla, secure from human intrusion.
