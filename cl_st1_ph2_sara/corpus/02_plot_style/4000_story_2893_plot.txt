Bannadonna, a brilliant and prideful mechanician, is commissioned to build a magnificent bell-and-clock tower. During the casting of its enormous state bell, he kills a frightened workman whose blood and a splinter from the fatal blow fall into the molten metal. Bannadonna secretly conceals a resulting defect in the bell.

He then constructs a fearsome mechanical figure designed to move through the belfry and strike a rotating clock bell at each hour. While making final adjustments before its public unveiling, Bannadonna becomes absorbed in sculpting the figure representing the first hour and forgets that the automaton is set to activate at one o’clock. It advances as programmed and, finding its creator’s head between its hammer and the bell, kills him.

The automaton is secretly destroyed at sea. At Bannadonna’s funeral, the immense state bell breaks loose because of its concealed flaw and crashes from the tower. The tower is repaired, but a year later an earthquake topples it entirely. Bannadonna’s creation, unchecked ambition, and concealed wrongdoing thus bring about his death and the destruction of his masterpiece.
