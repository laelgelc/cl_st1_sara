Newly dressed in long trousers, Abner decides to make his first formal social call on Champe Claiborne, guided by a book of etiquette. He recruits his friend Ross, and the nervous boys rehearse proper conversation, dress carefully, and prepare calling cards. Once at the Claiborne home, however, they are too intimidated and confused to announce their purpose. The family assumes they have simply come to visit, feeds them supper, includes them in the evening’s activities, and even puts them to bed for the night.

The next morning, the household openly delights in the boys’ embarrassment. Before they can finally declare that they came to call on Champe, a messenger arrives with a humiliating threat from Abner’s mother, sending Abner fleeing home. Ross starts after him, but Champe’s mocking laughter makes him turn back. In a burst of courage, he kisses her in front of the entire household and then leaves triumphantly, dodging the stones she playfully throws after him.
