Seventeen-year-old Maria Gavrilovna falls in love with Vladimir, a poor army officer whom her wealthy parents reject. The lovers plan to elope and marry secretly at a nearby village church. During a violent snowstorm, Maria reaches the church, but Vladimir loses his way and arrives only after dawn.

Maria returns home and becomes gravely ill, while Vladimir, devastated by what happened, rejects her parents’ belated consent to the marriage and rejoins the army. He is wounded at Borodino and later dies. Maria’s father also dies, leaving her a wealthy heiress, but she remains devoted to Vladimir’s memory and refuses every suitor.

Years later, Maria falls in love with Bourmin, a decorated hussar colonel. Although he returns her feelings, he confesses that he cannot marry her because, four years earlier, a snowstorm led him to an unfamiliar church where people mistook him for an expected bridegroom. On a reckless impulse, he went through the wedding ceremony with the waiting bride, who recognized the mistake only after they were married. Bourmin fled and never learned her identity. Maria then realizes that she was that bride, meaning Bourmin is already her lawful husband.
