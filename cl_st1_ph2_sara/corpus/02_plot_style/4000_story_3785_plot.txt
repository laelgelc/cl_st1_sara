Dudley Hemenway, returning from his seventh unsuccessful moose hunt, waits for a delayed train and tells engineer Angus McLeod about repeatedly trying to kill a magnificent bull moose called Silverhorns. During the hunt, bad positioning, darkness, noise, and sheer bad luck allow the animal to escape every encounter. Hemenway comes to respect Silverhorns for having “played a good game” and admits he is not sorry the moose survived.

While Hemenway rides in the locomotive cab, a large bull moose appears on the tracks. Defying the approaching train, it charges the headlight and is killed in the collision. The shattered animal proves to be Silverhorns, though its great antlers remain intact. McLeod observes that Hemenway has finally killed his first moose, but Hemenway shares the credit with him and, awed by the animal’s courage, declares it will likely be their last.
