Two newly released prisoners, a man and woman without friends or family, meet outside prison and begin walking west together. Overwhelmed by London’s crowds and fog, they wander into Kensington Gardens, become locked inside overnight, and panic at its seemingly limitless size before being freed the next morning.

Pretending to be husband and wife, they continue west in search of food and farm work. Near Reading, an elderly astronomer invites them into his home after the man falsely claims the woman is pregnant. After eating, the man confesses the lie and asks to see the stars. The astronomer describes the immense distances and speeds of celestial bodies, but the vastness of the universe terrifies the pair. Feeling safer within confinement than in an incomprehensibly large world, they eventually return voluntarily to prison.
