Identical twins John and Henry Stevens are so alike that even their family cannot reliably distinguish them. After moving to California and losing their parents, they work separately in San Francisco. One day, Henry is mistaken for John by Mr. Margovan and inexplicably knows the stranger’s name; meanwhile, John has felt an equally inexplicable impulse to obtain Margovan’s address. John accepts Margovan’s dinner invitation, becomes a frequent guest, and becomes engaged to his daughter Julia.

Before meeting Julia, Henry is mysteriously compelled to follow a dissolute-looking man to a secret rendezvous with a beautiful woman. He later recognizes the woman as Julia. When he confronts her, she admits her predicament without explaining it and assumes he intends to blackmail her. Henry instead offers help but warns that he will oppose her marriage to John.

The following night, Henry is overwhelmed by a premonition and twice hears John cry out in mortal agony, though his brother is nowhere nearby. Acting almost unconsciously, he rushes to the Margovan home, where Julia has died by poison and John has shot himself. John dies as Henry reaches him, and Henry himself collapses and remains ill for weeks.

Years later, Henry encounters Julia’s former lover in Union Square. The ruined man mistakes him for the dead John, reacts with terror and rage, strikes feebly at him, and falls dead. His identity and the exact circumstances behind Julia’s death and John’s suicide remain unknown.
