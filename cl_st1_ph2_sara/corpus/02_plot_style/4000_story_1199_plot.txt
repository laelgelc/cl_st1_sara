Elderly sisters Melissa Abbot and Sarah Drew live in adjoining houses and share a deep, lifelong affection. Their harmony is shattered when Mira Holmes gives them two nearly identical yellow kittens. Each sister names one—Billy and Susy—but after the kittens are set down, both claim the same one. Too proud and stubborn to yield, they quarrel and stop speaking.

The estrangement makes both women miserable and harms their health. Friends, neighbors, Mira, her mother, and even the minister try unsuccessfully to reconcile them. Eventually, the sisters leave town separately to visit relatives, while their servants care for the cats and quietly recognize the truth that their mistresses refused to hear.

Just before Thanksgiving, both sisters return. Melissa approaches Sarah and admits that the cat she believed was Billy must be Susy because it has given birth to kittens. Sarah reveals that her own supposed Billy has also had kittens: both cats were female, so the entire quarrel was pointless. Each sister had already resolved to apologize and let the other keep the disputed cat. They laugh at their foolishness, forgive each other, and resume their close relationship.

They celebrate Thanksgiving together and invite Mira, who has reconciled with Harry Ayres and is going to marry him. The sisters offer the young couple two yellow kittens for their future home, and the gathering ends in shared happiness, affection, and gratitude.
