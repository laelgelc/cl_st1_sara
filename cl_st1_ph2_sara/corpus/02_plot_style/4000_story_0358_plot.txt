Five childless women find an enormous egg and take turns incubating it. An ugly, insatiably hungry boy named Grumblegizzard hatches from it, but his appetite soon forces them to send him away. Possessing extraordinary strength, he works for a farmer and then a king, completing enormous tasks but causing destruction and consuming vast quantities of food.

Hoping to be rid of him, the king repeatedly sends him on deadly missions. Grumblegizzard single-handedly defeats an invading army, forces the Devil’s mother to pay a supposed land tax, escapes the pursuing Devil, kills a seven-headed troll, rescues an enchanted man, and recovers the king’s ancestral sword. He then survives a haunted castle, outwits and traps the Devil, and compels him to build a bridge in exchange for the first soul to cross it. When the bridge is finished, Grumblegizzard tricks the Devil into refusing the promised toll.

The king still tries to withhold Grumblegizzard’s promised reward, including his daughter’s hand. In response, Grumblegizzard takes his wages by hurling the dishonest king into the sky, along with a sack of food, leaving him suspended between heaven and earth.
