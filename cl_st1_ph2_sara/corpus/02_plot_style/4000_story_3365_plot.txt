Gerasim, a strong young peasant, returns to Moscow before Christmas and spends weeks unsuccessfully searching for work. Desperate and sometimes hungry, he asks Yegor, a coachman from his village, for help. Yegor persuades his wealthy employer, Sharov, to hire Gerasim as a general servant by dismissing Polikarpych, an elderly longtime worker.

Overjoyed at first, Gerasim overhears Polikarpych and his wife grieving over their impending destitution and blaming Yegor for replacing him with a younger friend. Realizing that his good fortune would ruin the elderly couple, Gerasim refuses the position despite Yegor’s anger. He leaves to continue his search for work, feeling happy and morally relieved.
