At Miss Wayland’s school, a fierce March blizzard delights Maine, a student accustomed to harsh northern winters, while frightening many of her classmates. During the storm, the girls hear a child crying outside. Equipped with snowshoes and guided by a length of twine tied to the school door, Maine ventures into the blinding gale. She finds Benny Withers nearly buried and freezing beside a stone wall, carries him to his nearby home, and reunites him with his distraught mother. Although the twine breaks and Maine struggles to find her way back, she eventually returns safely to the anxious school.

The next morning, Maine uses her snowshoes and a sled to collect food for the snowbound school. She then lends the snowshoes to the village doctor, enabling him to reach dangerously ill patients and save a life. In gratitude, he makes a generous donation to the school’s missionary fund.
