A traveler lunching at a secluded country inn encounters a strangely noble old rustic whom no one else can see. The man speaks of having returned to summon someone needed elsewhere and invites the traveler to meet him at midnight in the nearby Wood of the Dead. After the figure vanishes, the innkeeper’s daughter explains that he is the ghost of a revered local benefactor who claimed to see those destined to die entering the wood and whose appearance is said to foretell a death.

Compelled by the encounter, the traveler enters the wood at midnight and is guided by the ghost into a vision of a sunlit hayfield. There he sees the innkeeper’s daughter happily flirting with her future husband. When the ghost calls her name, she leaves the young man, enters the wood, and rests against the spirit, addressing him as father and saying she is tired and has come willingly. The vision collapses into darkness, and the traveler returns to the inn.

A year later, he revisits the village and learns that the girl never married: only days after his first visit, she suffered sunstroke in the hayfields and died within a week.
