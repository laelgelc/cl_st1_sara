In the final game of the season, Worcester must defeat Buffalo to secure the pennant. The exhausted, injury-ridden players entrust field leadership to veteran captain Spears, while their overworked star pitcher, the Rube, struggles to last the entire game.

Worcester takes an early lead, but Buffalo deliberately forces the Rube to expend his remaining strength and eventually pulls ahead. Worcester rallies to tie the score, and Spears hits a home run before provoking the umpire into ejecting him because he is too injured to continue playing. With the score tied late, Worcester loads the bases. Buffalo’s pitcher strikes out two of the team’s best hitters, but Stringer drives in all three runners, giving Worcester an 8–5 lead.

In Buffalo’s final inning, the Rube is nearly spent, McCall is injured making a crucial stop, and Buffalo scores twice. With Worcester leading 8–7, two outs, and a runner on first, the final batter drives what appears to be a game-winning home run. Stringer leaps to knock the ball down, then relays it to Ashwell, whose throw reaches the catcher just in time to tag out the tying runner at home. Worcester wins the game and the pennant, fulfilling the team’s season-long struggle and securing the narrator’s hoped-for future.
