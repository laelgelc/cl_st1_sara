A semi-barbaric king administers justice through an arena where accused subjects must choose between two identical doors. Behind one waits a savage tiger that will kill them; behind the other waits a suitable woman whom they must immediately marry.

When the king discovers that his daughter is secretly in love with a handsome but low-ranking courtier, he imprisons the young man and sentences him to this trial. The princess secretly learns which door conceals the tiger and which conceals a beautiful court lady whom she intensely envies and suspects of admiring her lover.

In the arena, the young man looks to the princess for guidance. She signals toward the right-hand door, and, trusting her, he opens it. The story ends without revealing what emerges, leaving unresolved whether the princess chose to save her lover for another woman or send him to his death rather than lose him to her rival.
