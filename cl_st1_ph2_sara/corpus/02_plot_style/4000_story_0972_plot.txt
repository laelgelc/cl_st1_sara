Mrs. Bargrave, a mistreated but steadfast woman, receives an unexpected visit from her estranged childhood friend, Mrs. Veal. During nearly two hours of conversation, Mrs. Veal apologizes for neglecting their friendship, comforts Mrs. Bargrave with religious reflections on death and the afterlife, and asks her to tell Mrs. Veal’s brother to distribute certain rings and gold. She then leaves, saying she is about to undertake a journey.

Two days later, Mrs. Bargrave learns that Mrs. Veal had died the day before the visit, at the exact hour of noon. Details from the encounter—including Mrs. Veal’s distinctive gown, private financial information, and knowledge of hidden possessions—are confirmed by others. Witnesses also report hearing Mrs. Bargrave speaking to someone during the visit. Although Mrs. Veal’s brother tries to discredit and suppress the account, Mrs. Bargrave consistently maintains that her friend’s apparition came to renew their friendship, comfort her in her suffering, and provide evidence of life after death.
