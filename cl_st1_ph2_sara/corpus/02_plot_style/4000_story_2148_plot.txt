On the sandy High Veldt, a pale Leopard and an Ethiopian hunt animals easily because both blend into the landscape. To escape them, the Giraffe, Zebra, and other prey move into a shadowy forest, where over time they develop blotches, stripes, and darker markings that camouflage them.

Unable to find game, the starving hunters consult the wise baboon Baviaan, who cryptically advises them to change and to “go into other spots.” They follow the animals into the forest but discover that their own coloring makes them conspicuous while their prey nearly disappears among the patterned shadows.

Realizing they must match their new surroundings, the Ethiopian changes his skin to a dark color. He then interprets Baviaan’s advice literally for the Leopard, pressing his black fingertips across the animal’s coat to create clusters of spots. With their new camouflage, the two resume hunting in the forest and remain content with their changed appearances.
