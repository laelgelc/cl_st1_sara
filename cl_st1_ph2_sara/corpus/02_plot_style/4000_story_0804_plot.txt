Father Brown hears a gunshot and a series of strange, sneeze-like sounds outside Major Putnam’s house. Inside, silverware and a cruet-stand appear to have been stolen, while Colonel Cray claims he fired at an intruder. Cray believes he is being persecuted by an Indian secret society after entering a forbidden temple, and recounts several apparently supernatural attempts on his life. Putnam dismisses these incidents as delusions, jeopardizing Cray’s engagement to Putnam’s ward, Audrey Watson.

Father Brown discovers the supposedly stolen silver and cruet-stand hidden in the dustbin. During a special lunch prepared for Cray, he brings the cruet condiments to the table and notices Dr Oman watching anxiously from outside. When Cray suddenly shows signs of poisoning, Brown gives him mustard water as an emetic and saves his life.

Brown explains that Cray’s earlier “supernatural” attacks were staged by Putnam using a razor, gas, and a boomerang. Putnam, who loved Audrey and wanted Cray discredited or dead, had poisoned Cray’s meal, then hidden the cruet because its contents could provide an antidote. Cray’s bullet had struck the pepper-pot Putnam was carrying, scattering pepper and making him sneeze. Exposed, Putnam pretends to leave for the police but instead flees, while Audrey and Cray are reconciled.
