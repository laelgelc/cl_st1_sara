Dot Burton, newly arrived from England to help her Australian cattle-farmer brother Jack, declares she would shoot the notorious cattle thief Buckskin Bill if she encountered him. While Jack and Inspector Fletcher Hill’s men hunt the outlaw, Dot is bitten by a venomous snake at the homestead. A stranger appears, sucks the poison from her arm, treats her, and accepts a meal. Assuming he is Inspector Hill, Dot confesses her dislike of hunting criminals and agrees to treat him without prejudice if Buckskin Bill escapes. The stranger kisses her wounded arm and leaves.

When Jack returns with the real Inspector Hill, Dot realizes that her rescuer was Buckskin Bill in disguise and refuses to betray him. Jack initially suspects that the outlaw merely charmed her, but after learning that Bill saved her life—and seeing a lamb killed by the same snake—he understands her gratitude. He conceals the encounter from Hill and expresses relief that Bill escaped, while Hill tacitly accepts that the outlaw has outwitted them.
