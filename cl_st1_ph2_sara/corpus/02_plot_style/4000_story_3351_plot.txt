Herbert Scofield follows Minnie Madison to a Hudson summer resort, hoping to study her character before deciding whether to pursue marriage. Her reserve, along with the attentions of two rival suitors, frustrates him, while his supposedly cautious interest deepens into love.

During a mountain excursion, Minnie compassionately tends a child with a broken leg, and Herbert energetically fetches a doctor. The incident reveals her tender nature and prompts him to abandon caution and court her openly, but she continues to appear elusive. Convinced she does not care for him, he finally resolves to withdraw and leave the resort.

While rowing alone, Herbert discovers Minnie stranded on a small island by the outgoing tide. He wades through the mud and carries her to his boat, all the while bitterly assuming that she merely tolerates his presence. When one of her suitors approaches, however, Minnie hints that if Herbert sits beside her, the rival may understand that she has chosen him. Realizing that his love is returned, Herbert eagerly joins her, and the other man rows away.
