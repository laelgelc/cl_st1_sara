On baking day, anxious Mrs. Tabitha Twitchit tries to confine her mischievous kittens, but Tom hides in the chimney while Moppet and Mittens escape from a cupboard. As Tabitha and her neighbor Ribby search the old, rat-infested house, Moppet reports that a female rat has stolen dough, and Mittens says a huge male rat has taken butter and a rolling pin.

Lost inside the chimney passages, Tom falls into a hidden room occupied by Samuel Whiskers and his wife, Anna Maria. The rats bind him, coat him with butter and dough, and attempt to make him into a roly-poly pudding. Hearing the rolling pin beneath the attic floor, Tabitha and Ribby summon John Joiner, whose sawing and dog-like scratching frighten the rats away. Tom is rescued, cleaned in a hot bath, and the dough is removed and baked separately.

Samuel and Anna Maria relocate to Farmer Potatoes’s barn, where their rapidly multiplying descendants become a terrible infestation. Moppet and Mittens grow into successful rat-catchers, but the traumatized Tom remains afraid of every rat and will confront nothing larger than a mouse.
