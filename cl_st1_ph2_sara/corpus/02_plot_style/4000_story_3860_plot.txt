A father takes his well-behaved young son, Gip, into a mysterious shop that claims to sell genuine magic. Inside, an uncanny shopman performs increasingly impossible feats, produces gifts from nowhere, and leads them through a bewildering showroom filled with enchanted toys and disturbing figures. While Gip is delighted, his father grows alarmed, especially when the shopman makes the boy vanish during a game.

The father pursues the shopman through a door but abruptly finds himself back on Regent Street, with Gip safe beside him and the shop itself gone. Gip carries four parcels, which prove to contain three boxes of fine toy soldiers and a healthy white kitten. Months later, everything appears ordinary, though Gip insists that his soldiers come alive when he says a secret word. His father never witnesses this and remains uncertain whether the magic was real, while the vanished shop never sends him a bill.
