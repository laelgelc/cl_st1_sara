Three San Francisco adventurers—Hardenberg, Strokher, and Ally Bazan—accept a scheme from a shady financier to steal valuable sea-otter pelts from Russian trappers near Point Barrow. They sail north with Nickerson, the company’s agent, a former Methodist preacher who lost his memory and became a rough sailor.

At the Russian post, the men pose as rescuers searching for shipwreck survivors. They ply the trappers with counterfeit champagne until they pass out, then discover and steal a cache of costly pelts. During their escape, Nickerson falls from the rigging and strikes his head. As he recovers, his original identity and moral character return, and he is horrified to learn that his companions committed robbery.

Short-handed, the schooner stops at Juneau, where Nickerson immediately reports the crime to the authorities. Seeing a revenue cutter nearby and believing arrest imminent, the thieves burn all the pelts to destroy the evidence. They then learn that Nickerson was dismissed as a madman after causing a disturbance at the American consul’s house, and the authorities merely want the crew to retrieve him. Realizing they destroyed their fortune for nothing, the defeated adventurers decide to return home and confront the financier who sent them.
