Big George and his snow-blind companion, Charlie Captain, reach an isolated Alaskan mission pursued by the influence of Father Orloff, a corrupt missionary who has hated George for years. Barricaded inside the mission house, George explains that Orloff once tried to take Metla, the Native woman who rescued and later married George, and was responsible for the death of their infant son. Since then, Orloff has repeatedly used his religious authority and Native followers to persecute George.

A blizzard delays Orloff, but he finally arrives while snow-blind. His sled breaks through thin river ice, leaving him and his guide stranded on a frozen hummock. George initially refuses to help and prepares to shoot his longtime enemy, but Captain persuades him not to become a murderer. George organizes a dangerous rescue, saves Orloff, and discovers that the priest is severely frozen.

Rather than abandon him, George drives Orloff two hundred miles in five days to a mission hospital, where the priest survives after an operation but is left permanently crippled. When the doctor assumes George must be Orloff’s friend, George replies that friends are easy to find, but an enemy like Orloff is irreplaceable.
