Mose, a young boy terrified of ghosts, is sent alone on Halloween night to fetch a pumpkin for a party. Traveling past graveyards and a cemetery, he encounters a succession of ghosts, including a headless spirit, groups of specters, and the king of all ghosts. Each apparition insists that “there are no ghosts,” even as their frightening touch turns Mose’s hair white.

Mose returns home with the pumpkin and finds the party guests frightened by a ghost story. He confidently tells them that ghosts do not exist because every ghost he met said so, and the guests accept his logic. Although Mose now claims not to fear ghosts, he remains uneasy about “the ghosts that aren’t” and is reluctant to go to bed alone.
