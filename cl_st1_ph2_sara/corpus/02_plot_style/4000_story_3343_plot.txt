At a hilltop gathering, Caroline Summers remains haunted by the unexplained disappearance of her bridegroom on their wedding day. Osborne Kingsley, who loves her, asks her to abandon the memory and marry him, but she refuses unless the mystery is resolved.

Inside, the guests recount how Gregory Nettleton vanished for four years and unexpectedly returned to his faithful wife, claiming complete amnesia. His only clue to the missing years was an Egyptian ring with a red stone engraved with a “C.” Caroline reveals a matching ring, realizing that her vanished fiancé had been Nettleton during his lost years. Knowing he has returned to his wife and child finally ends her uncertainty; she declares her former love dead to her, leaving open the possibility of a future with Kingsley.
