Miss Trevor, a wealthy visitor, meets eleven-year-old orphan Paul Hubert on the shore at Noel’s Cove and is captivated by his beauty, intelligence, and vivid imagination. Paul lives happily with Stephen Kane, a quiet fisherman who once loved Paul’s mother, and invents “rock people,” including Nora, the Twin Sailors, and the Golden Lady. His stories and handwritten foolscap book convince Miss Trevor that he is unusually gifted.

Believing Paul deserves education, books, travel, and companionship beyond what Stephen can provide, Miss Trevor offers to adopt him. Stephen doubts that wealth will make the sea-loving boy happier but allows Paul to choose. Dazzled by the opportunities she promises, Paul agrees to leave, although he cannot bring himself to say goodbye to his imaginary friends.

During his first night in town, Paul is overwhelmed by homesickness. He misses the sea, Stephen, and his rock people, and realizes that no worldly advantage can replace them. Leaving Miss Trevor a note and his treasured foolscap book, he secretly walks the ten miles back to Noel’s Cove. At dawn he finds Stephen preparing his boat. Stephen quietly admits that he hoped Paul would return, restoring their close companionship, and Paul immediately runs to reunite with Nora.
