Stimson, the fierce and domineering owner of a seaside merry-go-round, discovers that his daughter Lizzie and his employee Frank are conducting a silent courtship through exchanged glances while they work. Outraged by Frank’s audacity, Stimson orders Lizzie to stop encouraging him and threatens to fire Frank if he does not attend strictly to his duties.

Despite the warning, Frank secretly joins Lizzie and her friend Jennie for an evening walk along the beach. Jennie tactfully leaves the couple alone, allowing them to acknowledge their feelings and reconcile the misunderstandings that have marked their hesitant romance.

Later, while Stimson is away, Lizzie and Frank abandon their posts and elope in a buggy. Learning what has happened, Stimson furiously hires a carriage and pursues them. His old horse cannot match the speed of the young couple’s horse, however, and during the chase Stimson dimly recognizes that their youth, love, and hope for the future are forces beyond his control. Forced to give up, he returns defeated, consoling himself with the claim that he is not responsible.
