In 2185, anti-aging medicine has made death rare and forced vast multigenerational families into cramped apartments. The 172-year-old patriarch, Gramps, controls his descendants by constantly revising his will and threatening to disinherit anyone who displeases him.

After Lou catches a relative diluting Gramps’s anti-aging medicine, he tries to replace it with full-strength doses but accidentally smashes the bottle. Gramps assumes Lou meant to hasten his death. The next morning, Gramps disappears, leaving a note and a new will granting his entire estate equally to all his descendants. Their argument over who deserves the apartment’s sole private bedroom erupts into a riot, and the whole family is arrested.

Lou and his wife discover that jail cells provide the privacy and comfort they have always lacked and hope for long sentences. Meanwhile, Gramps returns to the now-empty apartment, revealing that he staged his disappearance and arranged for an excellent prosecutor to keep his family imprisoned. At last alone, he settles comfortably before the television and orders a new medicine that promises to restore his youth.
