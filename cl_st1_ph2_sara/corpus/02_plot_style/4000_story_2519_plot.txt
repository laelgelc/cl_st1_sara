Greedy, distrustful Jacob Kent takes over a travelers’ cabin in the Klondike and charges visitors for shelter while obsessively hiding his hoard of gold dust. When sailor Jim Cardegee arrives bearing the same facial scar as a thief from Kent’s recurring nightmares, Kent becomes convinced he intends to steal the gold. During the night, the sleepwalking Kent unknowingly loads his own gold into his muzzle-loading shotgun.

The next morning, unable to find the dust, Kent blames Cardegee, binds him, and threatens to execute him at noon. Cardegee stalls while loosening his bonds and watching an approaching sled. At the final moment he rolls into a nearby pit. Kent fires, but the gold obstructing the shotgun causes the barrel to burst, killing him. Cardegee and the arriving traveler discover the missing gold pouring from the shattered gun.
