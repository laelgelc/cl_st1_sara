Sam Small, urged by his sister to advise her twenty-one-year-old son Joe against marrying too young, enlists his friends Ginger Dick and Peter Russet. Joe cheerfully agrees with their cynical claims about women and marriage but insists his own fiancée, Miss Gill, is uniquely perfect.

When the three men meet the attractive Miss Gill, Ginger decides to prove Joe foolish by winning her affection. He takes her to the zoo, where she flirts with him, hints at romantic interest, and tearfully reveals that she urgently needs three pounds. Ginger lends her the money, but when he kisses her, she slaps him and has him thrown out.

Unaware of the trick, Peter bets he can also take Miss Gill out. He succeeds, returns three pounds poorer, and likewise conceals that she slapped him. Soon afterward, Joe proudly displays a silver watch, chain, and gold ring that Miss Gill has bought him. Ginger and Peter compare experiences and realize that she extracted three pounds from each of them, rejected both after encouraging them, and used their money to purchase Joe’s gifts.
