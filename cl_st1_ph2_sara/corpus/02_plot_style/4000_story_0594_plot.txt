A destitute young sailor named Willie spends the night near a London park with other homeless people. Weak from illness and exposure, he collapses on a bench after telling his companion, Darkey, grim stories from his life at sea. He is taken to a hospital, where doctors determine that he has little time left.

Facing death, Willie regrets the careless life that estranged him from his family. Longing for his mother, he writes an unfinished letter apologizing for the sorrow he caused and sending love to his siblings. After Willie dies, hospital staff give the unaddressed letter to Darkey, who recalls that Willie’s mother lives in Staffordshire and promises to deliver it. Instead, he gets drunk in a tavern and absentmindedly tears up the letter, burning both halves to light his pipe, so Willie’s final message never reaches his family.
