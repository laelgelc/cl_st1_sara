Hours after his girlfriend Jaclyn undergoes a medication abortion, a young man awakens to apparitions of their unborn twins, hostile Jackie Gunner and affectionate Jamie Lou. They demand to know about their mother, why they were aborted, and what their lives might have been. Burdened by guilt over avoiding Jaclyn during the painful procedure, he takes the rapidly deteriorating twins to a psychic, hoping for answers.

At the psychic’s home, he unexpectedly finds Jaclyn. During their tense session, he confesses that he had previously bribed the psychic to encourage her to proceed with the abortion. Jaclyn angrily insists that the decision was hers and recoils from his attempt to manipulate her. He leaves and discovers that Jamie Lou has apparently killed Jackie after following his advice not to let herself be bullied. When he condemns her, she points out the hypocrisy of his judgment.

As the twins wither, he abandons them on the sidewalk, admitting he did not love them. Jaclyn appears, gathers both into her hand, and revives the barely moving Jackie. The man tries to explain that he cared but feared showing it might change her mind. Jaclyn, disappointed yet exhausted rather than openly hostile, says she will handle the children and may let him help later. They walk in opposite directions, and he returns home to sleep on the bloodstained pillow.
