Aileen Mavourneen, a dog raised by a brave and kindly mother, is taught to risk herself for others without expecting reward. After being sold to the wealthy Gray family, she enjoys an affectionate home, gives birth to a puppy, and becomes especially devoted to the family’s baby.

When the baby’s crib catches fire, Aileen overcomes her fear, remembers her mother’s teaching, and drags the child to safety. Misunderstanding the scene, Mr. Gray beats and injures her, believing she attacked the baby. Once the truth is discovered, the family celebrates Aileen as a hero, and even Mr. Gray praises her intelligence and courage.

Later, while the rest of the family is away, Mr. Gray and his fellow scientists use Aileen’s puppy in an experiment intended to prove that a brain injury causes blindness. The experiment succeeds but kills the puppy. Not understanding death, Aileen believes the puppy has merely been planted in the garden and waits beside its grave for it to grow into a dog. She refuses to leave, gradually stops eating, and becomes too weak to stand. As the family’s return approaches, the servants grieve because they must reveal that both the puppy and the heroic mother who saved the baby have died.
