Larry gives his unhappy wife, Doris, an antique cuckoo clock. Doris becomes convinced that the cuckoo is alive, affectionate toward her, and hostile to Larry. While Larry treats it with suspicion and contempt, the bird pecks him and sometimes refuses to emerge in his presence.

When Larry unexpectedly finds Doris at home with Bob Chambers, he assumes they are having an affair and throws Doris out, though he keeps the clock. After she leaves, the cuckoo grows uncooperative, deepening Larry’s resentment. One night, Larry threatens to smash it with a hammer unless it performs. The cuckoo shoots from its door and strikes him in the eye, causing him to fall from a chair and break his neck.

The doctor declares Larry’s death an accident, but Bob looks toward the clock and suspects that the cuckoo deliberately killed him.
