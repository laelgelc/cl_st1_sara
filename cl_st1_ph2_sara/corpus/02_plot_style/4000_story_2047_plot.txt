A struggling journalist befriends Cyril Harjohn, a backward schoolboy who grows into a brilliant, idealistic young doctor devoted to advancing medicine and serving humanity. Cyril forms a deeply spiritual bond with the saintly Elspeth Grant, yet he is simultaneously overwhelmed by a physical passion for Geraldine Fawley, a beautiful, cruel, materialistic singer whom he recognizes would destroy his ideals and reduce him to pursuing wealth for her sake.

Geraldine openly admits that she wants Cyril only for his position and earning potential, while Cyril confesses that his noble character masks darker appetites he has long struggled to control. At his friend’s urging, he leaves England for several months, hoping distance and Elspeth’s compassionate support will help him master himself.

While Cyril is in India, Geraldine travels to Calcutta and arrives just before his intended departure. Cyril then ceases writing. Three months later, the narrator learns that Cyril has died at a shooting party after a bullet passed through his head. The death is presented publicly as an accident, but its circumstances suggest that Cyril may have taken his own life rather than submit to the destructive passion he feared.
