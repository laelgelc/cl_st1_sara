Tim O’Connor abandons his youthful religious and poetic ideals for journalism, decadent companions, alcohol, gambling, and an exploitative marriage. Despite his efforts to become cynical and corrupt, he retains an innate gentleness and purity. Increasingly terrified of darkness and ghosts, he confesses to his friend Rick Dodson that he fears his past misdeeds taking shape before him.

One night, after they attend *Faust*, Dodson accidentally leaves Tim alone in a darkened office. Returning, he sees Tim transfixed by a radiant, beautiful apparition embodying perfect goodness. The vision paralyzes Tim. He later explains that it was the shape of all the good he might have done—and that this unrealized goodness is what he truly fears. After another breakdown, he dies peacefully in a madhouse, while his friends respond to his death with mournful, darkly comic concern about the darkness of his grave.
