A Russian submarine ventures through an enemy minefield but becomes disoriented after striking a sunken wreck. Trapped among mines and running out of oxygen, the crew is forced to surface, where enemy aircraft and warships attack. The submarine escapes toward a rocky inlet, torpedoing an enemy mine-layer before hiding on the seabed.

For two weeks the crew shelters in the inlet, surfacing only at night and eventually surviving on caught fish. When a nearby naval battle opens a path home, they attempt to break through the blockade. Instead of escaping, however, Captain Andrey and his men seize the chance to attack a powerful enemy battleship. Their first torpedo misses, their periscope is destroyed, and a second close-range attack badly damages the submarine. Andrey is knocked unconscious, Yakovlev is killed, and water begins flooding the vessel.

The surviving sailors struggle to pump out the water and raise the submarine, but darkness, failing equipment, and exhaustion leave them expecting death. Preparing to open the hatch and destroy the boat rather than surrender, they hear footsteps on the hull. When they cautiously open it, they discover that Russian forces have found them, bringing their ordeal to an unexpectedly hopeful end.
