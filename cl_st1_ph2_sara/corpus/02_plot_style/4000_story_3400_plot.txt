As a young minister in Balweary, Murdoch Soulis dismisses local superstition and rescues the suspected witch Janet M’Clour from a mob, then employs her at his isolated manse. The next morning Janet appears horribly transformed, with a twisted neck, distorted speech, and an inability to utter God’s name. Although the villagers believe the real Janet is dead and something evil inhabits her body, Soulis attributes her condition to illness and continues protecting her.

During an oppressive summer, Soulis encounters a sinister black man in an old graveyard and pursues him toward the manse, where the stranger mysteriously vanishes. Seeing Janet immediately afterward, Soulis begins to suspect a connection between them and wonders whether she is a demon occupying a corpse.

On the night of August 17, Soulis hears a struggle in Janet’s room and finds her hanging dead from a single nail by a fragile thread. After he locks the room and retreats downstairs, the corpse frees itself, unlocks the door, descends the stairs, and approaches him outside. Soulis confronts it in God’s name and commands it to return to the grave or hell. Lightning strikes the animated corpse, reducing it to ashes as a violent storm breaks. The black man is seen fleeing the district that morning, suggesting that the devil had inhabited Janet’s body and has now been driven away.

Soulis survives but suffers a prolonged breakdown. The ordeal permanently transforms him into a solitary, haunted preacher who annually delivers a terrifying sermon about the devil on the anniversary of the encounter.
