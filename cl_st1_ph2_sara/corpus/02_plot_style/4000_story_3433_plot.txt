Margaret Delandre, after a bitter quarrel with her brother Wykham, becomes the companion—and possibly the secret wife—of the aristocratic Geoffrey Brent. While traveling in Switzerland, their carriage plunges into a torrent. Margaret is presumed dead, but she later returns horribly scarred and tells Wykham that Geoffrey deliberately removed the lynchpin, causing the crash. Bent on revenge, she goes to Brent’s Rock, where Geoffrey now lives with his new Italian wife, who is expecting a child.

Geoffrey encounters Margaret in the house, kills her, and conceals her body beneath a cracked hearthstone in the old hall, using lime intended for renovations. Soon strands of Margaret’s golden, grey-streaked hair begin growing through the crack. Geoffrey repeatedly burns them away while becoming increasingly terrified and secretive. His wife, disturbed by Wykham’s accusations and by the inexplicable hair, falls into a fever.

One night she insists that Geoffrey accompany her to the hall to watch the “growing gold.” She falls asleep against him while the supernatural hair continues to emerge, eventually entwining his feet. By morning, the locked hall is forced open, and Geoffrey and his wife are found dead beside the hearth: she appears to have died peacefully in her sleep, while his face is frozen in horror as Margaret’s hair coils around him.
