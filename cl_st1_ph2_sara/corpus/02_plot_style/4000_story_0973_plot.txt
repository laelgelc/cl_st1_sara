After impulsive, independent Hilary St. Orme publicly cancels her engagement to Viscount John Merrivale over his objection to her friendship with another man, Merrivale counters with an announcement that the wedding will proceed. Hilary retreats to her brother’s houseboat, where she meets “Jacques,” a rugged, mysterious friend of her cousin. Unaware that Jacques is Merrivale in disguise, she quickly becomes attracted to his strength, courtesy, and apparent simplicity.

Over several weeks, Hilary falls in love with Jacques. When her rejected suitor Cosmo Fletcher threatens to tell Merrivale about the romance, an accident interrupts them: Hilary’s sleeve catches fire, and Jacques burns his hands saving her. A tattoo exposes his identity to Fletcher, who recognizes him as the man who once thrashed him. Later, Jacques visits Hilary without his disguise and reveals that he is Merrivale. He explains that he adopted the deception because he loved her and now offers her the freedom she once demanded. Hilary admits she no longer wants it, having fallen in love with him, and they reconcile. Their wedding is soon officially scheduled.
