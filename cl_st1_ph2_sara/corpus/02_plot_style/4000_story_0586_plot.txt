Shorty, a formidable dwarf working for the Bar X ranch, stops at Bailey’s isolated roadhouse, where he violently rebukes anyone who mocks his appearance. Newly elected sheriff Ross Turney soon arrives with his bride, intending to stay overnight. Though initially hostile toward Ross, Shorty becomes deeply enamored of the bride because she treats him kindly and without ridicule.

That night, Shorty secretly drinks at the bar and observes Bailey signaling accomplices outside. Bailey admits the notorious Tremper gang, who overpower Ross and threaten to kill him after apparently attacking his wife. Shorty initially resolves not to interfere, but when he hears that the bride may have been harmed, he confronts the outlaws. Despite being drunk, he holds the gang at gunpoint, wounds their leader, and prevents Bailey from seizing a weapon. The unharmed bride brings Ross his gun, allowing him to secure the prisoners.

Ross offers Shorty the entire reward for capturing the Trempers, but Shorty insists that the bride receive half for their planned honeymoon. He returns to the Bar X at dawn, joking that the outlaws earned his affection because, apart from the bride, they were the only people present who had not laughed at him.
