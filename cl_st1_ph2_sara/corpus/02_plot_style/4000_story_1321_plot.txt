A group of children, newly captivated by classical myths, decide to reenact Jason’s quest for the Golden Fleece. Harold begins by imagining an old pig-trough as the Argo, but Edward proposes a real voyage in Farmer Larkin’s boat, which they take without permission.

Sailing upstream and transforming the narrow river into a legendary seascape through make-believe, the boys eventually land near a secluded, overgrown garden. There they meet Lucy, a pale, lonely young woman whom they identify with Medea. She eagerly plays with them, briefly escaping the sadness of being confined by her elderly aunt while waiting faithfully for someone who may have forgotten her. When the aunt appears and sends Lucy indoors, the boys retreat, wishing they could rescue her.

They reunite with Edward, who has been chased from a nearby village after provoking a woman’s cat, and begin the return journey. Expecting Farmer Larkin to be waiting for them, Edward devises a trick: they leave the boat drifting past the landing while hiding on the opposite bank. When the farmer follows it toward the bridge, they recover the boat, return it to its moorings, and escape before being caught.
