At a dinner celebrating his son’s return from university, a merchant gives an unexpectedly eloquent toast praising the younger generation and declaring that children should surpass their parents. Encouraged by this apparent openness, the son later corrects his father during a political discussion. The merchant takes the correction as disrespect, and their disagreement becomes a public quarrel.

An old teacher leads the upset young man away and explains that people often praise youthful independence in speeches without accepting it in practice. The son passionately vows that he will always respect truth and youth, no matter his age. The teacher predicts that, once the son grows older and gains his father’s position, he will behave in exactly the same way—and the story concludes that the prediction proves correct.
