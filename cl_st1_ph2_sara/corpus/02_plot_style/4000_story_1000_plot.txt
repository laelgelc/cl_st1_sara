Ten-year-old Tony Rossi, born on a planet conquered and colonized by Earth, considers it home and spends his days among the native Pas-udeti children. When the long-retreating Pas-udeti unexpectedly defeat the Terran fleet at Orion, generations of suppressed resentment erupt. Tony’s native friends reject him as an occupier, and a mob attacks him, destroying his robotic companion before a Terran ship rescues him.

Back at the human settlement, fighting spreads as the Pas-udeti move to reclaim their world. Tony realizes that his home is stolen ground and that the natives had tolerated Terrans only while humanity remained militarily dominant. His father finally admits that Earth’s expansion has reached its limit: the Pas-udeti victory marks the beginning of a centuries-long Terran retreat toward Earth, which humanity will resist every step of the way.
