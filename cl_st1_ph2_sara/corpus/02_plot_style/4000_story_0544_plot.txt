Left alone in her family’s house, young Martha explores the attic and discovers a mysterious locked chest sent from Italy by her missing Uncle Walter. Her curiosity leads her to find a key and open it, releasing three flamboyant Italian bandits named Victor, Beni, and Lugui.

Disoriented to learn they are in Chicago, the bandits reject Martha’s suggestions for honest employment and decide to continue their profession by robbing the house. They return to the attic carrying clothing, silverware, decorations, and other household valuables.

When the doorbell rings, Martha claims that 112 heavily armed police officers have arrived. Terrified, the bandits accept her offer of protection and climb back into the chest. Martha forces the lid shut, locks them inside, and is left to return all the stolen property downstairs.
