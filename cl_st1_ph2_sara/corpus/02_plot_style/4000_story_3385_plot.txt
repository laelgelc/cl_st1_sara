Timid Mrs. Laughton, alone in her remote prairie home and entrusted with her husband Tom’s payroll money, discovers an armed burglar hiding beneath her bed. Though terrified, she confronts him and uses incessant chatter, feigned innocence, and misdirection to keep him from finding the money. When an accident distracts him, she hides the cash beneath her chair. She then invents a story that an Apache is sleeping in the kitchen, frightening the burglar into fleeing. Once he is gone, her courage collapses and she faints. Tom later returns and revives her, whereupon she anxiously asks whether there really is an Apache in the kitchen.
