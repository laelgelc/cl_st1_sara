During an African campaign, a British force is trapped without water, its wounded men suffering in unbearable heat. Mortally wounded Private Ford speaks with army doctor Montagu Durant about Durant’s former friend Leonard Rotherby, who betrayed him, married Durant’s beloved, disgraced himself, and was supposedly left to die of thirst while prospecting in Australia.

That night, despite orders, Ford crawls through enemy fire to a nearby river and drags back water for the hospital. As he dies from the effort, his delirious memories reveal that he is actually Rotherby, living under another name; the tale of witnessing Rotherby’s death concealed his own identity. Durant recognizes and forgives his old friend, calling him Leo, but Rotherby dies with a smile before relief reaches the camp at dawn.
