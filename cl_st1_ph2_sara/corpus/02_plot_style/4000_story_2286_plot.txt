Nikolai Almazof, a poor army officer struggling through staff academy, accidentally blots a survey map and disguises the mark as a clump of bushes. When his professor insists no bushes grow at that location and plans to inspect it, Nikolai fears disgrace and expulsion. His devoted wife, Vera, devises a solution: they pawn their valuables, hire a gardener, and plant lilac bushes at the site that very night. The next day, the professor sees the bushes, apologizes for doubting Nikolai, and accepts his work. Relieved and triumphant, Nikolai and Vera celebrate their success, and the lilac becomes a cherished symbol of her resourcefulness and devotion.
