Martha Maria Mixon leaves her charming but chronically idle husband, Madison, in the South and builds a prosperous life in New York, promising to take him back if he ever makes a man of himself. Years later, while preparing to host a visiting preacher for her church’s Christmas celebration, she is astonished when Madison arrives on Christmas Eve claiming he has reformed. Her hope falters the next morning when he leaves on unexplained business instead of accompanying her to church. At the service, however, Martha discovers that Madison himself is the visiting minister. After he delivers a moving sermon and publicly recounts his transformation, she proudly reconciles with him and brings him home for Christmas dinner.
