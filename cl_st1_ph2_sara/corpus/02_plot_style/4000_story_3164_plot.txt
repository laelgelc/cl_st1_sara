A cruel, joke-obsessed king keeps a disabled dwarf as his court jester and delights in humiliating him. The jester shares a close bond with Trippetta, another captive dwarf. Before a masquerade, the king forces the jester to drink wine despite its harmful effects, then strikes Trippetta and throws wine in her face when she pleads for mercy.

Enraged, the jester proposes that the king and his seven ministers attend the masquerade disguised as chained orangutans. He coats their costumes in highly flammable tar and flax, chains them together, and arranges for the ballroom’s chandelier mechanism to hoist them above the crowd. During the celebration, he suspends the eight men in midair and sets their costumes ablaze, declaring the execution his final jest and revenge for their cruelty. After they burn to death, he escapes through the skylight, apparently aided by Trippetta, and the two are never seen again.
