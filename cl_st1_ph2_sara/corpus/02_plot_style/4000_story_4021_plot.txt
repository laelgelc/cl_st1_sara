An aged wizard, Aengus son of Forbis, lives in a ruined island monastery with a devoted seventeen-year-old servant. Having spent his life studying magic and summoning ancient gods and fairies, Aengus seeks a legendary instant when the Song of the Immortal Powers can be heard, granting eternal youth. A fairy tells him that the moment will arrive shortly after dawn and instructs him to have the monastery filled with green boughs, rushes, roses, and lilies.

Despite fearing the supernatural beings his master attracts, the boy faithfully prepares the room and dreams of the ordinary family life Aengus has promised to fund with his savings. At dawn, as the natural world bursts into song, the boy returns expecting to find his master rejuvenated. Instead, he discovers Aengus dead, clutching flowers beside a wallet of money and a traveling staff. The boy concludes that Aengus wasted his life seeking immortality from supernatural powers rather than finding meaning in prayer and in the mortal life already given to him.
