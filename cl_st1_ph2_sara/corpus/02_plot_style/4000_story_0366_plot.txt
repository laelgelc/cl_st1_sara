While staying at a Yorkshire estate, Weigall worries over the unexplained disappearance of his closest friend, Wyatt Gifford, who vanished two nights earlier. Walking alone beside the dangerous Strid, he sees a hand struggling against the current and recognizes Gifford by his cuff links and fingers. After a desperate effort, Weigall frees the submerged body and retrieves it from a quiet pool, believing his friend may still be revived. But as he prepares to resuscitate him, he notices a foul odor and discovers that the body has no face, revealing that Gifford has long been dead despite the hand’s seemingly living struggle—an eerie fulfillment of Gifford’s theory that the soul can linger within the corpse.
