A miserly old sailor named Thomas Geary falls gravely ill during a voyage from Sydney to London. Determined that no one should inherit his £600 savings, he gives the money to a cabin boy, Jimmy, and orders him to throw it overboard. After Thomas dies, sailors Bill Hicks and the narrator suspect Jimmy secretly kept the money. They search and threaten him until he admits possessing it and claims he hid it inside the second mate’s mattress.

Unable to reach the mattress, Bill secretly buys it from the mate for two pounds, intending to divide the fortune only with the narrator and cheat Jimmy. When the ship reaches London, Jimmy discovers their scheme and insists on receiving his share. Bill and the narrator take the mattress below and cut it open, but find no money. By the time they return to the deck, Jimmy has vanished without collecting his belongings or wages. They realize he deceived them about the hiding place and escaped with Thomas’s fortune.
