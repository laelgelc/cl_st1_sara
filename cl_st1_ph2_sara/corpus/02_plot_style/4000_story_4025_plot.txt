During a harsh winter, a ragged beggar seeks shelter in a monastic community at Tullagh. The monks welcome him and assign him the arduous task of turning the millstone. He works tirelessly and cheerfully while befriending Olioll, a young scholar considered unusually dull and inattentive.

Olioll suddenly becomes the monastery’s best student, arousing Brother Dove’s suspicion. Following the boy, Dove discovers that the beggar comforts him, waits until he sleeps, and prays for divine knowledge to awaken in his empty mind. A miraculous light surrounds the child, accompanied by the scent of roses.

The abbot recognizes the beggar as Aengus, a renowned holy man who vanished after fleeing the pride caused by his fame. Believing Aengus has achieved perfect selflessness and closeness to God, the monks process to the millhouse and kneel outside, waiting to honor him and ask him to guide them along his spiritual path.
