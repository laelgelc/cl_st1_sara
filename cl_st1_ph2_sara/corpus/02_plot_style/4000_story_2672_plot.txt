Sun and Moon, two young siblings, watch their home being transformed for an elaborate concert and supper. They marvel at the decorations and lavish food, especially an ice pudding shaped like a little pink house. After being dressed up and briefly shown to the guests, they are sent to bed but later sneak downstairs to observe the party.

Their unusually merry father brings them into the dining room after the feast. Moon eagerly eats the nut that formed the ice house’s door handle, but Sun is horrified to find the beautiful table ruined, the food scattered, and the little house broken and melting. Overcome by the destruction, he cries that it is horrid and is angrily sent back to the nursery.
