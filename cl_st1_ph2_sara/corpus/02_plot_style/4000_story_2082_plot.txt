Maria, a small, unmarried laundry worker known for making peace, looks forward to spending Halloween with Joe Donnelly, whom she helped raise. After serving tea at the laundry, she buys cakes and an expensive slice of plumcake for Joe’s family. On the tram, a courteous, slightly drunk gentleman distracts her, and she later discovers that the plumcake has disappeared.

At Joe’s home, the family welcomes Maria warmly. She tries to reconcile Joe with his estranged brother Alphy, but Joe angrily refuses. During a Halloween fortune-telling game, the blindfolded Maria first touches a saucer containing a soft, wet substance—apparently clay, an omen of death—but the others quickly remove it and let her choose again. She then receives a prayer book, which they jokingly interpret as a sign that she will enter a convent. The evening ends with Maria singing an old romantic song about wealth, status, and being loved, while her lonely and disappointed hopes remain unspoken.
