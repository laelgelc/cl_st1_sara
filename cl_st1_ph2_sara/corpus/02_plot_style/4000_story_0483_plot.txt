Ella Druce, a former artist’s model, marries the devoted son of ruthless financier Druce and is warmly accepted by her wealthy father-in-law. Despite her riches, she is miserable because the socially prominent Sneed sisters refuse to call on her. Druce asks their father, General Sneed, to arrange a visit, but Sneed admits he cannot persuade his daughters.

Feigning continued friendship, Druce buys Sneed’s crucial securities with a cheque he asks Sneed not to cash immediately, then takes him out on his yacht while a market panic destroys the Sneed fortune and the bank backing the cheque collapses. Afterward, Druce offers to return the securities—and help Sneed recover financially—if the Sneed sisters visit Ella. Facing ruin, the sisters promptly call on her.
