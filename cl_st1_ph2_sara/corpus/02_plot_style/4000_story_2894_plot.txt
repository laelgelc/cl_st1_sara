Ahab detects the scent and wake of a nearby sperm whale and alters the Pequod’s course. At daybreak, he sights Moby Dick and leads three whaleboats in pursuit while Starbuck remains aboard the ship.

After sounding, the white whale resurfaces beneath Ahab’s boat. Ahab narrowly turns the craft, but Moby Dick seizes it in his jaws, breaks it in two, and throws Ahab and his crew into the sea. The whale circles the wreckage threateningly until the Pequod drives him away, allowing the other boats to rescue everyone.

Though physically crushed, Ahab quickly revives and insists on continuing the hunt. Because Moby Dick is too swift for the exhausted boats, the crews return to the Pequod, which pursues him throughout the day. Ahab dismisses the shattered boat as an ill omen, claims the lookout’s gold doubloon for himself, and promises a greater reward when the whale is killed. As darkness falls, he reduces sail to avoid overtaking Moby Dick at night and keeps watch until dawn, determined to resume the chase.
