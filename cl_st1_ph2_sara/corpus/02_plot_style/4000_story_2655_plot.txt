After six years apart, Vera unexpectedly meets a former lover in a café. As they talk, he recalls their relationship in romantic, idealized terms, while her own memories initially center on his irritating habits and embarrassing behavior. Yet his stories of traveling through the countries they once dreamed of visiting awaken her buried longing for freedom, adventure, and the intimacy they once shared.

He tells her that she inspired his journeys and remembers wanting to protect her and carry her away like a magic carpet. Feeling uniquely understood, lonely, and regretful, Vera begins to believe she threw away her only chance at happiness and wonders whether they might reunite. But he abruptly reframes their former bond as nothing more than mutual egotism and self-absorption, treating it as a psychological condition he has outgrown. Deeply wounded, Vera leaves without a word. He is bewildered by her departure, then turns his attention to the bill and insists he not be charged for the untouched cream.
