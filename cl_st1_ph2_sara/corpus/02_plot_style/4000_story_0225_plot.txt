A little boy catches cold after secretly wading through a deep gutter. While his mother gives him elder-flower tea, an old storyteller claims that a tale is growing from the teapot. The boy sees an elder tree emerge, inhabited by a magical old woman who presides over the memories of an elderly sailor and his wife as they recall their childhood, courtship, separation, family, and golden wedding.

The woman then transforms into a young girl and carries the boy on a magical journey through the Danish countryside and all four seasons. He grows into a sailor, preserving an elder blossom she gives him as a reminder of home. In old age, he sits beneath an elder tree with his wife on their own golden wedding day. The maiden reveals that she is Memory, crowning the couple and showing that their life has echoed the earlier couple’s story.

The boy awakens uncertain whether he dreamed or heard the tale. The teapot is ordinary again, and his mother says the Elder-tree Mother remains inside it.
