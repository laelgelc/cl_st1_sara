James Holroyd, a brutal, drunken electrician, oversees three dynamos powering an electric railway and routinely abuses his foreign assistant, Azuma-zi. Fascinated by the largest machine’s immense power, ceaseless motion, and deadly electrical force, Azuma-zi begins to regard it as a living god. He secretly worships it and prays for protection from Holroyd.

After Holroyd discovers and punishes his worship, Azuma-zi interprets the dynamo’s sounds as a command to sacrifice his tormentor. He attacks Holroyd and forces him against the machine’s electrical contacts, killing him. Authorities mistake the death for suicide, convincing Azuma-zi that the dynamo has accepted the sacrifice.

Believing the machine may still be hungry, Azuma-zi attempts to sacrifice the scientific manager who arrives to take charge. The manager fights him off until another official approaches. To escape capture—or complete his devotion—Azuma-zi seizes the live terminals and is electrocuted. The manager then realizes that Azuma-zi also murdered Holroyd, ending the brief religion with both a human sacrifice and the death of its worshipper.
