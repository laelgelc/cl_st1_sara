In Mandeville, Louisiana, the beautiful Juanita Alvarez defies her stern grandfather, Grandpere Colomes, by openly favoring Mercer Grangeman, a blond American sailor. Grandpere resents both Mercer and his regatta boat, which is named after Juanita and carries her pink-and-gold colors. Before the race, Juanita promises to marry Mercer if he wins.

During the regatta, a violent storm sweeps across Lake Pontchartrain and threatens the entire fleet. Mercer abandons concern for victory, takes command of the other boats, and guides them through the gale to a sheltered inlet. After the storm, all nine boats and every sailor return safely, with Mercer’s boat leading them home.

Mandeville celebrates Mercer as a hero, Juanita greets him with love and admiration, and even Grandpere grudgingly admits that an American can nearly equal a Frenchman. Juanita and Mercer’s betrothal is celebrated the following week.
