A young woman interested in psychic phenomena spends a night alone in a supposedly haunted London house, believing it to be haunted by a murdered woman. Inside, she encounters a sorrowful man who reveals that he is a ghost—the spirit of a misanthrope who died there from terror and has remained trapped by fear, hatred, and remorse. He explains that curious investigators only deepen his torment by approaching him with fear, and that genuine sympathy and love could release him.

Though initially terrified, the woman overcomes her fear and feels compassion for him. She embraces and kisses the ghost with sincere love, whereupon he dissolves into her arms and the oppressive haunting vanishes. Later, the house’s owner admits that the murdered-woman story was fabricated as a test: the real ghost was his cousin, who had gone mad and died in the house from morbid terror. Realizing that her encounter matched the true history, the woman keeps the details of the ghost’s release to herself.
