Eighty-five-year-old Amelia Bellowes pays a supposed spiritual guide, Mr. Thirkell, to take her and a group of elderly women from Mars into deep space in search of God. After repeated delays, the women force him to reveal that his rocket is a dilapidated wreck and the entire expedition a fraud. With their savings gone, little left to return to on Earth, and death already near, Mrs. Bellowes persuades the others to take the promised journey anyway, compelling Thirkell to serve as navigator.

The rocket launches but soon explodes. Thirkell is hurled toward the Sun, while Mrs. Bellowes and the other women drift outward through space with only minutes of oxygen remaining. Accepting their fate, Mrs. Bellowes imagines the darkness as a church, the stars as candles, and God’s golden hand reaching toward her, convinced that despite the deception, they are finally approaching the Lord.
