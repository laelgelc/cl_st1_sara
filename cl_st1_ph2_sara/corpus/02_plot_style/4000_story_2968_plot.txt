Van Cheele encounters a strange, naked boy living in his woods who claims to hunt on four legs at night and eat animals—and sometimes children. Troubled by recent livestock disappearances and the unexplained loss of a miller’s child, Van Cheele suspects the boy may be dangerous. When the boy enters his home, Van Cheele’s aunt mistakes him for a homeless orphan, clothes him, names him Gabriel-Ernest, and takes him in.

Van Cheele consults his artist friend Cunningham, who reveals that he saw the boy transform into a wolf at sunset. Realizing Gabriel-Ernest is a werewolf, Van Cheele races home, only to learn that the boy has gone to escort a young child home. As the sun sets, Van Cheele hears a terrified scream near the mill-stream. Neither the child nor Gabriel-Ernest is ever seen again. Because the boy’s discarded clothes are found by the water, everyone assumes he drowned trying to save the child, and Van Cheele’s aunt erects a memorial honoring his supposed sacrifice. Knowing the truth, Van Cheele refuses to contribute.
