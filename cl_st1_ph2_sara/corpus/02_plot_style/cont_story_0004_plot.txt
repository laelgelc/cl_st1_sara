An unmarried man visiting his elderly parents learns that Raphael, a former houseboy from his childhood, has been arrested as the leader of an armed-robbery gang. The news revives memories of the intense bond he formed with Raphael at twelve through their shared love of kung fu films and secret practice sessions.

When Raphael contracted conjunctivitis, the boy defied his parents’ orders and tenderly administered Raphael’s eye drops, developing feelings of intimacy and admiration. After catching the infection himself, he expected Raphael to show the same devotion. Instead, he found Raphael flirting with a female house help and felt jealous and betrayed. During their confrontation, the boy accidentally fell from a veranda, then falsely claimed that Raphael had pushed him. His parents immediately dismissed Raphael. Although he had time to confess the truth, he remained silent—a betrayal that haunts him years later when he learns what became of Raphael.
