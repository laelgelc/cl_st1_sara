Madeline Henry grows up in a loving, cheerful home and marries Edward Leslie, a prosperous and respectable but cold, calculating businessman. Although Edward believes that providing wealth and comfort proves his love, he withholds the tenderness and affection Madeline needs. His emotional distance, impatience, and prolonged silences gradually break her spirit and damage her health.

After years of melancholy, Madeline realizes that her children need her loving presence to counter their father’s harshness. She resolves to live for them, suppressing her own suffering and devoting herself to their care. This purpose temporarily revives her, but cannot restore her health or mend her marriage. She eventually dies before her children are grown, asking Edward for one final kiss. Only then does he understand that his coldness has slowly destroyed her, leaving him burdened by lasting grief and remorse.
