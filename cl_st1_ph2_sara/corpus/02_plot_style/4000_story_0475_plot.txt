After the beloved Sir Jasper dies from a hunting accident, his loyal housekeeper, Nelly, fulfills his final request by placing a secret green case in his coffin. His selfish son, Jasper, fears the case contains documents affecting his inheritance and secretly opens the coffin to steal it. Nelly and the family doctor catch him, and the doctor discovers that the case contains proof that Robert, the gamekeeper, is Sir Jasper’s legitimate firstborn son, while Jasper is the son of a later marriage.

At the will reading, Robert is awarded the personal property, while Jasper initially expects to inherit the entailed estate. However, because Jasper violated his father’s coffin and exposed the documents his father meant to conceal, Robert’s senior birthright becomes known, making him heir to the estate as well. Jasper leaves in fury and unsuccessfully challenges the succession. Robert takes possession of the Hall, proves as kind and beloved as his father, and retains Nelly as housekeeper, while she treasures the portrait Sir Jasper left her.
