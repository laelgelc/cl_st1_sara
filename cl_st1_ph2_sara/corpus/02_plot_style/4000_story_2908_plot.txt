David Hartley has loved and repeatedly proposed to his neighbor Josephine Elliott for eighteen years. Independent and comfortable on her own farm, Josephine refuses him again and warns that if he ever proposes once more, she will stop speaking to him.

Soon afterward, David’s sister and housekeeper, Zillah, develops pneumonia. Despite her estrangement from Zillah, Josephine answers David’s desperate request for help and stays at his house during the illness. There she discovers the household is filthy and disorderly and sees how poorly David has been cared for. She cleans, cooks good meals, mends his socks, and becomes increasingly sorry for him, while David grows accustomed to her cheerful companionship and dreads her departure.

Once Zillah begins recovering, Josephine decides that she cannot return home and leave David to live in neglect. Interpreting her concern as duty, she suggests that Zillah recuperate elsewhere and offers to keep house for David—provided they marry. Astonished and delighted, David embraces and kisses her, and Josephine returns the kiss.
