A young Greek and German instructor at Willard’s School mishandles the traditional hazing of new teachers by punishing the ringleaders severely, earning a reputation as a tyrant and losing the confidence of both students and faculty. After further disciplinary trouble threatens the school baseball team and confirms his apparent failure, he writes a letter of resignation.

Before he can deliver it, the school’s team asks him to replace its exhausted pitcher in the deciding championship game. Though out of practice and initially rusty, he recovers the baseball skill that once made him a college star. His pitching shuts down the opposing team, while his athletic ability transforms his students’ resentment into admiration. In the final inning, he reaches base and steals home, scoring the winning run.

After the victory, an old college acquaintance persuades him to destroy his resignation. With his relationship to the school renewed, he remains at Willard’s and becomes a successful teacher over the next two years.
