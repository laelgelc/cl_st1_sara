A dying baseball fanatic known for his famous “Well-Well-Well” yell attends one final game to watch his nephew and protégé, rookie outfielder Burt, play for Philadelphia against New York. Though weak and visibly ill, he becomes increasingly absorbed as Burt displays exceptional speed, fielding, and throwing ability throughout a long, closely contested game.

In the thirteenth inning, with Philadelphia trailing and down to its final out, Burt drives in the tying and winning runs and races home himself. Overcome with pride and joy, the old man summons his remaining strength and unleashes his legendary yell across the stadium. Burt recognizes it and rushes toward him, but the effort causes the old fan to collapse. He is carried away in an ambulance, pale and motionless but conscious and smiling, with Burt beside him.
