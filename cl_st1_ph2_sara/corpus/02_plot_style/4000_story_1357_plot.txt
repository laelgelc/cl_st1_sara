Professional gambler Jack Hamlin becomes captivated by a beautiful woman traveling to Wingdam, only to discover that she is the long-absent wife of Brown, a weak, indebted acquaintance. Jack secretly helps Brown become prosperous, while Mrs. Brown grows popular and increasingly estranged from her jealous husband.

Months later, Brown confides to Jack that he believes his wife loves another man and plans to run away. Jack advises him to kill the rival, but then reveals through his actions that he himself is the man Mrs. Brown intended to meet: he carries her note directing him to bring a buggy to the corral at three. Torn between love and loyalty, Jack burns the note, urges Brown to sell everything, leave Wingdam with his wife, and rebuild their marriage elsewhere. He then abandons the planned elopement and rides away alone, singing into the dawn.
