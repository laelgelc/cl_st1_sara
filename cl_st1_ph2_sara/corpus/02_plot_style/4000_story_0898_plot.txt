Alphonse Pettipon, a meticulous second-class steward aboard the *Voltaire*, takes immense pride in keeping his cabins spotless but longs for promotion so he can afford a violin for his talented son. When a disheveled passenger finds a bedbug in his berth, Pettipon is devastated, believing the discovery will destroy both his career and honor.

Determined to prove that the passenger brought the insect aboard, Pettipon reports the incident successively to his immediate superior, the chief steward, and the captain. Each treats the matter as trivial, but Pettipon mistakes their impatience and humor for concealed condemnation. Finally, he appeals to Victor Ronssoy, president of the steamship line, explaining his spotless record, devotion to his work, and hopes for his son.

Ronssoy recognizes that Pettipon’s extraordinary distress demonstrates rare dedication and promotes him to steward of his private suite. Overjoyed, Pettipon refuses to discard the bedbug and resolves to preserve it as a lucky watch charm.
