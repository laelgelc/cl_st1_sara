Texas Ranger Bob Buckley is secretly a physical coward, though his comrades consider him exceptionally brave because he repeatedly forces himself into danger. When the vengeful Leandro Garcia disguises himself as an old woman, crosses the guarded border, and shoots saloonkeeper Bud Dawson, Buckley pursues him alone.

Nearby, Alvarita, a celebrated snake handler renowned for her fearlessness, searches for her escaped python, Kuku. Garcia encounters her in a ravine and threatens her, but Buckley arrives. Seeing that Garcia is unarmed, Buckley discards his own guns to fight fairly. When he and Alvarita exchange a glance, each undergoes an unexpected change: Buckley’s fear disappears, while Alvarita discovers vulnerability. Garcia draws a hidden knife, but Buckley disarms and knocks him out.

After Garcia is taken away, Buckley and Alvarita walk together, she now acting shy and dependent. Alvarita then screams at the sight of a harmless caterpillar and takes refuge in Buckley’s arms, completing the ironic transformation of the fearless snake queen into a timid woman and the secret coward into a genuinely fearless man.
