Richard Keeling dies aboard a transatlantic steamship after falling through an unguarded hatch. The shipping company denies liability and persuades his impoverished widow to accept only a refund of his fare.

On later voyages, Keeling’s ghost repeatedly boards with a valid ticket for Room 18, insisting that the company fulfill its contract to deliver him to New York. Each time, he mysteriously falls into the hold and dies again despite the hatch being secured. He tells the terrified purser that he will keep returning until the company compensates his widow, demanding £2,000 and adding £100 for every voyage.

After repeated accidents, damaging publicity, and mass resignations among the crew, the managing director sails in Room 18 to investigate. Upon returning, the company pays Mrs. Keeling £2,100, publicly presenting the payment as an act of corporate generosity.
