Effie Bauer, a successful and independent department-store buyer, is courted by Gabe Marks, a traveling salesman who has taken her out during his quarterly visits for six years. When Gabe proposes, Effie refuses despite liking him, arguing that they are too old to begin saving and sacrificing and that marriage would force her to surrender the comforts she has earned.

After a severe bout of typhoid leaves Effie weak and lonely, she realizes how deeply she longs for a husband, home, and family. She sends word that she has changed her mind, and when Gabe visits, she accepts his renewed proposal.

Upon returning to work, however, Effie is quickly drawn back into her former life of fashionable clothes, financial independence, and freedom from a husband’s objections. Tempted by an expensive blouse and reminded that she is her own boss, she decides to wire Gabe that their arrangement is off—changing her mind once again.
