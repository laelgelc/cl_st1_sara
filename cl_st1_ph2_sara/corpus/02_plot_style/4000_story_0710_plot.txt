A middle-aged surveyor’s clerk travels to a country estate to assess alterations intended to improve the owner’s view. Spotting the small wood he believes is to be cut down, he enters it as a shortcut. Inside, the paths seem to shift, strange green-clad figures appear, and the trees, gates, and glades repeatedly turn him away. Though he tries to dismiss the phenomena as tricks of light, wind, and fear, the wood appears increasingly alive and hostile, finally trapping and attacking him before mysteriously throwing him back into the meadow.

Shaken, he continues to the house and discovers that the obscured line on his instructions had suggested a shortcut through the very wood. His client then identifies it as an ancient place known locally as the Fairy Wood and prepares to accompany him there to plan its destruction.
