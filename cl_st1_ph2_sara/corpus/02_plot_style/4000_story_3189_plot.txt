In the isolated, perfectly ordered borough of Vondervotteimittiss, generations of inhabitants live identical lives centered on clocks, cabbages, routine, and the conviction that nothing worthwhile exists beyond their surrounding hills. Their greatest pride is the flawless town clock, whose strokes synchronize every timepiece and activity in the village.

Just before noon, a strange, sinister fiddler crosses the hills, dances erratically into town, and climbs the clock tower. He assaults the revered belfry-man, interferes with the clock, and causes it to strike thirteen. Every clock and watch in the borough follows suit, throwing meals, habits, animals, and inhabitants into chaos. As the intruder continues ringing the bell and playing his fiddle wildly from the tower, the narrator flees and calls upon supporters of accurate time and traditional order to return with him and drive the disruptive stranger out.
