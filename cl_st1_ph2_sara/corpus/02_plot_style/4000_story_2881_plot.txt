George Spencer, a widowed New Orleans businessman suffering from overwork, twice dreams of a moonlit islet, two mysterious men, a palm tree, a marked rock, and precise geographic coordinates. He discovers that the coordinates point to Fernando de Noronha, a Brazilian convict island, and accepts his sea-captain nephew Ralph’s invitation to sail aboard the brig Sea Witch to Pernambuco.

On the return voyage, Ralph reluctantly agrees to stop near the island. George, the mate Seth Hawkins, and two sailors obtain permission from the governor to gather melons on a nearby islet. George recognizes the landscape from his dream, finds a rock inscribed with directions to go farther west, and digs beneath a blasted palm. He and Seth uncover a heavy zinc-covered wooden box, then narrowly escape officials who pursue them after becoming suspicious of their tools and activities.

Aboard the brig, they open the box and find 34,000 Brazilian milreis, along with a note explaining that Leon da Costa, a military commander loyal to Emperor Dom Pedro, buried his fortune there during political unrest in 1889. Unwilling to keep money that might belong to a living man, George holds it while making inquiries. After returning home, he learns that Da Costa died of yellow fever in 1890, so he accepts the treasure and gives $5,000 to Ralph. Alice later creates a parlor decoration displaying the mysterious coordinates, which the family describes to curious visitors as a marine puzzle.
