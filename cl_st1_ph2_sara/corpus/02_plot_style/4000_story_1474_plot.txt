Ralph Cranfield returns to his New England village after ten years of wandering the world in pursuit of three foretold signs: a heart-shaped jewel identifying his destined bride, a pointing hand revealing buried treasure, and three venerable messengers summoning him to power.

At home, he discovers that the signs have appeared in humble, familiar forms. A hand-shaped growth on a tree points toward the earth by his mother’s cottage, suggesting that his treasure lies in cultivating the land. Three village selectmen, led by a cane-wielding squire, offer him authority not as a king, but as the village schoolmaster. Finally, his childhood companion Faith Egerton still wears the heart-shaped brooch he gave her before leaving and accepts his proposal using the very words foretold.

Ralph realizes that the destiny he sought across the world was waiting at home: prosperity through honest labor, influence through teaching children, and happiness through Faith’s enduring love.
