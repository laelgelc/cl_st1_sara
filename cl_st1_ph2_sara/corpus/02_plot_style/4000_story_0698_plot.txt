A writer named Colston argues that ghost stories must be read in suitably frightening surroundings. When his skeptical friend Willard Marsh challenges him, Colston sends him alone at night to read a manuscript by candlelight in an abandoned, reputedly haunted house. Near midnight, a lost farm boy peers through the window and sees Marsh anxiously reading. A screech owl cries, Marsh leaps up and extinguishes the candle, and the terrified boy runs away.

The next day, the boy leads several men back to the house, where they find Marsh dead beside the overturned table. Colston’s manuscript contains a warning that it will explain Marsh’s death and that Colston intends to visit him after midnight, ominously implying a supernatural encounter and Colston’s own death. A relative of the house’s former owner burns the manuscript, leaving the exact cause of Marsh’s death uncertain.
