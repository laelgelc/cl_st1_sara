Kenelm Jerton meets a young woman in a crowded hotel dining room who claims to have lost her memory and remembers only that she is a titled lady. Having arrived without identifiable belongings, she has appropriated luggage labeled Kestrel-Smith and had its locks forced. She tries to determine her identity by ruling out various society women through tests involving food, flirting, tipping, and gambling.

After losing most of her money on a horse, she persuades Jerton to lend her three pounds for a return trip to London, where she hopes a club porter will identify her. She also convinces him to guard the stolen luggage while she slips away. Jerton then learns from another guest that she is actually Mrs. Stroope, a society golfer prone to periodic memory loss. When the furious true owner of the Kestrel-Smith luggage arrives and discovers that its locks have been forced, Jerton flees to the hotel’s Turkish bath to avoid the ensuing trouble.
