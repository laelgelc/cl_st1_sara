During a railway journey, an elderly man explains his belief that seemingly random events follow a mysterious, cruel logic. He recalls traveling from Tomsk to St. Petersburg with a cheerful young engineer returning home after five years in the Far East. Eager to reunite with his beloved wife and daughter, the engineer showers fellow passengers with gifts, repeatedly exchanges affectionate telegrams with his family, and becomes increasingly impatient as the train nears its destination.

At the St. Petersburg station, he spots his wife and daughter on the platform and calls out to them. In his excitement, he falls from the train and is crushed beneath its wheels just as their long-awaited reunion is about to occur. His widow later suggests that their certainty of future happiness tempted fate, while the narrator sees the death as evidence of an inscrutable power that completes supreme joy with an ending both absurd and horribly logical.
