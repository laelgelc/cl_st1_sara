Eleven-year-old Tommy and Billy run away from a mountain farm to imitate the hunters in their adventure books. After discovering they brought the wrong lunch basket, they survive on berries and poorly cooked trout, trap a woodchuck, and meet a real hunter who feeds them and warns them to return home. Tommy later kills a harmless bird with an arrow, regrets his cruelty, and secretly buries it.

Trying to find their trap, the boys lose their way as night falls. Frightened by reports of a bear, they sleep in a tree, but Billy rolls from his branch and is left dangling by his belt. Tommy goes for help, falls into an abandoned bear pit, and eventually falls asleep there. Meanwhile, their worried mothers organize a search. Guided by the hunter and his dog, the searchers rescue Billy from the tree and find Tommy unharmed in the pit.

The boys return safely to the farm, with Tommy celebrated as the hero of the adventure. Privately, however, he decides to wait until he is older before attempting another hunting expedition and resolves never again to kill a harmless bird.
