A young man dies before fulfilling his dreams and is buried far from the secluded spot he had chosen for his grave. His ghost lingers in a crowded cemetery, where he observes other spirits bound to earth by greed, guilt, forbidden love, patriotism, jealousy, grief, or fame. He realizes that earthly passions and the sorrow of the living delay the dead from ascending to heaven.

The ghost befriends the spirit of a pure, lonely young maiden newly buried in her ancestral vault. Her presence elevates him, and he hopes they will eventually rise to heaven together. One night she no longer answers his call: her purification is complete, and she has ascended without him. Though devastated, he trusts that she remembers him and believes his own release is near.

While awaiting it, he inspires a solitary writer through dreams, supplying the strange reflections that become this story. As the writer grows too worldly to receive him, the ghost departs and seeks instead to bless a pure living maiden, hoping to guide her toward a spiritual life so that, after death, neither of them will remain imprisoned near the grave.
