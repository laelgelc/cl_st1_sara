During a plague, a gravely ill man receives an accidental overdose of opium and enters a vivid, terrifying dream. He awakens in an exotic cottage perched above a storm-lashed promontory, where unnatural seas rapidly erode the land. Fleeing inland through strange tropical vegetation, he is drawn toward a colossal palm tree.

Beneath the tree, a radiant child and godlike beings promise to take him to Teloe, a celestial realm of eternal youth, beauty, and pleasure. He rises with them through the sky, surrounded by luminous singers, but disobeys a warning not to look back when he hears the pounding ocean below.

Looking down, he witnesses Earth’s destruction: seas consume the continents and abandoned cities, then drain into a vast fissure, exposing drowned civilizations and ancient, unknown lands. Steam engulfs him, his celestial companions vanish, and the planet finally explodes, leaving the sun and planets searching for their lost world. He then awakens in convalescence, permanently haunted by the vision and unwilling ever to take opium again.
