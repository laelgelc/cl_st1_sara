A miserly woman reluctantly buys her husband a silk umbrella after his coworkers mock his old and cheap ones. When the new umbrella is mysteriously riddled with cigar burns at his office, she refuses to replace it until a friend suggests claiming the damage on their fire insurance.

Obsessed with recovering the cost, she enlarges the damage herself and nervously visits the insurance company. She invents a story that a match accidentally fell into the umbrella at home and set it alight. After persistent bargaining, she persuades the manager to reimburse the full repair cost. Armed with his written authorization, she takes the umbrella to a first-rate shop and orders it re-covered with the finest, strongest silk available, unconcerned about the price because the insurer will pay.
