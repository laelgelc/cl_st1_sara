In a future society where aging and disease have been conquered, population is strictly controlled: each newborn may live only if someone else volunteers to die. Edward Wehling waits as his wife gives birth to triplets, but he has secured only one volunteer and must choose which child survives.

When the hospital’s chief obstetrician defends the system and a euthanasia hostess discusses its benefits, Wehling draws a gun. He kills them both, then himself, creating three vacancies so all his children can live. An elderly painter witnesses the deaths and reflects on humanity’s irreconcilable drive to reproduce and live forever on a finite planet. Unable to shoot himself, he calls the Federal Bureau of Termination and schedules his own death.
