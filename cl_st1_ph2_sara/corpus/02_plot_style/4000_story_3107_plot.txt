Publishers Sloper and Dodge face ruin when Australians violently drive away their book canvassers, leaving the firm unable to sell a costly new atlas. An inventor offers them a mechanical, cast-iron canvasser that can deliver an enormous sales pitch, withstand dogs and beatings, retaliate with a devastating punch, and automatically collect signed orders.

They test the automaton in the lethargic town of Ninemile, first sending it to Macpherson, a huge land agent who despises canvassers. Macpherson’s dog bites off part of its tar-padded leg, and when Macpherson strikes it, the machine knocks him unconscious. Its handlers flee when a police sergeant, O’Grady, arrives.

Mistaking the machine first for a drunk and then recognizing it as a canvasser, O’Grady tries to lead it to jail. His touch activates its arm, which traps him in a crushing embrace while it marches toward the river, endlessly shouting its sales pitch. Constable Dooley attempts a rescue but is knocked senseless by the machine. Still clutching the terrified sergeant, the automaton falls into Ninemile Creek, drowning them both.

The incident is never properly reported and fades into local legend. The inventor later drinks himself to death, while Dooley advances in the police, Sloper becomes a minister, and Dodge enters Parliament.
