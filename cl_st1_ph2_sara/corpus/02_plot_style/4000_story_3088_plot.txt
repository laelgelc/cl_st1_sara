Bunt McBride recounts joining Hardenberg, Strokher, and Ally Bazan on a mission to smuggle rifles to Barreto Palachi’s revolutionaries in Guatemala. Their instructions come through Palachi’s supposed female agent, the beautiful Esperanza Ulivarri. Hardenberg and Strokher both become infatuated with her, and their rivalry escalates during the voyage south. They agree to settle who may court her through a bare-knuckle fight, but after two brutal hours neither can defeat the other, and the contest is declared a draw.

At San Diego, the crew expects to collect Palachi’s male representative, identified by half of a torn calling card. Instead, Esperanza appears. The battered rivals prepare to explain that their injuries were honorably suffered for her sake, only for “Esperanza” to remove the disguise and reveal himself as Barreto Palachi, who had posed as a woman to evade the police. Humiliated, Hardenberg and Strokher immediately abandon the schooner and row ashore. Bunt later meets them again but, fearing their fists and guns, never mentions the incident.
