Tommy Puffer, a poor and mischievous boy disliked by the respectable townspeople, secretly loves flowers and is deeply devoted to Bessie, a kind, disabled girl who treats him with affection. When Tommy shows Bessie an exquisite blue-silk doll in a shop window, she becomes enchanted with it and names it Roselle Geraldine. After the doll is sold, Bessie grows despondent and ill, leaving Tommy guilt-ridden because he cannot afford to replace it.

One night, although he resents the harsh and judgmental Miss Octavia, Tommy learns that frost threatens her cherished garden. Out of love for the flowers, he covers the plants with newspapers and saves them. Discovering his kindness, Miss Octavia regrets misjudging him and offers him a reward. Tommy asks only for a doll for Bessie. By coincidence, Miss Octavia had purchased Roselle Geraldine herself, and she gives it to Tommy. The gift restores Bessie's happiness and health.

Recognizing Tommy’s good character and passion for flowers, Miss Octavia then arranges for him to work and learn the florist trade with her brother. Tommy gains a promising future while remaining close to Bessie, who assures him that, much as she loves her doll, she loves him more.
