Reclusive widow Mercy Crane receives Sarah Ellen Dow, a hardworking nurse who reports that their neighbor, Sister Barsett, has died after a long history of illnesses. Hurt that Barsett’s sisters displaced her during the final hours, Sarah Ellen recounts the household’s quarrels, Barsett’s faults, and her own conflicted grief. Mercy comforts her with tea and shortcake, while the women reminisce, gossip, and discuss Sarah Ellen’s uncertain future.

As Sarah Ellen prepares to return and help with the funeral arrangements, a neighbor arrives with startling news: Sister Barsett was not dead but merely suffering another of her “spells.” She has revived, driven everyone else away, demanded a hearty supper, and called urgently for Sarah Ellen. Rested by Mercy’s hospitality, Sarah Ellen solemnly returns to her patient, while Mercy remains behind laughing at the absurd reversal.
