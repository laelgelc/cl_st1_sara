A six-year-old girl named Nadya grows mysteriously weak and indifferent to life. Doctors tell her distraught parents that medicine cannot help as much as fulfilling a desire that might restore her interest and happiness. After rejecting toys, sweets, dolls, and visitors, Nadya asks for a real, living elephant.

Her father first brings her a mechanical toy elephant, but she explains that she wants a live one. Desperate to save her, he visits a menagerie and persuades its German manager to bring the trained elephant Tommy to their home. After making special arrangements and widening the entrance, they lead Tommy upstairs with pieces of pistachio tart and settle him in the dining room overnight.

The next morning, Nadya eagerly eats and dresses so she can meet Tommy. She is delighted by the gentle elephant, feeds him rolls, introduces him to her dolls, shows him a picture book, plays with him, and shares a meal with him. Her laughter and enthusiasm return, and she eventually falls asleep beside him.

Tommy is taken back to the menagerie during the night. Nadya awakens the next morning energetic, hungry, and healthy. When told that Tommy has gone home but hopes she will visit once she recovers, she declares that she is already well.
