Ribby, a cat, invites Duchess, a dog, to tea and promises her a mouse-and-bacon pie. Duchess hates the thought of eating mouse, so she secretly brings her own veal-and-ham pie to Ribby’s house, intending to exchange the pies while Ribby is out. Unable to find Ribby’s pie, which is baking in the lower oven, Duchess places her own in the upper oven and leaves.

At tea, Duchess mistakenly assumes Ribby is serving the substituted pie and eats several portions. When she cannot find the patty-pan she had baked inside her own pie, she panics, believing she has swallowed it, and Ribby fetches a doctor. Left alone, Duchess discovers her untouched pie in the upper oven and realizes she has actually eaten Ribby’s mouse pie. Embarrassed, she hides her pie in the backyard and says nothing.

After Duchess leaves, she returns to retrieve it but finds the doctor and some jackdaws eating it. Ribby later discovers the broken pie dish and patty-pan in her yard. Bewildered by the mystery, she decides that next time she gives a party, she will invite her cousin instead.
