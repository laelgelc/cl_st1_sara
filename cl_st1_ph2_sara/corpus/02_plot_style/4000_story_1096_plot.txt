Jack Turnbull and Tom Donahue, former law students who emigrated to South Africa, remain poor after years of failed ventures. Their friend Dick Wharton tells them of a supposedly haunted valley where he saw a mysterious red light glowing from a cliff. Recalling an account of a luminous diamond, Tom suspects the “demon’s eye” is actually a precious stone.

Tom and Jack travel to the valley at night, locate the light, and use a makeshift sighting device to mark its exact position. At dawn they extract what they believe is a huge diamond, only for a farmer to identify it as worthless rock salt. Tom then realizes they removed the wrong crystal and returns with Jack to the cliff. Although the true gem appears to be missing, Jack notices that a small rock knob they had used as a fulcrum has also disappeared. Searching below, they find the broken knob and discover a brilliant diamond embedded in it.

The jewel makes both men wealthy, earning Tom his reputation for extraordinary luck. They also help Dick establish a business, while Jack prospers as a farmer in Africa.
