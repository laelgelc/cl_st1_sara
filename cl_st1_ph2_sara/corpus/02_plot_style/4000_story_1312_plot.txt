Three impoverished laborers—Semka, Mishka, and the narrator—survive on irregular jobs while living together in a ruined building. Hired by a stern, devout old woman to demolish a bathhouse and clean a well, they pause while she reads Scripture to them. Mishka listens reverently, but Semka secretly steals the Bible’s silver clasps.

After they are paid, Semka reveals the theft and sells the clasps to Mishka. Troubled that the sacred book has been damaged and that the old woman will be hurt, Mishka returns them at his own expense. She recognizes his goodness but subjects him to moral instruction and more Bible reading until, hungry and exhausted, he begs to leave. The three friends later reunite at an inn, where Mishka humorously complains that his honest act brought him only an ordeal, and they drink to the affair’s conclusion.
