Ed Loyce discovers a mutilated corpse hanging from a lamppost in the center of Pikeville, yet everyone else treats it as normal. Because he spent the day working in his basement, he alone escaped the mind control imposed on the town by giant insectlike beings that disguise themselves as humans and operate from City Hall. After escaping two impostors posing as police officers, he witnesses the creatures entering the town, realizes the population has been enslaved, and flees. In his panic, he kills a man who may also have escaped their control, then returns home only to discover that his family has been compromised and one son replaced by an insect mimic.

Loyce escapes on foot to nearby Oak Grove and reports the invasion to its authorities. He theorizes that the creatures have infiltrated humanity for thousands of years, taking over communities from the top down. The local commissioner appears to believe him but reveals that the hanging corpse was bait meant to identify anyone who had escaped control. Oak Grove has already been taken over as well, and Loyce is executed and hung publicly as the next lure. A bank employee who had been isolated in a vault all day notices Loyce’s body while everyone else ignores it, beginning the cycle again.
