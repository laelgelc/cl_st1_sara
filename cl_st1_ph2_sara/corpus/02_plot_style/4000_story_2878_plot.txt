After a hurricane leaves many lumber-laden vessels drifting in the Atlantic, the Philadelphia tug Atlas sets out to recover the abandoned brig Neptune and earn salvage money. Captain James calculates the wreck’s course through the Gulf Stream and quickly locates it. Two crewmen, Joe Miller and Sharkey, remain aboard to steer while the Atlas begins towing the battered, waterlogged brig toward Boston.

A fierce gale strains the towline until it breaks, leaving the Neptune and the two men adrift in the darkness. The Atlas is unable to pursue safely, and after the storm Captain James searches for ten days without finding the wreck. With coal running low, he reluctantly returns to Philadelphia.

There he discovers that Miller and Sharkey are already safe. A fruit steamer found the Neptune the morning after it broke loose, used the tug’s still-attached hawser to tow it into Boston, and delivered the men and wreck safely. Because the Atlas’s crew had remained aboard, the brig was legally still in the tug’s possession. The fruit steamer receives a quarter of the salvage award, while the Atlas receives the remainder. The cargo is sold, and the badly damaged brig is converted into a harbor lighter.
