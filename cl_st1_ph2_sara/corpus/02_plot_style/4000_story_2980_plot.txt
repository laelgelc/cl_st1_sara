A suburban grocer worries that his small shop cannot compete with larger stores offering entertainment and other attractions. An artist lodging above the shop proposes exploiting the public’s love of mystery instead.

The artist and his sister stage an exotic intrigue inside the shop. A dark-skinned young model repeatedly buys unusual goods such as pomegranates and quail seed, while a theatrically bearded “foreigner” searches for him. The grocer falsely denies that the boy has visited, and the boy hides whenever the stranger appears. A veiled woman further heightens the drama by hinting where the boy is concealed.

Local shoppers become fascinated, inventing theories involving crime, romance, drugs, and international conspiracies. They crowd the store, linger to watch developments, and buy unusually large quantities of groceries while waiting for the mysterious figures to appear. The performance culminates in a tense encounter that sends the bearded man fleeing in apparent terror.

Afterward, the grocer gratefully thanks the artist and his sister: their fabricated mystery has transformed his shop into a popular destination and cleared his Christmas stock. The artist reveals that the mysterious boy was his model, while the grocer insists on at least paying for the hired black beard.
