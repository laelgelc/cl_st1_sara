Business partners William Strong and John Danby become bitter enemies, allowing their feud to bankrupt their successful paper-collar company. Strong emigrates to Texas and becomes a cowboy, but later discovers that Danby is still selling their collars under the old firm’s name. Learning that Danby will cross the prairie alone, Strong ambushes him, disarms him, and shoots him in the knee and eye.

To disguise the murder as a fair gunfight, Strong places a fired revolver in the apparently dead Danby’s hand. Danby is still alive, however, and with his last strength shoots Strong dead, bringing their partnership and feud to a final, fatal end.
