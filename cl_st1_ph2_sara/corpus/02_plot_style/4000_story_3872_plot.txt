At an old private club, Clayton tells his friends that the previous night he encountered a timid, ineffectual ghost who had come to haunt the building but had forgotten the ritual gestures needed to return to the spirit world. After hearing the ghost’s unhappy life story, Clayton helped him rehearse the complicated passes until the ghost finally performed them correctly and vanished.

Clayton demonstrates the gestures to his skeptical companions. Sanderson recognizes them as related to esoteric Masonic practices and supplies a missing twist, enabling Clayton to complete the ritual accurately. Despite Wish’s fearful warnings, Clayton performs the full sequence. At the final gesture, he suddenly freezes, collapses, and dies. Whether the ritual truly carried him into the spirit world or he merely suffered a fatal seizure remains unresolved.
