Billy Malowney, a charming but penniless young Irishman, falls in love with Molly Donovan. Her parents oppose the match because Molly has a substantial dowry and Billy owns almost nothing. After quarreling with Molly at a wake, Billy gets drunk and is tricked by a recruiting sergeant into joining the army. He is sent abroad to fight Napoleon’s forces.

Reports of Billy’s extraordinary bravery eventually give way to news that he has been killed in battle. Molly refuses other suitors and mourns him for years. In reality, Billy was badly wounded and left for dead but survived, received a military pension, and returned home after seven years.

Billy reunites with Molly while her family is away at a fair. When her parents unexpectedly return, she hides him in a bedroom closet. He falls asleep and snores, convincing her parents that Billy’s ghost has come to punish them. The resulting panic spreads through the household, even terrifying the visiting priest, until Billy finally explains that he is alive.

The priest persuades Molly’s parents not to resist the combined forces of love and providence. Once they learn that Billy receives a lifelong pension, they approve the marriage. Billy and Molly celebrate with a famously riotous wedding and go on to have a large family.
