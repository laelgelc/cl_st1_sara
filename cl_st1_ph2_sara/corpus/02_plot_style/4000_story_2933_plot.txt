Five treasure hunters swear that any member who deceives the others must demand to be set adrift with only one oar. When Rossiter is caught cheating at cards, he honors the compact and is abandoned in a small boat far from land.

After four days without food or water, delirious and tormented by a shark trailing him, Rossiter reflects on his wasted life, broken promises, and desire to die an honorable man. He challenges the shark to a final, fair gamble, tossing a folded visiting card to decide which of them will eat the other. The shark wins.

At that moment, a steamer arrives to rescue Rossiter. Despite the crew’s warnings and pleas, he refuses salvation, declaring that an honest man must pay his debt of honor, and leaps into the sea.
