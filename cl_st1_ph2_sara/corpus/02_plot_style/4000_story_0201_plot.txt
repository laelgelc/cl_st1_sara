Two American sisters visiting London leave a late evening gathering and reluctantly hire a hansom cab during a dense fog. Their driver, unfamiliar with the city and apparently drunk or exhausted, cannot find their Bayswater address. Despite asking a policeman for directions, he continues wandering through deserted streets as midnight approaches, leaving the women increasingly anxious, bedraggled, and eventually amused by the absurdity of their predicament.

They finally appeal to a respectable older gentleman arriving home, who places them in a reliable four-wheeled cab and pays off their first driver. Yet the fog confounds the second cabman as well. After mistaking Colville Square for Colville Gardens and driving around until his horse is exhausted, he stops near a lighted house. The narrator rings the bell, prepared to seek shelter, only to discover that they have accidentally arrived at their own lodging. Safely home, the sisters entertain the other boarders with their adventure and resolve never again to travel by hansom or become lost in a London fog.
