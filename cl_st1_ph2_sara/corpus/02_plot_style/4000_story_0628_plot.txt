Mary Jane Dement, a wealthy heiress ashamed of her impoverished childhood in Redhorse, becomes fascinated by the handsome, sophisticated Dr. Barritz. Encouraged by her childhood friend Jack Raynor’s stories that Barritz may possess mysterious powers learned in India, she falls deeply in love with him but fears he will reject her if he learns about her humble past and her childhood nickname, “Gunny.”

During a private encounter on the beach, Barritz declares that he has loved her for years. He then reveals that he is actually “Dumps,” the destitute boy who once belonged to Mary Jane and Jack’s childhood trio and was later believed to have been killed by road agents. He survived, traveled widely, and returned transformed into Dr. Barritz after Jack directed him to her. Mary Jane joyfully accepts him, while learning that his supposed connection to black magic was merely Jack’s invention to spark her curiosity.
