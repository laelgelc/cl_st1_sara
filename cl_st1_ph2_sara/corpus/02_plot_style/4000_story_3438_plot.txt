Sam Lawson tells two boys about a local attempt to recover Captain Kidd’s buried treasure. According to legend, a marked rock by the river conceals a fortune guarded by the spirits of people Kidd murdered. Impoverished Mother Hokum urges her husband to organize a dig with Toddy Whitney, Harry Wiggin, and Primus King, an expert laborer who wisely demands payment in advance. Sam accompanies them but refuses a share, believing the treasure is cursed.

At midnight under a full moon, Primus digs beneath the rock while Sam warns everyone that they must remain silent if they find the treasure or it will disappear. The men uncover what seems to be a large iron pot and begin hauling it from the hole. Just as it reaches the surface, Toddy excitedly announces that they have it. The rope snaps, the pot apparently sinks deep into the earth, and a terrifying laugh is heard.

Sam admits that he slept through the crucial event and saw none of it himself. He awakens to find Hokum fighting Toddy for ruining their chance, while Primus laughs because he has already secured his payment. The disappointed men fill in the hole, and Sam concludes that losing the treasure spared them from its curse, though he cannot say what—if anything—the pot contained.
