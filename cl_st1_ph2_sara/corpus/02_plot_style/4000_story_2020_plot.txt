After being orphaned, twelve-year-old Stephen Elliott is taken in by his reclusive cousin, Mr Abney, an expert in ancient pagan beliefs. At Aswarby Hall, Stephen learns that Abney had previously sheltered two destitute children—a gypsy girl and an Italian boy—both of whom mysteriously disappeared.

Stephen begins experiencing supernatural signs: he dreams of a corpse hidden in a disused bathroom, finds claw-like scratches on his nightclothes and bedroom door, and hears of voices in the wine cellar. On the spring equinox, Abney secretly summons him to the study late at night. Before going, Stephen sees the ghosts of the missing children, each bearing a wound over the heart.

When Stephen reaches the study, he finds Abney dead, his chest violently torn open. Abney’s papers reveal that he murdered the two children, removed and consumed their hearts, and planned to do the same to Stephen in an occult ritual intended to grant supernatural powers and immortality. The children’s vengeful spirits have returned to kill Abney before he can complete the ritual.
