After a missionary’s execution, a group of Indigenous warriors prepares to torture a captured corporal before attacking the remaining settlers. The defiant soldier refuses to beg for mercy and, seizing The Weasel’s tomahawk, kills him and wounds several others before being restrained. Impressed by his courage but determined to avenge the slain chief, the warriors sentence him to be torn between two bent saplings.

Hoping to provoke a quick death instead, the corporal insults his captors and taunts them over their defeats by white forces, but their leaders prevent anyone from killing him prematurely. He is bound by the wrists to the saplings, which spring apart when their fastenings are cut. At that instant, a hidden ally fires from the nearby thicket, shooting the corporal through the head and mercifully sparing him the prolonged torture.
