Semen Ivanov, an impoverished and sickly war veteran, becomes a railroad trackman and settles with his wife in an isolated watch-house. He befriends a neighboring trackman, Vasili, an angry man who resents the railway administration’s exploitation and petty tyranny. After a division master strikes Vasili for making a complaint, Vasili leaves, claiming he will seek justice.

Days later, Semen discovers Vasili prying a rail loose just before a crowded passenger train is due. Unable to repair the track or retrieve his signal flag in time, Semen cuts his own arm, soaks a handkerchief in his blood, and waves it from a stick as an emergency red flag. Weak from blood loss, he collapses as the train approaches, but Vasili reappears, catches the falling flag, and holds it up until the engineer stops the train. Confronted with Semen’s sacrifice and the passengers whose lives were nearly lost, Vasili confesses that he sabotaged the rail and asks to be arrested.
