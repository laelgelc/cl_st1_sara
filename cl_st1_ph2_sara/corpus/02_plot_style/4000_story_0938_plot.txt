Patsy Tulligan, a fearless but foolish Cherry Street tough, gets drunk with two friends in an unfamiliar saloon. He casually insults a young Cuban, who responds by demanding satisfaction in a sword duel. Though Patsy has never fenced and is warned that the skilled Cuban would quickly kill him, pride and courage compel him to accept.

As the two prepare to leave for the Cuban’s hotel to obtain weapons, the bartender returns with a policeman. The Cuban arrogantly tells the officer that the dispute is none of his business and is dragged outside. The duel never occurs, leaving Patsy to boast that he would have given the Cuban a tremendous fight.
