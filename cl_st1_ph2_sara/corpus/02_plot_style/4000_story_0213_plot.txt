Lily carries her mother’s large red umbrella to school and is swept away by a strong wind into a magical land made entirely of candy. At first she delights in eating everything and playing with the sugar people, but overindulgence makes her ill-tempered and destructive. After biting off the candy king’s head, she flees across a desert into Cake-land.

There she meets Ginger Snap, a hardworking gingerbread man who teaches her to bake and explains that the cake people strive to earn yeast, transform into bread, and enter the healthier, more purposeful Bread-land. When Ginger Snap earns his reward and becomes Muffin, Lily follows him there.

In Bread-land, Lily discovers a wholesome society devoted to farming, studying grain and yeast, and making nourishing food. She befriends Johnny Cake and Sally Lunn, attends school, and learns to bake excellent bread. Muffin tells her that bread achieves its highest purpose by nourishing good people and helping them contribute to the world.

After Lily makes a perfect loaf, she decides to return home and share it with her mother. Muffin becomes part of the loaf so that he can nourish her. Lily wishes herself home, where she becomes a skilled housekeeper and bread maker. By choosing wholesome food over excessive sweets, she grows from a sickly, fretful child into a strong and capable woman.
