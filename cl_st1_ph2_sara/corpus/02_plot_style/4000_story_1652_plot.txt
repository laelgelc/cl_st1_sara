On Christmas Eve, Della has only $1.87 to buy a gift for her husband, Jim. Though the young couple is poor, each possesses one prized treasure: Della’s long, beautiful hair and Jim’s inherited gold watch. Determined to give Jim something worthy of him, Della sells her hair for twenty dollars and uses the money to buy a fine platinum chain for his watch.

When Jim comes home, he is stunned by Della’s short hair. He then gives her his Christmas present: an expensive set of jeweled combs she had long admired. Della is moved but cannot use them until her hair grows back. She eagerly presents Jim with the watch chain, only to learn that he sold his watch to buy her combs. Although both gifts are temporarily useless, their mutual sacrifices reveal the depth of their love and make them truly wise gift-givers.
