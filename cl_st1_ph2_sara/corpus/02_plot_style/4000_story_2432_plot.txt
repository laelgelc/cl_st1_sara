Boyd and Minnie Duncan celebrate Christmas aboard their yacht while sailing through the South Pacific. Their captain, Dettmar, becomes drunk and confronts Boyd, who reveals that he knows Dettmar has been stealing from him but had retained him to give him a chance to rebuild his life after a murder conviction and pardon. Dettmar then admits that he was in fact guilty of the murder, prompting Boyd to announce that he will dismiss him at their next port.

That night, Dettmar throws Minnie overboard. Boyd dives in to rescue her, unaware that the captain deliberately intends to abandon them. Clinging to a life buoy, the couple watches the yacht sail away and spends Christmas Day drifting under the tropical sun, nearly dying from exposure and thirst. Against overwhelming odds, a passing American cruiser spots and rescues them.

After reaching Attu-Attu, Boyd reports the attempted murders to the U.S. consul. The yacht soon arrives, and Dettmar comes ashore pretending that the Duncans died accidentally despite his exhaustive search. Boyd emerges from hiding, exposes him, and severely beats him with his fists before turning him over to the consul and the law.
