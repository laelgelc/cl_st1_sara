After Peyton Marshall’s death, his library is found ransacked and his will missing. Anthony Gosford, an Englishman who had gained Marshall’s confidence, claims the will left him a large sum. Suspicion falls on Marshall’s young son, who admits staging the burglary and hiding the document because it apparently gives Gosford the entire estate, contrary to his father’s promise to leave him a substantial inheritance.

The recovered will grants Gosford fifty thousand dollars, but the son insists the original bequest was only thirty thousand. An investigator examines the document through a magnifying crystal and discovers that the figure three was altered into a five. Gosford claims Marshall made the correction himself, but Doctor Gaeki tests the inks with acid. The original writing reacts differently from the added stroke, proving Gosford forged the alteration. Gosford is exposed as a fraud who intended to steal the estate, vindicating both Marshall and his son.
