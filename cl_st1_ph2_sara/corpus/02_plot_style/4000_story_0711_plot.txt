An impoverished New York reporter lives in a cramped boarding-house room with two equally destitute friends. One evening, while they are out seeking food and work, he struggles to write a story but drifts into a hungry, exhausted sleep.

He dreams that a mysterious, sinister man arrives with ten thousand dollars from an anonymous benefactor and asks for a receipt. Suspicious, the reporter decides to summon an elderly neighbor as a witness. He then notices blood on the visitor’s face and finds the neighbor upstairs with his throat cut, surrounded by new dollar bills. Returning to his room, he discovers that the stranger has vanished, leaving bloodstained gloves, a butcher’s knife, and scattered money. The reporter realizes the stranger has smeared blood on the door handle so that it transfers to his hands and face, framing him for the murder.

Police arrive, led by the mysterious man, and seize the apparently red-handed reporter. At that instant, he awakens to find his roommates shaking him awake. They have returned with supper and good news, revealing that the entire crime and conspiracy were a nightmare brought on by hunger, fatigue, and anxiety.
