A highly skilled mall salesman known as IceKing targets a family shopping for winter coats, expertly reading and manipulating each member to steer them toward an expensive purchase. Proud of his national sales ranking, he sees selling as his sole source of status and views Florence, a talented coworker he once mentored and secretly helped keep her job, as his chief rival.

Just as IceKing convinces the father to buy a costly coat, Florence arrives wearing a newly promoted assistant manager badge. She takes over the sale, adds another jacket, and receives all the credit while IceKing is sent on break. The family quickly forgets his help, except for the young daughter, who smiles at him. When the district manager enters and looks between IceKing and Florence, IceKing realizes that his reputation and privileged position may be shifting to her.
