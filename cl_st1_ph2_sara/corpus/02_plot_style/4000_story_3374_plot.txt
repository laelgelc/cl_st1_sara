Hockey star Carl Hemmer leads undefeated Taber High with fearless solo attacks, but a brutal illegal check by Howard Prep leaves him unconscious with a concussion. Though he soon returns physically healthy, he develops an instinctive fear of collisions. His hesitation nearly costs Taber an easy game and becomes a serious liability before its decisive rivalry match against Siddall High and its star, Whiz Deagen.

During the showdown, Carl repeatedly avoids Siddall’s defense while Whiz scores, giving Siddall a 1–0 lead. Carl confesses his fear to his teammate Lank Broderick and asks Lank to break through the defense for him. Lank does so at great personal risk, clearing a path and passing the puck back so Carl can tie the game, but suffers a sprained knee and head injury in the collision.

Realizing that Lank was hurt doing what Carl should have done, Carl regains his courage. He battles Whiz aggressively, prevents a likely goal, and finally charges through Whiz and Siddall’s defense without flinching. While sliding toward the net, he knocks in the winning goal just as time expires. Taber completes an undefeated season, and Carl credits Lank’s sacrifice with giving him the emotional jolt he needed to overcome his fear.
