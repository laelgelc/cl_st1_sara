While riding night watch over a restless cattle herd in Idaho, the narrator listens as the veteran cowboy Bunt recounts the fate of Cock-eye Blacklock, a notorious gambler, thief, and killer who repeatedly evaded justice.

After being driven from a California mining camp, Blacklock secretly earns money by using dynamite to kill trout in a protected river. The mine boss, the marshal, and Bunt suspect him but repeatedly fail to catch him. Eventually they hide near the river and witness Blacklock throw a lit stick of dynamite into a pool.

A playful camp dog named Sloppy Weather, trained to retrieve thrown sticks, swims after the explosive and carries it back toward Blacklock. Terrified, Blacklock runs, but the dog chases him, believing they are playing. The dynamite explodes, killing them both and leaving a crater in which Blacklock’s remains are buried. Bunt finishes his story at sunrise, and he and the narrator return to camp as the herd begins to stir.
