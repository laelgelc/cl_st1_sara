An elderly farm couple remain deeply devoted after a lifetime of hardship, loss, and shared labor. The grandmother treasures a topaz-and-pearl pin her husband bought through sacrifice when they were young; it becomes a symbol of his love and of the baby they lost. Years later, after the pin briefly disappears and is recovered from a bird’s nest, her failing eyesight turns to complete blindness. A doctor confirms that nothing can restore her sight. Though her husband is devastated, she accepts her condition through faith, believing comfort and strength can be miracles in themselves. Their son Martin moves his family into the farmhouse, and the grandchildren become her eyes, companions, and helpers. Surrounded by their affection, she finds renewed happiness, while her presence enriches the children’s lives and makes her the beloved spiritual center of the household.
