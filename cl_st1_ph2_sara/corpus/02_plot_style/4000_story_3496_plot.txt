Hemanta’s wife, Kusum, has long concealed that she is a Kayestha orphan and child-widow who was falsely presented as a Brahmin before their marriage. When Hemanta’s orthodox father, Harihar Mukerji, discovers the truth, he orders Hemanta to expel her.

Hemanta learns that Peari Sankar Ghosal engineered the deception as revenge against Harihar, who had once ostracized Peari Sankar for accepting his disgraced son-in-law and later ruined his nephew’s marriage prospects. Knowing that Hemanta and Kusum loved each other, Peari Sankar arranged their marriage by passing Kusum off as a Brahmin maiden. Years later, he exposed her identity to sabotage the marriage of Hemanta’s sister and complete his vengeance.

Though initially devastated, Hemanta refuses to abandon Kusum. When Harihar threatens to renounce him and strip him of caste, Hemanta calmly declares that he does not care about caste and chooses his wife instead.
