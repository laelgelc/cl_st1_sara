In the slow, close-knit factory village of Leyden, Maria Brewster and David Emmons remain devoted sweethearts for decades. David, notoriously hesitant in everything, visits Maria every Sunday but never manages to propose, while Maria patiently cares for him by bringing food, cleaning his cottage, and mending his clothes. Though she once bought and made up a pearl-colored wedding dress in anticipation, she eventually gives it away when old age convinces her they will never marry.

As David grows frail, Maria’s concern for his lonely life deepens. One Sunday he apologizes vaguely for all the things his slowness has kept him from doing, and she assures him she holds nothing against him. The next morning, summoned urgently to his sickbed, Maria arrives as he is dying. With his last strength, David finally tells her that he had always meant to ask her to marry him.
