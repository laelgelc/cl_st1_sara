An elite retail salesman works a brutal Black Friday in a mall where consumer frenzy turns shoppers into violent, barely human creatures. Because he was bitten during a previous sale, he can understand their incoherent demands and uses this ability to achieve record sales while fending them off and witnessing numerous injuries and deaths. He hopes to win the store’s sales contest and give his unemployed mother an expensive parka as proof of his love.

A new employee, Duo, gains the same ability after being bitten but quits, unable to accept the suffering around him. He tells the salesman that a coat cannot prove his love and that his mother already knows he cares. Soon afterward, the salesman sacrifices the only parka in his mother’s size to satisfy a frenzied shopper and save his manager from being attacked. Though his prize is gone and the horror of the event is clear, he returns to his section, embraces his role, and welcomes another stampeding crowd with practiced enthusiasm.
