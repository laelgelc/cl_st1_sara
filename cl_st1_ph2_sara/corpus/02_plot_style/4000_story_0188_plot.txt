Ten-year-old Effie, spoiled by many repetitive and extravagant Christmases, complains that the holiday no longer excites her and even imagines poor children must be happier than rich ones. After reading *A Christmas Carol*, she dreams she is a cold, hungry beggar lost in the snow. A childlike Christmas spirit rescues her and takes her through a magical world where spirits prepare gifts, encourage generosity, and bring comfort to the needy. He then shows her impoverished families whose Christmases are transformed by charitable people, teaching her that she can create the same happiness by helping others.

Inspired by Effie’s dream, her mother secretly arranges a celebration for twenty-four orphan girls. Effie is dressed as the Christmas spirit and placed among a grove of decorated trees, each laden with food, clothing, toys, and some of her own donated possessions. She distributes treats and watches the orphans joyfully receive their individual trees and presents. A lame girl named Katy, noticing that Effie received nothing, gives her a small doll in gratitude. Effie realizes that bringing happiness to others is the finest Christmas gift: the celebration becomes her best Christmas, Katy’s doll becomes her cherished keepsake, and she retains a lifelong love of the holiday expressed through charitable deeds.
