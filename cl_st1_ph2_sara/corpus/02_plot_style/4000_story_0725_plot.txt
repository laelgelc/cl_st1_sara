A lonely, overworked English chemist vacationing on the Italian Riviera becomes intrigued when an olive dropped by a young woman rolls across the hotel dining room to him. Captivated by her spontaneous smile, he keeps the olive as a token.

Before dawn, the woman mysteriously summons him from his room and leads him into the surrounding olive groves. There they abandon themselves to passion and join a procession of nymphs, fauns, and satyrs bound for an earthquake-ruined village. In its shattered church, the revelers worship a powerful pagan god whose appearance shakes the earth and sends them fleeing. As the woman promises that they will remember one another, the man awakens in his hotel bed, uncertain whether the adventure was real or a dream.

The next day he meets the woman formally. Although she denies having shared his dream, her words and gestures echo it. Within days they fall in love, and when he asks to keep the olive, she confirms that she recognized in him someone already partly attuned to her wild, pagan world—and soon to belong to it altogether.
