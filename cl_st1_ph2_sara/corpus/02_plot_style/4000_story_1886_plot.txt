A sailing-barge captain and his mate begin a voyage while locked in a bitter feud. Provoked by the captain’s abuse, the mate strikes him with a handspike and knocks him overboard. The captain survives by climbing into the trailing boat, but refuses to forgive the attack and vows to hand the mate over to the police. After enduring the night in the boat, he forces his way back aboard, preserves the incriminating weapon, and repeatedly reminds the resigned mate of his impending punishment.

The next day they rescue an apparently drowned sailor. Working together, they anchor the barge, warm and revive the man, and then take turns nursing him as they continue toward port. Their shared effort softens the captain despite his continued threats. Once the barge is safely berthed, he orders the mate below to care for the survivor, tells him to claim that the captain’s injuries came from a fight ashore, and leaves without reporting him, silently granting him mercy.
