Old Mother Magpie constantly meddles in the other forest creatures’ affairs while neglecting her own. She overwhelms Mrs. Oriole with unwanted advice about her nest and marriage, then misrepresents their conversation to Tommy Oriole. The resulting quarrel causes Tommy to spend even less time at home.

Magpie next tells the elderly music teacher Dr. Bullfrog that the forest parish intends to replace him because his style is outdated. She persuades him to leave his comfortable waterside home, live on land, and imitate fashionable bird singing. His attempts make him a laughingstock. After a gardener nearly strikes him and a dog chases him, Bullfrog flees back to the river, permanently dispirited and bitterly referring to Magpie as “Old Mother Mischief.”
