Croly Addicks, a timid, underpaid piano-company clerk mocked for his weak chin, endures constant bullying from his domineering supervisor, Cowdin. On his thirtieth birthday, he is falsely accused of cheating at lunch and slighted at a soda fountain, reinforcing his belief that his appearance makes everyone treat him as weak. He hopes an imminent promotion will raise his salary enough to marry Emily, who cares for him but doubts they can live on his current wages. Although Croly knows the business thoroughly and deserves advancement, his lifelong ridicule has left him unable to assert himself. Returning to work, he anxiously approaches the bulletin board to discover whether he has received the promotion.
