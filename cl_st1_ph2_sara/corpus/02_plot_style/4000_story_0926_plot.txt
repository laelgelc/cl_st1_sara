A lonely child befriends a small stray dog but initially treats him cruelly, striking him even as the submissive animal responds with humility and affection. The dog persistently follows the child home, where the child comes to value him and persuades his hostile family to let him stay.

The child and dog become devoted companions in a violent, impoverished household. Other family members abuse and neglect the dog whenever the child is absent, while the child fiercely defends him, though he too sometimes beats him without cause. Despite everything, the dog remains completely loyal, comforting the child in distress and joyfully accompanying him on neighborhood expeditions.

One day, the child and dog return home to find the father exceptionally drunk and destructive. The child hides, but the unsuspecting dog approaches him as if to play. The father beats the dog with a coffee pot, then grabs him by a leg and throws him through a fifth-story window. The dog falls onto a shed and then into an alley. After slowly making his way downstairs, the grieving child is found sitting beside his dead companion.
