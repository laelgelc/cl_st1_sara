Two teenage gang members, Danny and Tigo, are chosen to settle a dispute between their rival clubs by playing Russian roulette in a basement. Though their jackets mark them as enemies, neither has any personal grievance against the other.

As they take turns firing the revolver at their heads, they gradually discover shared interests, fears, family frustrations, and doubts about gang loyalty. Their guarded hostility gives way to trust and friendship, even as they add more bullets and increase the danger. They finally decide to make one last spin, defy their clubs, and go boating together with their girlfriends on Sunday.

Danny raises the gun and toasts their new plans, but the chamber fires, killing him instantly. Horrified, Tigo collapses over the table and weeps for the friend he has just found and lost.
