Left alone while her mother shops and the maid works, Jane Gladys receives a strange book from a disgruntled salesman seeking revenge because her father had thrown him out of his office. When she opens the book, its illustrated characters spring to life: a clown with no back, a talking monkey, a lopsided donkey, a toothless leopard, and finally a dangerous grizzly bear.

The bear announces that, according to the book, he must eat little girls. As he approaches, Jane Gladys thinks quickly and argues that because the book was given to her and bears her name, she owns everything in it—including the bear—so he cannot eat his owner. The clown confirms her claim, and the bear reluctantly relents. When the doorbell signals her mother’s return, all the characters rush back into the book, leaving Jane Gladys safe.
