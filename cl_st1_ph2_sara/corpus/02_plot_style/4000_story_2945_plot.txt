When her husband returns to their California ranch, Violante notices that the vaquero Basilio has seen him give her a large bag of gold. After the men ride out again, Basilio secretly climbs toward her upstairs window with a dagger, intending to rob and kill her. Violante calmly obtains a pistol from her maid, Alice, holds Basilio at gunpoint on the vine, and sends Alice to summon her husband.

Basilio becomes trapped in an agonizing position but cannot move without risking being shot. He finally drops his dagger and begs Violante for mercy. The prolonged ordeal overwhelms her, and she faints when her husband arrives. Concerned for his wife and moved by Basilio’s remorse, her husband spares the robber. Basilio nevertheless insists on physical punishment, so a vaquero lashes him bare-chested to a horse and releases it into the corral.

The frightened horses stampede, and Basilio’s mount breaks through the fence into an apiary. As the horse destroys the hives, swarms of bees attack both animal and helpless rider. Violante hears Basilio’s cries and rushes to save him, risking the maddened horse and the bees. She controls the horse, cuts Basilio free, and drags him to safety, but his wounds are fatal. She prays for his unshriven soul as he dies, then collapses into her husband’s arms.
