After losing a catastrophic interplanetary war, the solar system’s last dictator escapes aboard a loyal cruiser and is secretly marooned on an isolated Venusian beach. Living alone in an abandoned radar shack, he broods over his defeat, relives the destruction of his regime, and gradually descends into madness. He becomes obsessed with exterminating the antlike native kifs, admiring their totalitarian organization even as he hates their relentless numbers.

During the kifs’ seasonal mass migration, he stumbles into their advancing army and is badly bitten. Fleeing in terror, he reaches a Venusian village, where peaceful natives nurse him back to health. Mistaking their kindness for submission, he retrieves his uniform and weapon, returns proclaiming himself their supreme ruler, and murders their chief and several others. The villagers overpower him and debate how to deal with a dangerous madman without violating their taboos.

They finally bind him to a tree in the path of the marching kifs, reasoning that any harm will be done by the insects rather than by them. Unable to escape or cry out, the dictator regains enough sanity to understand that one final army is approaching. As the sound of billions of kifs draws near, he laughs in horror while awaiting death.
