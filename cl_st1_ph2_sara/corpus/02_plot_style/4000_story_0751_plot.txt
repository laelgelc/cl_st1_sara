A married couple vacationing at a summer boarding house befriend the charming Bredes, but grow suspicious when the pair give contradictory accounts of their children, home, travels, and family history. Gossip spreads among the other guests, and the innkeeper finally asks Mr. Brede to prove that he and his supposed wife are married. Offended, Brede announces their departure.

As the distressed couple leave, Mrs. Brede opens her parasol and accidentally releases hidden wedding rice. She confesses that they are newlyweds who concealed the fact to avoid being treated as a bridal couple, only to become trapped in increasingly inconsistent lies. The guests realize they have misjudged them, the innkeeper welcomes them to stay, and the two couples reconcile.
