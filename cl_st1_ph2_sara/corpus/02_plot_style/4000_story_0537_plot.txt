A wealthy but neglected little girl is smothered by servants and forbidden to run, play, or associate with other children. Unhappy and unhealthy, she is sent to the country on a doctor’s advice, but her nurse continues imposing the same restrictions. She escapes, secretly rides in a farmer’s cart, and ends up at his farmhouse, where she enjoys helping make cheese until the hard work exhausts her.

Given curds and whey for supper, she sits on a grassy mound to eat. A large black spider approaches, frightening her into spilling her food and fleeing to the farmhouse. When her nurse arrives searching for her, the girl gladly returns but insists that she be allowed to play freely or she will run away again. The nurse agrees, and the girl’s newfound freedom restores her health and happiness.
