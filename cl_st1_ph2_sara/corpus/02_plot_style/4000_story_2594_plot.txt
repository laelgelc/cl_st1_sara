During World War I, a ship’s supercargo escapes a German raider in a small boat and drifts alone across the Pacific. He awakens to find the ocean floor mysteriously raised into a vast, rotting plain covered with dead marine life. Traveling across it, he reaches a deep canyon containing a water channel and an enormous carved monolith depicting gigantic, grotesque, fishlike humanoids worshipping beneath the sea.

A colossal living creature then rises from the water, embraces the monolith, and performs an apparent act of worship. Driven mad, the narrator flees to his boat and later awakens in a San Francisco hospital after being rescued at sea. No one believes his account, and recurring visions of the creature lead him to depend on morphine. Penniless and out of the drug, he writes down his experience and prepares to kill himself. When an immense, slippery presence begins forcing its way through his door, he leaps toward the window to escape it.
