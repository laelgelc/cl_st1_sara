Ermengarde Stubbs, the supposedly sixteen-year-old daughter of a poor farmer-bootlegger, is courted by her childhood sweetheart, Jack Manly, and the wealthy, villainous ’Squire Hardman. Hardman secretly knows there is gold on the Stubbs farm and threatens to foreclose its mortgage unless Ermengarde marries him. Jack vows to seek his fortune in the city and return in time to save the farm.

Hardman kidnaps Ermengarde to force her consent, but abruptly releases her after realizing he can obtain the land simply by foreclosing. A city hunter, Algernon Reginald Jones, then discovers the gold and persuades Ermengarde to elope with him. On the train, she finds a love letter from another woman and pushes him out the window.

Stranded in the city, Ermengarde endures poverty until her honesty impresses the wealthy Mrs. Van Itty, who adopts her. Later, Algernon reappears as her chauffeur, reveals the gold beneath her father’s farm, and helps prompt her return home. Using her new wealth, Ermengarde stops Hardman’s foreclosure. Jack also returns, but with another bride, freeing Ermengarde from their youthful engagement.

Mrs. Van Itty then recognizes Hannah Stubbs as the nurse who stole her baby, Maude, twenty-eight years earlier, revealing Ermengarde’s true identity and age. Realizing that this discovery could cost her any claim to the farm’s gold, Ermengarde blackmails Hardman over her earlier kidnapping, agrees to marry him, and lets him foreclose on the property so they can share its riches.
