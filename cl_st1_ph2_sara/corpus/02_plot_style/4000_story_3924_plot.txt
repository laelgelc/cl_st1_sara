River boss Jimmy is ordered to deliver a huge log drive in only twenty days, despite knowing the schedule is nearly impossible. When thousands of logs become stranded in shallow water above a dam, the owner, Daly, warns that missing the deadline will cost him a valuable contract. Jimmy calmly promises to clear the shallows overnight, though he admits it may cost something.

After secretly resting half his crew, Jimmy leads them into town at midnight. Evading the police, they illegally install flashboards across a power dam, raising the river level by three feet. The resulting flood frees the stranded logs, but also inundates local cellars and factory rooms. Jimmy refuses to surrender until the logs have passed safely through the dam, then peacefully submits to arrest.

Jimmy pleads guilty and pays a five-hundred-dollar fine with money evidently supplied by Daly. Outside court, Daly publicly rebukes him for exceeding his instructions, thereby denying responsibility. Jimmy meekly asks whether the logs arrived on time, then gives Daly a knowing wink, revealing their shared understanding that completing the job mattered more than obeying the law.
