A pretty but inexperienced young hen becomes broody, and her owner secretly places eight duck eggs beneath her. She successfully hatches them and proves a devoted mother, despite neighbors who had predicted she would fail. Because no one realizes the chicks are ducklings, their broad bills, webbed feet, and attraction to water are treated as deformities and illnesses. A pompous doctor prescribes absurd medicines and forbids them from getting wet, while gossiping hens blame the mother’s inexperience.

The healthy ducklings eventually ignore these warnings, enter the river, and swim away happily. Believing them drowned, the hen collapses in grief. A younger, better-informed doctor then explains that her children are ducks, not malformed chickens. The ducklings soon return safely, proud of their swimming and newly able to support themselves. Their mother is praised for raising such a fine brood, while the previously mistaken adults take credit for the outcome.
