Two broke bushmen, Dave Regan and Jim Bently, encounter drovers waiting near Mulgatown to be paid. Knowing that a shrewd publican called Poisonous Jimmy hopes to lure the drovers into spending their cheques, Dave and Jim hide their swags and pose as members of the party. Jimmy gives them free drinks, lends them a sovereign, and sends them away with a can of beer for their supposed mates.

After giving the beer to the real drovers, Dave and Jim secretly bypass the shanty and escape through Mulgatown. When the drovers later pass Jimmy’s place without stopping, he realizes he has been tricked. He pursues them, angrily demands repayment, and starts a fight before learning that the two swindlers never belonged to their group. Enraged, Jimmy gets drunk and sets out after the pair with a tomahawk, but mounted police arrest him, and he is fined.
