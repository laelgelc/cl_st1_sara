A gentleman builds a glass-filled cottage in a forest, alarming the animals, who fear that humans will destroy their peaceful home. Their suspicions fade when the residents protect the plants and birds, and Aunt Esther kindly feeds the wildlife. Two young squirrels, Tit-bit and Frisky, gradually overcome their mother’s warnings, enter the cottage, and become so tame that they eat from Aunt Esther’s hand and play throughout the house. Their friendship ends sadly when Frisky drowns in a water tank, and Aunt Esther later dies.

The cottage remains popular with the squirrels. Eventually, a squirrel family moves into its eaves and garret, enjoying the warmth and storage space but disturbing the human occupants with early-morning activity and noisy quarrels. Although frustrated, the residents find the squirrels so charming that they repeatedly postpone driving them out, leaving the uneasy coexistence unresolved.
