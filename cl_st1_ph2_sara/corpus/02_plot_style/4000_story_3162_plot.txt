A young man grows up in an isolated, enchanted valley with his cousin Eleonora and her mother. After years of companionship, he and Eleonora fall deeply in love, and the valley itself seems to blossom in response. When Eleonora realizes she is dying, she fears he will leave the valley and give his love to another woman. To comfort her, he solemnly vows never to marry and invokes a terrible curse upon himself should he break that promise. Eleonora dies peacefully, promising to watch over him in spirit.

As the years pass, the valley’s beauty fades, though the narrator senses Eleonora’s presence in perfumes, sighing winds, murmurs, and a single spectral kiss. Eventually, grief and loneliness drive him from the valley into a bustling city and royal court. There he meets Ermengarde, falls overwhelmingly in love, and marries her despite his vow.

No curse follows. Instead, Eleonora’s spirit speaks to him one final time, telling him to rest peacefully: the power of love has absolved him of his promise, for reasons he will understand in Heaven.
