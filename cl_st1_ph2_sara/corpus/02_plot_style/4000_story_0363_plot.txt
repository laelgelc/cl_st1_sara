A woman keeps a solitary vigil beside her dying husband in a deserted New York boarding house. Overwhelmed by grief, she struggles to accept that the once-vital man she loves is now unconscious and unreachable, though his heart still faintly beats. Determined to remain with him until the end, she rests her hand on his chest so she can feel the exact moment of his death.

As darkness falls, her sorrow turns into irrational terror. She begins imagining Death as a physical presence creeping toward them. A cat entering through the window badly frightens her, and soon afterward she hears slow footsteps ascending through the silent house. Convinced that Death itself is approaching to claim her husband, she listens in mounting panic as the steps reach the landing, advance down the hall, and stop outside the room.

When someone knocks and opens the door, the terrified woman throws herself across her husband. Mary, the servant, enters and discovers that both husband and wife are dead.
