Cadets from Colby Hall Military Academy hold a skating race across Clearwater Lake and back. Jack Rover and his cousins Fred, Andy, and Randy compete against several strong skaters. After conserving his strength, Jack makes a powerful final sprint and wins in record time, while Randy finishes second. Dan Soppinger and Walt Baxter tie for third.

As the racers are congratulated, a large crowd gathers on ice that is not completely safe. The weight causes ominous cracks, and panic breaks out as people attempt to scatter. Several skaters fall, including Ruth Stevenson and May Powell, who become stranded where the ice is sinking and water is rising around them. Realizing the danger, Jack, Randy, Andy, and Fred rush to rescue the girls as the ice threatens to collapse.
