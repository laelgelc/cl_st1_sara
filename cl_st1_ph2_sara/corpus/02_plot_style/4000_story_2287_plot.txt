Father Olympus, a celebrated cathedral deacon with a magnificent voice, is deeply moved by a novel by Leo Tolstoy that portrays life with compassion, freedom, and joy. During a Lenten service, after solemnly pronouncing a long series of traditional anathemas, he is unexpectedly ordered to excommunicate Tolstoy. As he recites the ritual curses, passages from the novel return to him, and he becomes horrified at condemning the writer whose words awakened such tenderness in him.

Unable to reconcile Christian faith with institutional malice, Olympus abruptly abandons the curse and instead publicly blesses Tolstoy, proclaiming “Many years” to him before the astonished congregation. He leaves the cathedral despite attempts to restrain him, accepting that he will lose his position and may be punished. When his wife berates him, he declares that he would rather perform humble labor than continue in a role that violates his conscience. For the first time, his forceful conviction silences her, and he walks away with solemn dignity.
