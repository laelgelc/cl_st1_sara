Kadambini, a childless widow devoted to her brother-in-law’s young son, is mistakenly declared dead after her heart suddenly stops. Taken to a remote cremation ground, she revives and leaves before her frightened attendants return. Believing that she must be a ghost, she feels unable to return home and instead seeks refuge with her childhood friend Jogmaya.

Kadambini’s strange behavior and conviction that she is dead gradually frighten Jogmaya’s household. When Jogmaya’s husband visits Kadambini’s family, he learns that she supposedly died just before arriving at their home. Overhearing the couple argue about her identity, Kadambini declares herself dead and flees.

She secretly returns to her former home to see the boy she raised, who is ill and still longs for her. Their affectionate reunion convinces her that she is alive, but the family reacts to her as a ghost. Desperate to prove her living state, she wounds herself to show that she bleeds, yet no one believes her. She finally throws herself into a well and truly dies, proving through her death that she had previously been alive.
