In colonial Communipaw, kindly tavern keeper Teunis Van Gieson raises his mischievous nephew, Yan Yost Vanderscamp, and shelters a mysterious castaway named Pluto. Pluto encourages Yan’s lawlessness, and the pair eventually disappear. Years later, Yan returns as a pirate captain with Pluto and a band of violent seafarers, turning the tavern into a notorious hideout. After several of his companions are captured and hanged on nearby Gibbet Island, Yan vanishes again.

Yan later returns with a domineering wife and claims to have retired, though his secret nighttime dealings suggest smuggling or continued piracy. One stormy night, while Pluto rows him past Gibbet Island, the drunken Yan sees the corpses of his three executed comrades and recklessly invites them to supper. When he reaches home, his wife complains that three strange guests are already waiting upstairs. Yan finds the dead pirates drinking beneath a supernatural blue light; horrified, he falls down the stairs and dies.

The tavern becomes feared as a haunted house, occupied only by Yan’s widow and the increasingly sinister Pluto. During another violent storm, villagers hear shrieks and fighting inside. The next morning they find the house ransacked and the widow strangled, while Pluto has disappeared. His overturned boat and body are later found near the gallows on Gibbet Island, leaving the villagers to believe that he invited the dead pirates once too often.
