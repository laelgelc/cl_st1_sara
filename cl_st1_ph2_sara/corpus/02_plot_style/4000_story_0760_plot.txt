Sally accuses Silas, her husband Noah’s tenant, of abducting her, nailing her inside his shack for three days, and repeatedly pouring water down the chimney whenever she tried to make a fire. After escaping, she attacks Silas with a bed slat until a policeman intervenes. Silas denies imprisoning her, claiming he was away attending a prophet’s failed gathering for believers expecting to ascend to heaven.

Under questioning, the nearly deaf Noah accidentally reveals the truth: believing Silas was inside the shack, he nailed it shut to prevent his rent-dodging tenant from going to heaven before paying what he owed. He also poured water down the chimney to stop any supernatural escape by fire, repeatedly declaring that Silas was not going to heaven yet. Noah had unknowingly trapped Sally instead. The judge dismisses the affair as a domestic misunderstanding, while the furious Sally threatens to punish Noah herself.
