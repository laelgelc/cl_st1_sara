On Christmas Eve, guests gathered in an old house exchange stories by the fire. George Grady recounts how, years earlier in the same house, he encountered an unusual boy named Joseph wandering downstairs at night. Joseph claimed that his bedroom was the room in which he had been murdered in a previous life. He vividly remembered a woman creeping toward his bed and strangling him, while Grady himself had sensed an unseen presence moving upstairs toward the room.

When Grady finishes, a young girl among the listeners anxiously asks whether Joseph is still alive. Upon learning that he is now a living adult, she panics and confesses, “I did it,” before collapsing. Grady reveals that he deliberately told the story because he had long suspected the girl of being Joseph’s murderer and wanted to provoke a confession.
