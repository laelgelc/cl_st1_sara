On his half-holiday, William plans to meet his band of Outlaws but is forced to take Mrs. Butler’s baby out in a pram. Drawn toward the Outlaws’ barn, he leaves the pram tied to a railing and carries the baby to the meeting, claiming he has kidnapped it for ransom.

The boys abandon the baby while seeking adventures, and it spills paraffin oil over itself, smears itself with potatoes and dumpling, and ruins much of their feast. Their attempts to clean it with licorice water only worsen its appearance. Meanwhile, another boy brings a stolen cow to the barn in imitation of legendary outlaws. The cow eats the baby’s hat, knocks it with its tail, and tramples the boys’ fire, while the baby creates further chaos throughout their games.

When it is time to return home, William finds that other boys have taken the pram for joyrides and badly battered it. The Outlaws recover it, place the filthy, oil-soaked, food-smeared baby inside, and raise the hood to conceal the damage. William reluctantly wheels the baby home, where its grateful mother thanks him—before she sees what has happened to her child.
