Tom Robinson, a young clerk for an Odessa corn merchant, is sent secretly to Solteff to negotiate a grain deal with the landowner Mr. Dimidoff. His furtive behavior and English-labeled luggage cause both the police and a local Nihilist society to mistake him for Gustave Berger, an English revolutionary agent.

Taken to the Nihilists’ lodge, Robinson assumes he is meeting Dimidoff’s representatives and unwittingly participates in their council. After witnessing the execution of a suspected traitor, he realizes the truth but pretends to be the expected agent to stay alive, bluffing his way through questions about revolutionary operations.

The real Gustave Berger then arrives and exposes Robinson’s identity. The Nihilists decide Robinson must be killed to preserve their secrets. He draws his revolver, shoots Berger when attacked, and is knocked unconscious as the police raid the lodge. He awakens to find the conspirators captured and learns that the suspicious man who followed him from the train is the local police chief. Robinson is finally taken to Dimidoff, completes the grain negotiations successfully, and earns a promotion for his conduct during the ordeal.
