Henry tells the story of Tom Sleight, a reckless young sailor who secretly married fourteen-year-old Mary Godselle in New Hampshire. Immediately after the wedding, Tom was injured in a brawl and carried away aboard his ship. His attempts to contact Mary failed, while she assumed he had abandoned her out of shame. Years later, after inheriting his father’s estate, Tom unknowingly encounters the now-grown Mary working as a café waitress in Paris under the name Marie.

Mary recognizes Tom, but he does not recognize her and promptly falls in love with her again. Hurt by his apparent inconstancy, she conceals her identity and tests his character, encouraging his courtship while flirting with other men to provoke his jealousy. Eventually Tom declares his love, but Mary persuades him that, before seeking a new relationship, he must find the wife he deserted and make amends.

Guided by Mary’s carefully planted clues, Tom travels to America and traces his missing wife’s movements back to Paris. Mary arranges a meeting with him in the Tuileries, identifying herself by the onyx brooch he gave her before their wedding. Before leaving to meet him, she asks Henry whether she should wear both that brooch and the opal brooch Tom recently gave “Marie.” Henry advises her to wear only the onyx, allowing Tom to discover that the wife he has sought and the woman he has fallen in love with are the same person.
