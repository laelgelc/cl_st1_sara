On the bleak Dardale Moss, fortune-teller and suspected witch Mall Carke encounters a sinister giant who asks her to lure the unbaptized orphan Laura Lew—known as Laura Silver Bell—to him by means of a charm. Mall refuses and warns Laura that the handsome young lord courting her is actually a malignant fairy who will claim her if she crosses a stream, accepts his gifts, or joins his revels.

Though initially frightened, Laura becomes infatuated with the stranger. While gathering berries with her friend Bessie, she sees the ragged, hideous figure as a splendid nobleman. Ignoring Bessie’s warnings, Laura crosses the brook, takes his hand, and disappears with him.

More than a year later, while performing a forbidden spell, Mall is summoned to attend a mysterious Lady Lairdale in childbirth. A magnificent coach turns into a wretched cart, and the grand castle into a ruined hovel. There Mall discovers Laura, now filthy, emaciated, and imprisoned as the sinister creature’s mate. Laura gives birth to an inhuman child and secretly warns Mall to accept only her proper fee, lest she too be trapped. Mall obeys and awakens the next morning on her own doorstep. She abandons witchcraft thereafter, while Laura is believed to remain forever among the fairies.
