In 1860 Rivermouth, Margaret Callaghan, the Bilkins family’s longtime cook, secretly leaves their house at dawn and marries Larry O’Rourke, a sailor eighteen years her junior. She returns before the household wakes, hides the marriage certificate, and guiltily serves breakfast without revealing what she has done.

Later that morning, Larry arrives at the Bilkins home extremely drunk and demands his wife. Mrs. Bilkins initially assumes he is merely a troublesome sailor, but his companion explains that Larry has just married Margaret. Shocked and angry at both the secret marriage and Margaret’s choice of husband, Mrs. Bilkins confronts her servant and orders her to take Larry away. Margaret accepts the consequences, leads her intoxicated husband down the street, and begins married life with him in a shabby tenement near the river.
