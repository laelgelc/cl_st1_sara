Edward Marvel, unable to find employment in England and humiliated by relying on his father-in-law’s support, considers seeking opportunity in America. His devoted wife, Agnes, insists on accompanying him, but Edward refuses and instead returns alone to London for one final attempt at finding work. When that effort fails, he secretly sails for New York, promising to send for her once established.

Agnes immediately follows on a faster steamship and reaches New York before him. Edward becomes gravely ill during his voyage and is taken to a hospital upon arrival. After learning that his ship carried sick passengers, Agnes traces him to the hospital and reunites with him when he is near death. Her presence and care help him recover.

A month later, Edward worries that they remain dependent on strangers and that he still has no prospects. Agnes reveals that, through the friends she has made, she has already secured employment for him once he regains his strength. She urges him never again to face hardship alone, reminding him that husband and wife must meet life’s trials together.
