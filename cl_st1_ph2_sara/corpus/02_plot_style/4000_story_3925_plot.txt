In a lumber town on the Fourth of July, an outsider meets riverman Jimmy Powers and watches the annual log-birling contest. Champion Dickey “Roaring Dick” Darrell defeats every challenger, including Jimmy, by secretly driving his spiked boot into Jimmy’s foot and tripping him. Jimmy refuses to complain, saying he should have protected himself, but vows to settle the score.

The following spring, the outsider visits a massive logjam where Darrell is now rear boss and Jimmy works under him. The jam suddenly breaks, leaving Darrell trapped in open water between two moving masses of logs. Despite his hatred, Jimmy races across the unstable timber, grabs Darrell, and carries him to safety just before the gap closes.

Harry Thorpe, a renowned lumberman who witnessed the rescue, rewards Jimmy’s courage by recruiting him directly into his elite camp. When the narrator praises Jimmy for saving an enemy out of common humanity, Jimmy rejects the heroic interpretation. He insists he saved Darrell only so he can defeat him fairly in the next Fourth of July birling match.
