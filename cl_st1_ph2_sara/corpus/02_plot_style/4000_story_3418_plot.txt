Uncle Pete, an illiterate but respected preacher in a rural Virginia Black church, is inspired by a quarrel with his sharp-tempered wife to preach that every woman is possessed by seven devils. He inventively links this claim to Eve’s tasting seven apples offered by the serpent. The sermon amuses some men but infuriates the women, who organize a meeting and resolve to remove him unless he can prove his doctrine from Scripture.

After returning from a week of work away from home, Pete faces a packed congregation demanding biblical evidence. He asks whether Scripture says that Jesus cast seven devils out of Mary Magdalene; the congregation agrees that it does. He then asks whether the Bible records their being cast out of any other woman. When no one can say it does, he concludes that all other women must still have theirs. Stunned by his clever reasoning, the congregation allows him to continue the service.
