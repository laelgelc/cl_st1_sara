Nineteen-year-old musical prodigy Gilbert Swann is chosen to play the violoncello in the orchestra at the Five Towns’ inaugural musical festival. His proud mother believes the festival’s success rests largely on him, especially after the socially prominent Mrs Clayton Vernon invites him to dine with the orchestra leader before the opening concert.

Worried that the freezing weather will stiffen Gilbert’s hands, Mrs Swann carries two hot baked potatoes to Mrs Clayton Vernon’s house for him to use as hand warmers. Intimidated by her grand hostess, she nervously crushes one potato inside her muff and accidentally drops and steps on the other, all while trying to conceal her embarrassing errand. Mrs Clayton Vernon then casually reveals that the orchestra leader has requested hot potatoes for the same purpose and that she is already preparing some for Gilbert. Too proud to confess what has happened, Mrs Swann privately explains everything to her son, hides the ruined potatoes behind the fire, and hurriedly leaves.
