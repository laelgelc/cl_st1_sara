A man recounts a frightening night alone on the Seine. After anchoring his boat to rest and smoke, he becomes unnerved by the river’s unnatural silence and apparent movements. When he tries to leave, the anchor refuses to rise, leaving him trapped as a dense fog envelops the boat. His imagination fills the unseen water with threatening creatures, and despite attempts to calm himself with reason and rum, his fear grows into terror.

Hours later, the fog retreats to the banks, revealing a dazzling moonlit river, and his fear briefly subsides. He eventually falls asleep, then wakes in darkness and bad weather. At dawn, two fishermen help him pull up the stubborn anchor. They discover it has snagged the corpse of an old woman weighted with a large stone around her neck.
