Prince Padema flees Venice after accidentally murdering a nobleman and hides in Florence. There he becomes infatuated with a beautiful Venetian exile living below him and secretly courts her from his balcony. When she appears to yield, she persuades him to descend toward her balcony on a rope whose end she controls.

Once he is suspended high above the street, the woman reveals that she is the sister of Meela, whose drowning she blames on Padema. Having deliberately lured him into a trap, she prevents him from climbing back, rejects his desperate promises, and releases the rope. Padema falls to his death, while the circumstances are left to resemble an accident.
