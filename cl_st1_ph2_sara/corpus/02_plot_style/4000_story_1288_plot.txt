In a Ukrainian village at dusk, Leoko, the headman’s eldest son, secretly visits his beloved Hanna. They affirm their love and discuss marriage, though Leoko’s father refuses to approve the match. Hanna then asks about an abandoned house beside a nearby pool. Leoko recounts the legend of a young woman whose new stepmother was a witch. After discovering the witch’s identity, the girl was mistreated and cast out by her bewitched father, so she drowned herself in the pool. When approaching villagers force the lovers to part, Hanna remains haunted by the tale as she watches the eerie house and moonlit pool before returning indoors.
