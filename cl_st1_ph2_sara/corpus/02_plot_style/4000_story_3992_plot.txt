Police constable Edward Plimmer, assigned to a peaceful Battersea beat, falls in love with Ellen Brown, a cook in one of the residential flats. Ellen, however, is courting Alf Brooks, a charming and popular milkman, leaving the awkward, unattractive Plimmer painfully jealous.

When Ellen’s employer accuses her of stealing money and borrowing a brooch without permission, Plimmer must escort her to the police station. On the way, they encounter Alf, who realizes Ellen has been arrested and pretends not to know her to protect his reputation. Seeing Ellen’s heartbreak, Plimmer offers to let her escape, despite knowing it could cost him his career and freedom. Ellen refuses to let him sacrifice himself and insists on facing prison.

As they approach the station, Plimmer confesses his love and promises to be waiting when she is released. Moved by his loyalty and Alf’s cowardice, Ellen reveals that her feelings have changed and suggests she may soon return Plimmer’s affection.
