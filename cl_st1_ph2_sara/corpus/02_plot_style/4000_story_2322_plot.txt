A woman returns with her new husband, Frank, to the seaside village where she once lived, asking him to conceal her identity. Sending Frank out alone, she secretly visits the rectory garden associated with Archie, a former lover whom she believed had died after suffering sunstroke in Africa.

In the garden, she unexpectedly encounters Archie alive but mentally deranged. He fails to recognize her coherently, and the sight of the man she passionately loved reduced to a stranger leaves her devastated. Back at their cottage, she withdraws from Frank, whose jealousy and resentment finally force her to confess her past: she and Archie had been lovers, and she married Frank after hearing Archie was dead.

Her revelation humiliates Frank and confirms that she has never truly loved him. When she adds that Archie is alive but insane and did not recognize her, both spouses are stunned into emotional distance. Recognizing the immense breach between them, Frank leaves her alone.
