Winter Wedderburn, a shy and uneventful orchid enthusiast, buys a mysterious plant collected by an adventurer who died drained of blood in a tropical swamp. The ugly specimen revives in his hothouse, developing finger-like aerial roots and eventually producing spectacular flowers with an overpoweringly sweet perfume.

While admiring the bloom, Wedderburn is drugged by its scent and collapses. The orchid’s tentacle-like roots attach themselves to his face, neck, and hands and begin sucking his blood. His housekeeper discovers him, resists the intoxicating perfume, breaks the greenhouse windows for fresh air, and drags both man and plant outside before tearing away the roots.

Wedderburn survives after losing considerable blood, while the exposed orchid dies and decays. Far from being traumatized, he delights in finally having experienced the remarkable adventure he had always desired.
