At a railway station, friendly rivals Mr Sandbach and Mr Gale notice a shabby man standing immovably near a bookstall. Sandbach challenges Gale to make him move without physical force. Gale first drops a half-crown nearby, but the man ignores it. Sandbach then explains that the man is apparently concealing a lost half-sovereign beneath his foot while waiting for the bookstall clerk to leave.

Gale wagers that he can still move him and silently offers the man another half-sovereign to walk away, expecting to recover the hidden coin and win the bet. The shabby man accepts and leaves, but no coin is visible, causing Gale to suspect Sandbach of tricking him. As the two quarrel, the original coin falls from a crack in the shabby man’s boot, and Sandbach retrieves it.

They then argue over who deserves the money, only for a young woman to identify it by its date and a scratched initial. Sandbach returns it to her, and the men end the afternoon still disputing how they should divide Gale’s loss, while the shabby man has escaped with Gale’s half-sovereign.
