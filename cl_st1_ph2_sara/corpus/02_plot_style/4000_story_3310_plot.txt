The Golden family discovers an abandoned six-month-old baby on their doorstep with a note asking “the woman with the kind eyes” to care for him. Mother Golden persuades her cautious husband and five children to keep the child, at least temporarily. They name him Benjamin and raise him as part of the household.

Ten years later, Mother Golden has died, and mischievous Benny continually exasperates his now-grown siblings. After they complain about his latest pranks, Father Golden reminds them that Benny is not their blood relative and forces them to make a lasting choice: either send him to an orphanage or accept him fully as their brother, including giving him an equal share of the family inheritance.

Each sibling realizes how deeply Benny belongs in the family and solemnly asks him to stay. When Benny bursts in, cheerfully owning up to his mischief and inviting someone to fly his new kite, all five siblings eagerly volunteer to go with him, demonstrating their renewed commitment to him.
