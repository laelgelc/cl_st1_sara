During a break on the night shift at a Sierra foothills mine, Bunt tells an old friend about Peg-leg Smith, a vicious one-legged gunman with an obsessive hatred of profanity. Years earlier in Yuma, Peg-leg attacked Bunt’s consumptive friend Clarence for swearing during a peculiar gambling game in which tuberculosis patients competed to register the highest temperature. Bunt intervened by pulling off Peg-leg’s wooden leg, throwing it and the gunman’s weapon toward a canyon, and fleeing.

When the leg was recovered, Bunt kept it from Peg-leg and proposed a bargain: he would renounce profanity for as long as Peg-leg lived if Peg-leg promised never to harm him. Peg-leg accepted, and both honored the agreement despite Peg-leg’s continued cruelty and violence toward others.

Three years later, Bunt encountered Peg-leg during a hotel fire in Arizona. Trapped on the fourth floor, Peg-leg was told by men below to jump because they had a blanket ready. He jumped to his death, only to discover too late that there was no blanket. His death freed Bunt from his vow and rid the region of a dangerous killer.
