A younger student resents that his older brother, captain of the school baseball team, has avoided appearing partial by denying him a fair chance at his preferred position and using him only as a reserve outfielder. Their tension worsens before the deciding game against a rival academy, especially because their parents are attending and the younger brother wants an opportunity to prove himself.

The rival team takes an early four-run lead, but the home team gradually rallies. The captain finally sends his brother into center field for the ninth inning. With the score tied, the younger brother comes to bat with two runners on base and delivers a hit that drives them both home, giving his team a 6–4 lead.

In the final half-inning, the rivals put two runners on base with no outs and send their best hitter to the plate. He hits a shallow fly that seems certain to fall safely, but the younger brother races in from center and catches it just above the ground. Because both runners had assumed the ball would drop, he tags one and steps on second to retire the other, completing an unassisted triple play and ending the game. His teammates carry him off in triumph, while his brother and parents look on proudly.
