Brigand chief Toza is captured and condemned by Prince Lotarno to be beheaded for murdering the Prince’s brother. Surprised by the seemingly merciful sentence, Toza bribes his gaoler, Paulo, with the location of hidden treasure. Paulo helps him escape and promises to guide him through a secret mountain tunnel out of the valley.

For days, the fugitives evade soldiers while enduring hunger, rain, exhaustion, and constant fear. Their ordeal gradually destroys Toza’s former bravado. On the appointed execution day, they finally reach the tunnel. Once inside, Toza secretly plans to kill Paulo and recover his treasure, but Paulo distrusts him and makes him enter first.

At the tunnel’s far end, Toza opens a bolted door, only for Paulo to shove him through and lock it behind him. The tunnel leads directly into an execution chamber, where the Prince, his ministers, and a masked executioner await. Toza arrives precisely at four o’clock—the time fixed for his beheading—revealing that his apparent escape was an elaborate scheme to torment him with hope and terror before carrying out the sentence.
