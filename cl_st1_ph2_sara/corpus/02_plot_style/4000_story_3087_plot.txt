At an isolated California mining camp, young superintendent Lockwood becomes dangerously infatuated with Felice Zavalla, the beautiful, flirtatious wife of Chino, a trusted shift boss. Though he tries to avoid her, he convinces himself that she shares his love and begins wishing she could be free of her husband.

After Lockwood injures his foot, Chino must carry a valuable gold brick alone to town. Lockwood then receives a warning that the notorious Reno Kid plans to rob and kill the courier. For a moment, he considers doing nothing and allowing Chino’s death to solve his romantic dilemma, but his conscience prevails. He rides after Chino despite his injury.

Unable to find Chino before reaching town, Lockwood turns back. In the darkness he mistakes an approaching rider for the outlaw and shoots, only to discover that he has wounded Chino. He brings him home, where Felice assumes Lockwood deliberately tried to kill her husband so they could marry. Her approving reaction reveals her cruelty and treachery, instantly destroying Lockwood’s love for her. The doctor announces that Chino’s wound is superficial and that he will recover, while Lockwood realizes he has narrowly escaped both moral ruin and a disastrous relationship.
