When Alfred Tollet falls ill, his timid, devoted wife Lucy secretly adds gentian to his food after he refuses medicine. Alfred recovers, but Lucy is tormented by guilt and eventually confesses. Feeling betrayed, Alfred refuses to eat anything she prepares and declares that he will cook for himself. Believing she is no longer wanted, Lucy leaves to live with her outspoken sister Hannah.

Although nearly a year passes, Lucy remains deeply attached to Alfred and miserable away from home. One spring evening, she returns and begs him to take her back. He initially orders her to return to Hannah’s, but soon follows her and asks her to come home, admitting that he feels poorly again and wants her to prepare the gentian that helped him before.
