In Austin’s General Land Office, unscrupulous land agent Sharp discovers that a supposedly missing certificate validates a valuable tract occupied by a widow and her son, Edward Harris. Sharp has already claimed the land as vacant and is close to receiving a patent when he accidentally finds the certificate misfiled. Realizing it would destroy his claim, he secretly takes the entire file.

Edward, having observed Sharp’s suspicious actions, remains in the building after closing and confronts him, intending to present the file to the Commissioner. Enraged and desperate to protect his reputation and fraudulent claim, Sharp murders Edward, conceals his body in the building, and later removes and buries it near Shoal Creek. The file remains with the corpse, allowing Sharp to secure the patent and build his fortune from the stolen land.

Years later, after Sharp has died respected but melancholy, treasure hunters uncover Edward’s skeleton. In his coat they find the blood-soaked, knife-pierced remains of the long-missing land file, exposing the truth behind Edward’s disappearance and Sharp’s crime.
