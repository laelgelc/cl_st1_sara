Mrs. Penelope Carroll takes her poor, spirited niece Debby to a fashionable seaside resort, secretly intending to find her a wealthy husband. On the train, Debby befriends Frank Evan, a plainly dressed, book-loving stranger whom her status-conscious aunt dismisses as an unsuitable clerk. At the resort, Mrs. Carroll encourages the attentions of rich but shallow Joe Leavenworth, while Debby’s natural manners and honest references to her country upbringing clash with fashionable expectations and expose the artificiality of resort society. During a seaside bathing excursion, Mrs. Carroll loses her false teeth in the water. Frank reappears, retrieves them, and earns Debby’s gratitude, strengthening the connection between the sincere young pair despite her aunt’s matrimonial schemes.
