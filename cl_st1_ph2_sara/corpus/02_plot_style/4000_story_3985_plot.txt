Clarence Tresillian, the aristocratic son of a financially embarrassed earl, falls in love with Isabel Rackstraw, whose wealthy Radical father despises the peerage and insists she marry a working man. Forced by his family’s reduced income to earn a living, Clarence adopts the name Jones and becomes the star goalkeeper for Houndsditch Wednesday.

Isabel’s football-obsessed father becomes an ardent admirer of the mysterious Jones. When illness keeps Clarence out of the championship final, Houndsditch loses to Manchester United, prompting Rackstraw to arrange a private rematch and wager a prized football relic on the result.

Before the rematch, Clarence asks Rackstraw for Isabel’s hand. Rackstraw refuses, so Clarence threatens not to play well, thereby costing him the match and his treasured collectible. Impressed by this ruthless act of blackmail, Rackstraw decides Clarence is exactly the kind of son-in-law and business partner he wants. Even after learning that Jones is actually the son of an earl, he approves the marriage, valuing Clarence’s goalkeeping and audacity above his political prejudices.

Four years later, Clarence and Isabel are married with a son, Rackstraw has won the coveted football relic, and he and Clarence are prospering together in business by attempting to corner the wheat market.
