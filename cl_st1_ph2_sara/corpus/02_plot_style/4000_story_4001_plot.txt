Three friends—Tub, Frank, and Kenny—go deer hunting in the snowy countryside. Throughout the trip, Kenny and Frank mock the overweight Tub, abandon him as he struggles through the snow, and belittle his claim that a glandular condition causes his weight. The hunt proves unsuccessful, increasing tensions among them.

After receiving permission to hunt on a farmer’s land, Kenny impulsively shoots a fence post, a tree, and then the farmer’s old dog. When he turns to Tub and says he hates him, Tub believes Kenny is about to shoot and fires first, wounding him in the stomach. They later learn that the farmer had actually asked Kenny to put down the sick dog.

Frank and Tub load Kenny into their truck and set off for a distant hospital, but they repeatedly stop to warm themselves while Kenny lies bleeding and freezing in the truck bed. During these stops, Frank admits that he plans to leave his wife for a fifteen-year-old babysitter, while Tub confesses that his supposed glandular problem is a lie and that he secretly overeats. Their mutual confessions create a new bond, and Frank encourages Tub to indulge openly in a huge meal.

Growing increasingly indifferent to Kenny, they take his blankets for themselves and laugh over the misunderstanding about the dog. Kenny keeps reassuring himself that he is going to the hospital, unaware that Frank and Tub lost the directions and took a wrong turn long ago.
