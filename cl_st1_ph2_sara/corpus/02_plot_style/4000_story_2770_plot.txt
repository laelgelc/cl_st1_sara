A lawyer recounts being summoned by a wealthy dying woman who offers him a large fee to find her long-missing son. She confesses that, after becoming a widow, she resumed a relationship with a married former lover, who helped her raise her son, Jean, as a trusted friend and father figure.

When Jean was seventeen, he unexpectedly saw his mother and the man kissing. Horrified by the discovery, he fled. The lover searched for him but could not find him, while the mother suffered a nervous collapse and brain fever. After recovering, she banished her lover until he could bring Jean back. Neither man ever returned, although the lover wrote to her daily for twenty years.

Now dying after decades of remorse and anguish, she asks the lawyer to find Jean, tell him of her suffering, beg his forgiveness, and assure him that she never saw her lover again. The lawyer leaves in tears but is unable to locate Jean, whom he condemns for cruelly abandoning his mother.
