An expedition reaches a planet devastated by atomic war and finds its surface lifeless. Just as the crew prepares to leave, a precisely aimed automated weapon shoots down their ship. While repairs begin and their injured captain slowly dies, Nasha, Dorle, and the archaeologist Tance explore a ruined city and discover the gun that attacked them. It has survived its makers and automatically targets anything airborne.

Beneath the weapon, they find a protected vault containing the extinct civilization’s art, literature, films, history, and other cultural treasures. Realizing the gun cannot defend itself against people on the ground, the crew smashes it, removes its warheads, repairs the ship, and departs, intending to return for the archive.

After they leave, however, the damaged gun signals a distant underground facility. Automated carts loaded with tools, replacement parts, and atomic warheads emerge and begin traveling toward the city to rebuild it.
