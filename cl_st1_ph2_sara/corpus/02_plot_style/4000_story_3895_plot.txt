Wealthy, boastful Cobham Stilling has squandered much of his wife Isabel’s fortune through speculation and secretly taken his mother’s securities as well. Isabel is trapped in a miserable marriage but refuses to leave because of her son and her concern for Cobham’s devoted mother. She finds comfort in a clandestine relationship with Cobham’s lawyer and friend, Austin Wrayford.

During a secret nighttime meeting in the boathouse, Wrayford tells Isabel the extent of Cobham’s financial ruin. She confesses that, although she continues to fulfill her duties, she constantly wishes her husband dead. When the drunken Cobham unexpectedly approaches, Wrayford opens the boathouse’s sliding floor, and Cobham falls into the deep water below. Wrayford immediately dives in after him.

Unable to see clearly, Isabel lowers an oar and calls her husband’s name. Cobham grasps it and climbs to safety, while Wrayford loses his hold and drowns. Cobham survives to mourn his friend with hollow, self-centered platitudes, while Isabel remains bound to the husband whose death she had desired.
