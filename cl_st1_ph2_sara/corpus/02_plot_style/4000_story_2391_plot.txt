Jim Sulivan, an amiable man trapped in a violent marriage to the beautiful but bad-tempered Nell Gorman, storms out after she scalds and strikes him. On the road, he learns that his uncle is supposedly dying and travels through a snowstorm to visit him, only to find him recovering. Heavy snow then prevents Jim from returning home.

Believing Jim has killed himself, Nell sends servants to search for his body. They find a similarly dressed traveler whose face was destroyed in a fatal fall and mistake him for Jim. After the body is waked and buried, Nell quickly marries Andy Curtis.

When the snow melts, Jim returns at night. Nell and Andy, convinced he is a ghost, refuse to admit him despite his threats, so he sleeps in the cowshed. The next morning they summon a priest and pay him twelve pounds to banish the spirit. The priest’s ritual awakens Jim, and when Jim enters the house, the terrified priest strikes him with a bell and flees.

Once the mistake is understood, Jim is willing to leave Nell with Andy, but the priest insists that Jim and Nell remarry, while Andy receives a small penance for marrying a widow too hastily. The neighbors enjoy the extraordinary succession of a wake and two weddings.
