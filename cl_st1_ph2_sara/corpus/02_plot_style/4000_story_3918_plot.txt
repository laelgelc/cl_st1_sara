Richard “Roaring Dick” Darrell is a highly effective but harsh lumber-camp foreman whose competence and courage emerge only in the context of his work. In town, where alcohol makes him unstable, he meekly allows the swaggering Silver Jack to humiliate and throw him out of a saloon. Mistaking this behavior for cowardice, Silver Jack later ventures into the woods with whiskey, hoping to tempt exhausted lumbermen into abandoning their camps and spending their wages at his saloon.

With a difficult logging season already threatening morale and productivity, Darrell intercepts Silver Jack and his companion before they reach his headquarters. He orders them to turn back, but Silver Jack recalls their earlier encounter and attacks. Though repeatedly beaten and eventually confronted with deadly weapons, Darrell refuses to flee because protecting the camp and its work is his responsibility. After a brutal struggle, he defeats both men, destroys their whiskey and sleigh, cuts apart their harness, and forces them to walk their horses back through the frozen forest. Silver Jack finally realizes that the submissive man he bullied in town becomes an indomitable force when defending his work.
