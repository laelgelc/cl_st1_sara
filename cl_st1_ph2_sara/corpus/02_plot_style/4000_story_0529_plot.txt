The Daemons of Selfishness, Envy, Hatred, and Malice resent Santa Claus because his Christmas gifts make children happy and keep them away from their caves. After failing to tempt Santa into vice, they kidnap him during his Christmas Eve journey, hoping that children deprived of presents will become selfish, envious, hateful, and malicious.

Santa’s four assistants discover his disappearance but decide that fulfilling his mission must come first. They distribute the toys themselves, making a few mistakes but successfully bringing happiness to nearly every child. They then gather a vast army of magical beings to rescue Santa.

Meanwhile, the Daemon of Repentance regrets helping with the kidnapping and releases Santa from captivity. Santa meets the approaching rescue army and persuades it not to punish the Daemons, explaining that they cannot be destroyed and have their place in the world. After returning home, he corrects his assistants’ few gift-delivery errors. Realizing their scheme has failed and that Santa has powerful allies, the Daemons never interfere with his Christmas journeys again.
