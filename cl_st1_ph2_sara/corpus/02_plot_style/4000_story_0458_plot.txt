Three penniless student rogues pose as wealthy merchants at a prosperous, miserly innkeeper’s establishment in Tours. For five days they feast lavishly while causing chaos at the local fair, but when payment is demanded, they propose that each tell a bawdy tale and that whoever tells the worst one settle the bill.

The students recount comic stories involving drunkenness, sexual misadventure, and marital strife. The innkeeper joins the contest and tells an embarrassing story about his own wife’s flatulence on their wedding night. The students flatter him extravagantly and ask to meet the famous wife. As the innkeeper leads them upstairs, they notice the street door open and flee, leaving him unpaid and thoroughly duped.
