Tired of living where everything always stays the same, Gimme the Ax and his two self-named children, Please Gimme and Ax Me No Questions, sell all their possessions and buy a special train ticket to travel beyond the end of the railroad. Their journey carries them through fantastical lands where trains pass over and under one another, balloon pickers work on stilts, and circus clowns are baked in ovens and brought to life. Eventually, the tracks begin to zigzag, and they arrive in the Rootabaga Country, where pigs wear patterned bibs and the great city is the Village of Liver-and-Onions.
