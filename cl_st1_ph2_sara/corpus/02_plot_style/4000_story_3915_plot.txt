During the Revolutionary War, young Kitty and Harry Tracy anxiously await their soldier father’s return to their home in Bordentown, New Jersey, for Christmas. They prepare gifts for him, but enemy troops and the Delaware River separate Captain Tracy from his family. When he does not arrive, the children save their Christmas turkey so they can celebrate whenever he returns.

After Christmas, the town hears distant fighting and learns that Washington’s army has surprised and defeated the enemy at Trenton, causing the Hessians occupying Bordentown to flee. Captain Tracy then comes home, exhausted but safe. He brings a small spaniel whose dying British master entrusted it to him on the battlefield. The children adopt the dog, and the family prepares to celebrate a belated Christmas together, grateful for both their reunion and the patriot victory.
