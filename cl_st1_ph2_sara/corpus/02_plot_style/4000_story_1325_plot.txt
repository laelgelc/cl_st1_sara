Yale star pitcher Wayne arrives to play as a paid “ringer” for Bellville against the amateur Salisbury team. After Salisbury captain Rex Huling graciously welcomes him, Wayne meets Rex’s sister Dorothy and is immediately captivated by her. When she asks him to pitch for Salisbury instead, Wayne mistakenly assumes she is flirting as part of a scheme. He agrees on the conditions that she wear Yale blue and kiss him if he wins, but her hurt reaction makes him realize he has insulted her.

Regretting his behavior, Wayne resolves to win the game and apologize. In a tense pitchers’ duel, he overcomes Salisbury’s weak defense, strikes out Bellville’s best hitters, and drives in the only two runs, securing Salisbury’s victory. At the celebratory dance, he apologizes to Dorothy, renounces his demand for a kiss, and offers to pitch for Salisbury for the rest of the season if she will forgive him and become his friend. Dorothy reveals that she is still wearing the Yale-blue ribbon, signaling her forgiveness and affection.
