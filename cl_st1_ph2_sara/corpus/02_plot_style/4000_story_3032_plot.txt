Timid English businessman John James Abbleway travels from Vienna to Fiume to meet his fiancée, but a snowstorm causes his railway carriage to break away from the train in a remote forest. Stranded with a peasant woman, he grows terrified after she tells him wolves infest the area, while she insists that her patron saint will protect her because it is her name day. She exploits his fear and hunger by selling him food at an exorbitant price.

When animal heads appear at the window, Abbleway assumes wolves are preparing to devour them. The woman nevertheless leaves the carriage for a nearby inn, confident she is safe. Two animals rush at her, but they prove to be her cousin Karl’s friendly dogs. She admits she knew the inn was nearby but avoided mentioning it because Karl overcharges strangers. Before Abbleway can test this claim, a relief engine arrives to rescue them.
