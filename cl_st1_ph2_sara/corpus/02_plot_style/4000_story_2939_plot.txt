Captain James Freeman, a superstitious and possibly criminal sea captain, buys an expensive amulet from the mystic Rabaya to ensure the success of his ship’s next secret voyage. The charm is said to bring fortune when held by a worthy person but to unleash destructive evil in the hands of someone wicked.

A Malay sailor nicknamed the Flying Devil secretly witnesses the sale, steals the amulet, and deserts Freeman’s ship. Believing the charm makes him invincible, he tries to escape San Francisco but becomes increasingly violent. He murders a suspicious stranger, steals a woman’s satchel, stabs a pursuer, kills a sentry, and chokes a guard dog before fleeing into the bay in a small boat.

Soldiers fire on the boat just as it enters a fog bank, apparently destroying it, but evidence suggests that the Malay survives, reaches the fort unseen, and hides inside the barrel of the sunset cannon. When the cannon is later fired with a blank charge, its unusual recoil indicates that something was inside it. Offshore, Freeman’s departing ship is showered with shredded flesh, bone, and cloth. Among the remains, Freeman finds the stolen amulet, scorched but intact. The Flying Devil is never seen again.
