During a difficult summer on her family’s farm, Nora answers an advertisement for a housemaid and child-minder. She joins the Bowles household, where the mother is an invalid and previous servants have left the home neglected. Nora quickly restores order, cares tenderly for Mrs. Bowles, and wins the family’s trust.

While Mr. Bowles is away, Annie, a former servant, returns claiming she has left something in the shed chamber. Suspicious, Nora secretly watches as Annie admits a man through a window and hides him inside a cedar trunk, apparently so he can later rob the house. After Annie leaves, Nora locks the man in the trunk and runs to summon help from the neighboring Brett family. George Brett accompanies her back and subdues the robber when he breaks out.

Rather than condemn Annie, Nora recognizes that the motherless young woman has been manipulated. She visits and befriends her, helping her reform while keeping her involvement secret. The adventure also marks Nora’s first meeting with George, who later becomes her husband.
