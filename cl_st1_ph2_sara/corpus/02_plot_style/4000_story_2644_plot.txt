Jervas Dudley, a wealthy and solitary visionary, becomes obsessed in childhood with the chained tomb of the extinct Hyde family, to whom he is distantly related. As an adult, he believes he finds a key, enters the vault, and repeatedly spends nights among the dead, sleeping in an empty coffin bearing his own name. He gradually adopts the speech, knowledge, drunken habits, and fear of fire associated with Jervas Hyde, who died when the ancestral Hyde mansion burned.

During a thunderstorm, Jervas sees the vanished mansion restored and joins a ghostly revel there. Lightning sets the phantom house ablaze, and he panics at the thought of being burned to ashes and denied burial in the family tomb. He is found raving in the ruined cellar, where the lightning uncovers a miniature of a young man marked “J. H.” who looks exactly like him.

Jervas is confined to an asylum. His father insists that the tomb’s lock has remained untouched and that Jervas merely slept outside it, suggesting his experiences were delusions. Yet an old servant later breaks into the vault and discovers an empty coffin labeled “Jervas,” supporting Jervas’s belief that he is spiritually linked to—or reincarnated from—the dead Hyde and is destined to be buried there.
