Tom Bailey and three friends jointly buy a small boat, though Tom and two others are forbidden to use its sail. Taking advantage of a day off from school, they row to Sandpeep Island for a picnic, where they fish, cook chowder, explore, and swim.

As a storm approaches, Binny Wallace returns alone to the boat to fetch some lemons. The boat breaks loose with no oars aboard, carrying him helplessly out to sea. His friends can only watch as the rising waves and violent squall sweep him from sight. Stranded on the island, the three boys endure a terrifying night of wind, rain, cold, hunger, and grief.

Rescuers arrive at dawn, including Binny’s father, but no trace of the boy is found. Tom becomes feverishly ill after returning home. The boat is later discovered overturned, and Binny’s body eventually washes ashore. His death permanently haunts Tom, who remembers him as forever young, gentle, and inseparable from his lost childhood.
