A young bachelor visits Michael and Katty Hennessey, an Irish farming couple who have remained happily married for fifty years, to learn the secret of their marriage. Katty recounts how, the morning after their wedding, she tried to prevent Michael from attending a fair by hiding their only comb and ordering him to stay home. Michael insisted on his independence, struck her, and demanded the comb. She yielded, establishing him as master of the household. Yet Michael soon returned, choosing not to attend the fair because he loved Katty and did not wish to hurt her, while explaining that he could not tolerate being controlled. The couple credit this early confrontation—his assertion of authority tempered by affection, and her acceptance of it—with preventing further quarrels throughout their marriage.
