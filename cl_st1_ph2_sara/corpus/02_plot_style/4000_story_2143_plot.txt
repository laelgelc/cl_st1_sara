Toby, a lonely six-year-old boy in colonial India, rules his nursery but fears his emotionally distant parents, whose marriage has been poisoned by jealousy and resentment. Cared for chiefly by his devoted governess, Miss Biddums, he longs for parental affection. After seeing how warmly his friend Patsie is treated, he mistakenly believes her blue sash is the reason she is loved and tries to imitate her, revealing his unhappiness to Patsie’s sympathetic mother.

One day Toby secretly opens a package intended for his mother and takes a diamond star, which he calls a “sparkle crown.” Although no one asks about it, guilt torments him and makes him increasingly withdrawn. When he falls dangerously ill with fever, he confesses the theft and reveals the hidden jewel. A note accompanying it suggests an affair involving his mother, but because Toby intercepted the package weeks earlier, nothing came of the invitation. Confronted with how far apart they have drifted, his parents reconcile.

Toby awakens to a transformed household in which both parents openly love and indulge him. Proud of his new bond with his mother, he later refuses a kiss from Patsie’s mother on the spot he now reserves for his own mamma.
