After helping barrister Arthur Wilbraham secure the acquittal of brutal murderer John Turk on grounds of insanity, his secretary, Johnson, prepares for an Alpine holiday. Wilbraham lends him a kit-bag, but as Johnson packs in his lonely rooms, he hears stealthy footsteps, sees shadowy figures, and notices the bag moving by itself. He discovers an old bloodstain inside and realizes it resembles the bag in which Turk had packed his dismembered victim.

The apparition of Turk then appears beside the bag and declares, “It’s my bag. And I want it,” causing Johnson to collapse in terror. The next morning, Wilbraham’s servant explains that the wrong bag was delivered: it is indeed the bloodstained exhibit from the murder trial. He also reveals that Turk poisoned himself immediately after his release and left instructions asking to be buried in the same kit-bag, at approximately the time the haunting began.
