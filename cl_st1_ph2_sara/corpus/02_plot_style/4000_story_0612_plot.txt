At a Christmas party hosted by Stephen and Vera Cheswardine, Vera becomes enchanted by her admirer Charlie’s suggestion that her boudoir should have a French window and marble balcony. Stephen dismisses the idea.

The party’s chief attraction is a working-class fortune-teller who reads tea leaves. Though frightened and superstitious, Vera eventually consults her and hears that she will become a widow. Convinced this predicts Stephen’s death, she grows unusually meek and devotes herself to protecting his health, abandoning her demands for the balcony.

When Stephen finally learns the reason for her behavior, he reassures her that the same fortune-teller predicted he would live to ninety. Relieved, Vera immediately resumes her campaign for the balcony. After Stephen firmly refuses, she threatens to have it when she is widowed and begins planting trees to improve its future view.
