Theodosia “Tommy” Shirley is a capable, unconventional young woman who manages her father’s western bank and quietly compensates for the incompetence of its charming cashier, Jay Ellington Harper. Although she loves Jay, she recognizes that they are fundamentally mismatched. After studying in the East, she returns home with her delicate friend Jessica, who soon falls in love with Jay and appears better suited to him.

When a run threatens Jay’s new bank twenty-five miles away, Tommy and Jessica attempt to bicycle there with emergency funds. Jessica collapses from exhaustion, but Tommy completes the grueling ride in time and saves the bank. Afterward, despite Jay’s earlier romantic overtures toward her, Tommy insists that he marry Jessica and sends him to find her. Left alone, Tommy finds the white flower Jay dropped, briefly mourns the love she has relinquished, and throws it away.
