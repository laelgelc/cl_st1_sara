A young sculptor named Alfred returns from Italy after winning a scholarship and becomes captivated by Kaela, the beautiful but quiet daughter of an ignorant, talkative widow. Mistaking Kaela’s silence and striking appearance for signs of hidden intelligence and depth, he courts and marries her.

After the wedding, Alfred gradually realizes that Kaela possesses little intellectual or emotional substance, while her mother’s foolish conversation embarrasses him. Their home improves when Sophy, Kaela’s plain-looking but intelligent, capable, and compassionate friend, comes to stay. Kaela, always delicate and tired, eventually falls ill and dies despite Sophy’s devoted care.

Alfred later marries Sophy, recognizing that he had first valued outward beauty as a sculptor might admire a statue, but has now found the deeper companionship of a kindred mind and spirit. Sophy gently challenges his dismissal of Kaela, suggesting that in the afterlife Kaela’s soul may develop enough to harmonize with his more completely.
