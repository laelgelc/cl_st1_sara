After her sick mother dies, kind and courageous Rosy sets out alone to find her father, who left years earlier to mine gold beyond the sea, desert, and mountains. During her journey, she rescues a stranded fish, nurses an injured mouse, and frees a fly from a spider’s web. Each promises to repay her kindness.

When obstacles block her way, the animals summon powerful friends to help: the fish calls a whale to carry her across the sea, the mouse calls a lion to bear her over the desert, and the fly calls an eagle to fly her up the mountains. Rosy finds her father’s hut at the mining camp, but a storm causes a flood and collapses part of the mine. The fish directs beavers to build a dam, while the mouse organizes moles to dig air passages and help rescue the trapped miners, including Rosy’s father, Tom, who has discovered an enormous lump of gold.

Father and daughter are joyfully reunited. Fearing that other miners may steal the gold, they follow the fly to two waiting eagles, which carry them and their treasure safely to a distant city. There Rosy and her father make a new home and live happily together.
