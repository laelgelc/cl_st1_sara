Jacqueline, known as La Folle, lives alone on a plantation, confined by a childhood trauma that has made her terrified to cross the bayou separating her cabin from the rest of the community. She is especially devoted to Cheri, the plantation owner’s young son.

When Cheri accidentally shoots himself while hunting near her cabin, La Folle carries the injured boy to the bayou and desperately calls for help. Receiving no answer, she overcomes her terror, crosses the water, and delivers him to his family before collapsing from the ordeal.

After recovering overnight, La Folle awakens transformed. She calmly crosses the bayou again, marvels at the beautiful world she had feared, and goes to the plantation house to ask about Cheri. Learning that his injury is not serious, she waits contentedly on the veranda, watching the sunrise over her newly discovered world.
