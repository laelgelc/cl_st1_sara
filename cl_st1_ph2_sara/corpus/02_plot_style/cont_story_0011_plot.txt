In a small town, Gloria is mocked for her birdlike appearance, predictable remarks, and seemingly empty personality. She falls in love with her coworker Randy and, realizing he barely notices her, tries to become more interesting, opinionated, feminine, and flirtatious. Randy’s sharp-tongued mother, who owns the store, recognizes Gloria’s intentions and ridicules the possibility of a romance.

Once Randy learns that Gloria likes him, however, he becomes intrigued and begins appreciating her constant agreement and admiration. His mother’s opposition makes him feel protective of Gloria, while Gloria is transformed by receiving affection after a lifetime of rejection. He leaves her small gifts and increasingly passionate notes, and their growing intimacy leads to a kiss, a relationship, and eventually marriage despite the town’s mockery and predictions of failure.

Contrary to expectations, the marriage thrives. Gloria becomes radiant, Randy grows generous and proud of his work, and they continually praise and cherish each other. Even Randy’s formerly hostile mother eventually becomes devoted to them and celebrates their mutual devotion.
