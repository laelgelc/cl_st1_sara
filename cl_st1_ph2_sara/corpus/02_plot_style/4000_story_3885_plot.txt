Seventeen-year-old Theodora Dace secretly completes a sentimental novel under the pen name Gladys Glyn, neglecting her responsibilities in her struggling household while dreaming that literary success will allow her to provide for her family. Inspired by a famous novelist whose career began at the Home Circle, she submits her manuscript there.

Theodora receives an acceptance letter and becomes a local celebrity, but when the magazine appears, she discovers that the announced serial is actually by the famous Kathleen Kyd. She travels to Boston and learns that both women submitted novels with the same title on the same day. The magazine mistakenly sent Theodora the other author’s acceptance; her own novel had been rejected.

Devastated, Theodora returns home expecting ridicule and anger. Instead, her usually ironic father meets her at the station and comforts her. He reveals that he too once wrote a rejected novel and understands her pain, creating a new bond between them.
