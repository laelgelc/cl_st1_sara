Sympathy Joe tells Mitchell the hidden history of their eccentric older mate, the Oracle, once a well-liked young man named Tom Marshall. As a young digger, Tom became involved with a deceitful woman on the goldfields, then later fell deeply in love with Bertha Bredt in Gippsland. Just as Tom and Bertha began planning a future together, the first woman wrote that she was pregnant with his child, destitute, and desperate for marriage. Believing it his duty, Tom confessed everything to Bertha, who respected his decision, and left her to marry the pregnant woman.

When the baby was born, Tom discovered it was not his and that his wife had deceived him. Even so, he remained with her out of compassion and a sense of responsibility. After the child’s real father, Redmond, returned and resumed relations with her, Tom confronted him in a brutal fight and defeated him decisively. He then left his wife, though he apparently continued supporting her financially.

Years later, Bertha learned the truth and wrote repeatedly to Tom. Receiving little response, she travelled to Sydney to find him but became gravely ill. Tom was summoned to her deathbed, where she embraced him and died in his arms. Joe explains that these losses account for the Oracle’s silences, strange habits, melancholy, and sensitivity to old music, while also revealing the endurance and compassion beneath his apparently foolish exterior.
