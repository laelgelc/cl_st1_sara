After dying, a woman enters the afterlife and tells the Spirit of Life that her well-meaning but unimaginative husband never understood her deepest self. She longs for the emotional and intellectual communion she found only fleetingly through art, literature, and beauty. The Spirit introduces her to the kindred soul she had sought throughout life, and the two immediately discover that they share the same thoughts, tastes, and perceptions.

When her soulmate proposes that they spend eternity together, however, she realizes that paradise would not feel like home without her husband’s familiar habits—even his creaking boots, slammed doors, and mundane novels. Learning that he believed she was already his soulmate and will expect to find her, she rejects perfect spiritual companionship and chooses to wait for him instead. She remains alone at the threshold of the afterlife, listening for the sound of his boots.
