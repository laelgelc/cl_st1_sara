Rupert despises his sister Kathleen’s inept, self-satisfied fiancé, whose constant blunders include losing a bridge game, shooting a rare protected honey-buzzard associated with Rupert’s dead son, and making a disastrous speech that costs Rupert’s party an election. During a winter holiday, the fiancé ignores warnings and falls through unsafe lake ice. Rupert tries to save him with a ladder, but a young guard dog delays him just long enough for the man to drown. Kathleen later marries the capable Malcolm Athling and has a son named Robbie, while Rupert treasures both the child and the dog whose interference removed the unwanted fiancé.
