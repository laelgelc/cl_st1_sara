Two experienced Arctic travelers, Captain and Big George, winter in an Alaskan gold-seekers’ camp whose idle, inexperienced residents live in poorly ventilated dugouts and ignore warnings about scurvy. When the disease begins killing them, only Klusky, a despised and vindictive outcast, remains active. After George contracts scurvy, Captain sets out with Klusky toward St. Michael to obtain fresh food and reindeer.

During the journey, Klusky tries to murder Captain but is disarmed and forced onward. He soon develops scurvy himself, leaving Captain unable to continue. As Klusky lies dying, he confesses that he stole and concealed the camp’s entire supply of potatoes, intending to sell them at an exorbitant price after his tormentors became desperate. He had also planned to kill Captain to prevent the rescue.

After honoring his promise not to abandon Klusky before death, Captain races back to camp. He finds the hidden potatoes still safe, then rushes to George’s apparently lifeless cabin. Believing he has returned too late, Captain enters in despair, only to discover that George is still alive and has faithfully expected him on the promised twelfth day. Overcome with relief, Captain collapses weeping beside his friend.
