On baking day, the anxious Mrs. Tabitha Twitchit tries to confine her mischievous kittens, but Tom hides in the chimney while Moppet and Mittens escape from the cupboard. As Tabitha and her neighbor Ribby search the old, rat-infested house, Moppet and Mittens report seeing rats steal dough, butter, and a rolling pin.

Lost inside the chimney passages, Tom stumbles into the hidden room of Samuel Whiskers and his wife, Anna Maria. The rats bind him, coat him with butter and dough, and attempt to turn him into a roly-poly dumpling. Hearing the rolling pin beneath the attic floor, Tabitha and Ribby summon John Joiner, whose sawing and doglike scratching frighten the rats away. He lifts the floorboards and rescues Tom, who must be peeled out of the dough and bathed.

Samuel and Anna Maria flee with their stolen goods to Farmer Potatoes’s barn, where their descendants become a severe infestation. Moppet and Mittens grow into successful rat-catchers, but the traumatized Tom remains afraid of anything larger than a mouse.
