Mary Jane Dement, a wealthy young woman ashamed of her rough childhood in the mining town of Redhorse, becomes fascinated by the handsome, intelligent, and mysterious Dr. Barritz. Encouraged by her childhood friend Jack Raynor’s hints that Barritz possesses occult powers, she falls deeply in love but fears he will reject her if he learns of her humble past.

During a private encounter on the beach, Barritz confesses that he has loved her for years. He then reveals that he is actually “Dumps,” the impoverished boy who was once her childhood companion and was believed to have been killed by road agents. He survived, traveled widely, and eventually returned at Raynor’s invitation. Mary Jane realizes that the supposed mystery surrounding him was merely Raynor’s invention to spark her curiosity and bring the two former friends together.
