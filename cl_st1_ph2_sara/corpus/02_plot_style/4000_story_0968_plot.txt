Wealthy Ainsley retreats to his remote farm while waiting for Polly Kirkland, whom he ardently loves, to agree to marry him. Before leaving for Egypt, Polly says she wants to love him and promises an indefinable sign that he must “read in his heart” when her feelings change.

Ainsley obsessively searches for this message, repeatedly mistaking unrelated cables and gifts for it before losing hope. Months later, six exhausted white birds land on his lake. When a friend remarks that they resemble sacred ibises from the Nile, Ainsley interprets their arrival as Polly’s sign and immediately sails to Egypt.

He finds Polly at Luxor, where she happily accepts him. She reveals that she sent no literal signal: she meant that if he truly loved her, he would come without waiting for one. Ainsley pretends that this was exactly what he understood. Then the same six white birds appear overhead and descend onto the Nile, leaving him awed by the mysterious coincidence and aware that he will eventually have to confess what really prompted his journey.
