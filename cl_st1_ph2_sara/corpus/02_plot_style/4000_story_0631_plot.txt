A country schoolteacher near Brownville overhears a threatening exchange in the woods between a mysterious man and a frightened woman, but the figures vanish when he reveals himself. The next day he recognizes the woman as Pauline Maynard, who is staying at his boarding house with her sickly sister Eva and their handsome guardian, Richard Benning.

Although Benning appears attentive and charming, the teacher distrusts him. A month later Pauline is found dead in bed, officially from heart disease. Eva accuses Benning of killing her, but under his steady gaze she abruptly submits to him. The teacher later overhears Eva tell Benning that she knows he killed Pauline and intends to kill her as well. When questioned, Eva admits both her fear and her overwhelming love for Benning, refusing to testify against him.

Infatuated with Eva, the teacher accompanies her on a walk to a high cliff called the Eagle’s Nest. Benning joins them and fixes Eva with an apparently hypnotic stare. Smiling despite the terror in her eyes, she leaps from the cliff to her death. Benning coldly insists that she acted on her own and leaves to summon help, singing as he goes, while the teacher denounces him as her murderer.
