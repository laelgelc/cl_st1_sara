The mischievous fairy Tanko-Mankie brings a department-store wax mannequin to life and abandons her. Newly conscious but completely ignorant of the world, the wax lady dresses herself in store merchandise and ventures into the city, imitating people without understanding food, money, reading, transportation, or social customs.

Her strange behavior alarms everyone she encounters. After boarding a streetcar without paying, she causes the conductor and passengers to flee, then steps in front of another car. Though badly damaged, she remains alive, astonishing the police, who take her into custody. Meanwhile, the store owner reports that the mannequin has escaped with his merchandise.

Before anyone can resolve the mystery, Tanko-Mankie returns and removes the enchantment. The police and store owner find only a battered, lifeless dummy in the cell. The skeptical inspector dismisses the incident as nonsense, while a policeman remains certain that the mannequin truly lived.
