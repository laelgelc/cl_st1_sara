Arthur Cotterill and his calm, resourceful brother Simeon prepare to travel from Bursley to London for Arthur’s wedding. Arthur initially panics when he thinks they have overslept, only to learn that Simeon deliberately advanced the clock. When the expected porter fails to arrive, they wheel Arthur’s heavy trunk toward the station themselves, but the barrow collapses on the icy road and Simeon sprains his ankle. Simeon sends Arthur ahead with the essential item—apparently the wedding ring—while he remains with the luggage.

Arthur catches the local train, but delays and confusion at the junction lead him to board the Birmingham section instead of the London section. He reroutes through Birmingham, telegraphs those waiting in London, and arrives at Euston just after two o’clock, convinced he has missed the legal deadline and ruined the wedding.

To his astonishment, Simeon meets him at the station on a crutch. After Arthur left, Simeon hired a milk cart, reached the junction with the trunk, and caught the correct London train. He has already reassured the wedding party and postponed the registry-office ceremony until shortly before three, explaining that the old two-o’clock restriction no longer applies. He rushes Arthur to the hotel, helps him dress, and gets him to the registry office in time to be married.
