Katie Bennett, a shy young woman who runs a second-hand bookshop and cares for her delusional, paralyzed grandfather, unexpectedly attracts Ted Brady, a celebrated athlete. Ted quickly falls in love with her, proposes, and is accepted. However, Katie’s grandfather believes himself to be the King of England and refuses to let his granddaughter marry a commoner.

Unable to abandon the old man or risk shocking him by eloping, Katie reluctantly separates from Ted. Months later, Ted returns after being elected ceremonial King of Coney Island for the Mardi Gras. He presents this title to her poorly informed grandfather as genuine royalty, persuading him that the marriage will be a suitable royal alliance. The delighted grandfather grants his approval, allowing Katie and Ted to reunite and marry.
