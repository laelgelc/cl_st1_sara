For a while I listened for the Volvo outside, though Jaclyn didn’t have it. I listened for small feet crossing the mattress. For Jackie Gunner ordering me to look at her. For Jamie Lou saying it was okay until the words meant nothing.

Nothing came.

I closed my eyes anyway.
